## PART 0 — WHAT THE WALK FOUND (each a failing test first)

1. **Home's right column goes empty.** Beside the institutional list, the thank-you section and "Tell Steward what to do", the right column is a tall blank panel. Either the right column carries something useful the whole way down, or the lower sections run full width.
2. **"One thank-you ready" looks broken.** A heading, an indented grey explainer with a rule under it that doesn't line up, one name, "$1", and an underlined "Read the draft" link, then a large empty gap. The explainer belongs in a quieter place (one line, or behind "Teach Steward your voice"); each draft is a row with the person, amount, date and a **button** (no underlined links in the CRM, per the standing rule); no dead space under a single row.
3. **"Tell Steward what to do" duplicates the Agent.** Home should keep a one-line entry that carries the text into Agent (the FIX-1 brief), not a second full ask with its own sentence and a pale button. "Steward did 0 things for you yesterday, sent 0, 1 draft waiting." is awkward. Say only what's true and useful ("1 draft is waiting for you") or say nothing.
4. **The Agent's ask needs a go button.** In Agent → Plans/Ask, the instruction box must have a clearly visible primary button beside it at all times (disabled until there's text, not hidden), and Enter submits. Same on Home's one-line entry.
5. **Regression of FIX-1 finding 2: the plan read the whole file.** "ada just became a volunteer and wants to do 15 hours a week" produced "Read your 398 people". An instruction that names one person reads that person. Add a replay test with this exact sentence.
6. **The plan doesn't fit the instruction.** For that sentence it proposed a donor-style note, a task "Confirm which Ada", a follow-up and a draft. The right plan: tag Ada Volunteer, record her availability (15 hours a week) as a volunteer internal note in the Volunteers hub (never the donor timeline), and offer one welcome draft. If more than one Ada matches, ask which before planning, not as a task afterwards.
7. **The plan headline is one long run-on sentence.** "Steward will log a note…, tag…, create a task…, open a follow-up… and then draft a note…". The headline says it in one short sentence ("Make Ada a volunteer, note her 15 hours a week, and draft a welcome"); the steps list carries the detail.
8. **A real person's name in the demo.** "Jonathan Atkinson $1" shows in the Harborlight demo's thank-you list. The demo org holds only invented people. Find where it came from, remove it, and add a guard that the demo seed and the demo org contain no row matching a real staff or user email/name.
