## 1. Who this is between

"Steward" is Steward Software LLC, a Kentucky limited liability company.
"You" is the organization named on the signature page. "The Service" is the
Steward application at stewardapp.dev and the accounts created for your staff.

Steward is a donor management and stewardship system. It is not a payment
processor: your online gifts are processed by **your own** Stripe account and
settle to **your own** bank. Steward never takes a percentage of a gift and
never asks your donors for a tip.

## 2. Price, the first charge, and cancellation

**The plan.** One of:

| Plan | Monthly |
| --- | --- |
| Founding | $199 |
| Core | $249 |
| Team | $499 |

Prices are in US dollars and are charged monthly in advance.

**The first charge is thirty days after signing.** Signing is the moment you
complete checkout — the moment the card goes in. Nothing is charged that day.
The exact date is shown to you on the checkout page before you press the
button, and it is shown again in Settings → Billing for as long as it is in
the future.

**Nothing moves that date.** Not importing your donor file, not importing it a
second time, not rescheduling onboarding, not a delay on Steward's side. The
date is fixed at signing and it is the same date on every screen and in every
email you receive about it.

**Cancel before it and you pay nothing.** At any point in those thirty days you
may cancel and no charge is ever made. There is no fee, no minimum, and no
conversation required: there is a button in Settings → Billing, and a one-click
cancel link in the email described below.

**A week's notice, every time it matters.** Seven days before the first charge,
Steward emails your account administrator one message stating the date, the
amount, the last four digits of the card on file, and a link that cancels the
subscription in one click.

**After the first charge it is month to month.** You may cancel at any time.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you have already paid for;
you keep full access until then, and you are not charged again. Payments
already made are not refunded except where the law requires it.

**Failed payments.** If a charge fails, Steward retries and emails you. If it
is unresolved after seven days the account may be suspended. Suspension is not
deletion — see §3.

**Price changes.** Steward may change the price of a plan with thirty days'
written notice to the address on the account. A change never applies to a
month you have already paid for, and continuing to use the Service after the
notice period is acceptance of the new price.

## 3. Your data is yours

You own every donor record, gift, document, note and report you put into or
create within Steward. Steward stores and processes it only to run the Service
for you. Steward does not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone for
marketing.

You can export your data at any time, in full, in a standard format, without
asking. That remains true while an account is suspended and for thirty days
after the account ends. After thirty days the data is deleted.

You are responsible for having the right to hold the donor information you
import, and for using it in line with the law that applies to you.

---

## 4. What Steward does, and does not, do with money

Online giving runs on your own Stripe account. Steward takes no platform fee
and adds no donor tip; gifts settle directly to you. Stripe's own processing
fees are between you and Stripe.

Steward writes receipts, statements and ledger entries from the gifts you
record. They are only as accurate as what you put in, and they are not tax or
accounting advice.

---

## 5. What Steward owes you

Steward will make reasonable efforts to keep the Service available and to keep
your data backed up and secure. Steward is one person's company and does not
promise a formal uptime figure; it does promise to tell you honestly when
something is broken and to fix it.

Support is by email, directly, to Jonathan. During a founding-partner
engagement that includes onboarding your file and your first campaign.

## 6. Ending it

You may end this agreement at any time under §2. Steward may end it with thirty
days' notice, or immediately if the Service is used to break the law, to send
unsolicited bulk email, or to attack the system. If Steward ends the agreement
without cause, any prepaid month is refunded pro rata.
