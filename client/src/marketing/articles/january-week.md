The busiest giving season just ended. Before the inbox fills up again, a few hours in the first week of January make the rest of the year easier.

## Send statements early

Donors appreciate getting their [year-end statement](/glossary/tax-receipt) before they need it.

## Thank every December donor

Many gave on the last two days of the year. Make sure every gift got a thank-you, and call the largest.

## Start first-year plans

Every first-time December donor should begin a [first-year plan](/glossary/first-year-retention) now, not in March.

## Pull your LYBUNT list

[Last year's donors who did not give in December](/glossary/lybunt) are this year's first calls.

## Look at retention

Compare last year's [retention](/glossary/donor-retention-rate) to the year before, and pick one thing to improve.
