Two unlovely acronyms describe the most valuable people in your database.

## LYBUNT

[Last Year But Unfortunately Not This year](/glossary/lybunt). These people gave last year and have not given yet this year. They already know you and already said yes once. They are your best prospects for any [appeal](/glossary/year-end-appeal).

## SYBUNT

[Some Year But Unfortunately Not This year](/glossary/sybunt). Anyone who gave in any past year but not this one. A longer list, a colder one, and still warmer than any stranger.

## What to do with them

Sort LYBUNT by last year's gift and call the top twenty before your next appeal. Do not open with an ask. Say thank you for last year, share one thing their gift made possible, and ask what they care about now. Then include them in the appeal with a note that mentions last year.

## In Steward

Both lists are one click from Reports, and every count opens to the people behind it.
