# Steward stock photos

Every photo here is bundled with Steward and offered to every organization as a starting picture (EDITOR-1). Each is under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no permission or attribution required. We credit the photographers anyway. Nothing here shows a recognizable public figure or a logo; each was looked at before it was added.

A photo added here needs a row here, and a row needs its file: `tests/lib/stockPhotosCheck.js` checks both ways. Thumbnails in `thumbs/` are the same photos made small for the picker.

Every row is one photo, checked on its own page (FIX-60). **Photo ID** is the Unsplash ID at the end of the Source URL. **Checked** is the day that page was opened and found to name this photographer, say "Free to use under the Unsplash License" and offer a free download (an Unsplash+ photo is not free and never goes here), and the day the image that ID serves was compared with the file. **Fingerprint** is a 256-bit difference hash of that served image (17×16 grayscale, left > right); the check recomputes it from the file and fails when they are more than 60 bits apart. To add a photo, do the same three checks and fill these three columns.
