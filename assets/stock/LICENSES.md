# Steward stock photos

Every photo here is bundled with Steward and offered to every organization as a starting picture (EDITOR-1). Each is under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no permission or attribution required. We credit the photographers anyway. Nothing here shows a recognizable public figure or a logo; each was looked at before it was added.

A photo added here needs a row here, and a row needs its file: `tests/lib/stockPhotosCheck.js` checks both ways. Thumbnails in `thumbs/` are the same photos made small for the picker.
