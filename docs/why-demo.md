# The Ask why demo (under two minutes)

Harborlight Youth Collective, `director@harborlight.demo`. Seeded by `scripts/seed-demo.js` only.

1. **Open Home.** At the top of The Thread is the Ask why line: "Who should I call tomorrow?",
   "Why did Spring Appeal <this year> come in where it did?", and a box to type.
2. **Type "Why did the spring appeal come in under last year?"** The panel answers in one sentence:
   about $11,650 under, mostly because eleven of last year's donors haven't given yet. Below it, the
   reasons, largest first: eleven not back, seven who gave more, four who gave less, two who gave late
   last May (this year's letter went out twelve days later), three new donors. Say: "Every one of those
   is a fact you can open."
3. **Open one reason's rows.** Click the dollar figure beside "Last year's donors who haven't given yet".
   The rows are the eleven people, each with what they gave last time, and they foot to the figure.
4. **Plan the calls.** "Plan calls to the top five" puts five calls on tomorrow's Thread. Nothing is
   sent. Close the panel: the Thread shows them.
5. **Tap "Who should I call tomorrow?"** Five names, five different reasons, largest at stake first:
   an October major donor who hasn't given this October, a $10,000 ask past its date, Tidewater's
   unthanked $7,500, a donor drifting from their own rhythm, and a monthly card that failed.
   "Put all five on tomorrow's Thread."
6. If asked about AI: turn it off in Settings and ask again. The same answer, in Steward's own words.
