## LIST-1 · The donor list: your columns, 100 per page, filter by anything (2026-10-27)

- **One registry, `donorList.js`.** Every field the Donors list can show or filter by, grouped Contact, Giving, Pledges, Events, Volunteering, Communication and From your imports (the BUILD-78 custom field registry, so IMPORT-4's imported fields appear by themselves). A filter is JSON (`where`: join and/or, conditions of field, op, value) and runs inside `groups.js buildDonorFilter`, so the list, its export, Save as group, Show me and Ask are the same rows. A condition groups.js already had (city, state, a level, lapsed, an event, a campaign, a group, hours) is compiled by calling buildDonorFilter with that one rule; new rules: `zip` (exact, ZIP+4, prefix `405*`, list), `zipMiles` (centre = the org's own placed addresses in that zip; refused in words when none), `attendedNoGift`, `where`. A field or operator Steward doesn't know is a 400 with a sentence, never ignored.
- **Columns are the profile's numbers.** Lifetime, last and first gift and gift count are the stored totals NUMBERS-1's checker holds to the gifts; This year is `donor-gifts-between` from the fiscal year's start, set-wise; pledge balance is F-5's remaining balance. Cells are computed for the page only (`cellsFor`), one statement per column.
- **Layouts per person** (`donor_list_layouts`, user_id '' = the org default an admin sets) and **saved views** (`donor_list_views`, columns plus filter). `GET /donors/ids` is select-all-matching (cap 25,000); `POST /donors/bulk-tag` adds one tag.
- **Atlas and Ask:** "donors in 40502 who came to Finding Joy" is a question in Atlas's plain shapes, and Ask reads the zip and "came to <event>" (before the came-back shapes, which used to take "who came to") into the metric `donor_list`, the Donors list for those rules.
- **Defect found:** one gift with a blank date 500'd the Donors list for its page (`driftGiftAnswers` cast `g.date::date`). Undated gifts now stay out of the drift rhythm.
- **County** is a donors column the geocoder fills (Geocodio `address_components.county`, Nominatim `addressdetails`). Measured: a 100-row page with 11 columns on a 10,000-donor org in about 85ms (the suite's §6).
