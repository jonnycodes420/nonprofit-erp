## AUTO-1 · Journey steps that send themselves (2026-10-21)

Jonathan said yes on 21 Oct: nothing in a journey sent without a click, which kept it safe, and the second and third touches got skipped when a small team was busy. A journey email step now has "Send this step automatically", off everywhere, for words a person on staff saved, turned on by an owner or admin after a confirm with the words and the count. It sends through the one sender a tracked sequence uses (`sendStaffWrittenEmail`, pulled out of `processTrackedSequences` so there is no second sender), on weekday mornings 9 to 11 where the org is, the next weekday after the step opens, so "Sending tomorrow" (or "on Friday") and the Home line can be read and skipped the night before. Skip rules, the merge hold, Pause all, the 200-a-day cap and the demo rule are in the mail decisions file. Found while building: `actor().name` is the sign-in email, so "turned on by" reads the user's name from `users`; journey plan steps count from 1 and the switch from 0; and the journey sentence "Nothing is sent without you." had to name the step that now does. auto1-send failed on main (no `journey_sends`) and under four plants.
