## TABLE-1 · The Donors table reads cleanly, and stays clean when you move or resize columns (2026-11-04)

Jonathan, 4 Nov, demo org at a laptop width: "the columns need to be way cleaner, it looks so bad all jumbled, and when you move it around it gets even worse." Seen: "$40,709No owner Assign", headings over the wrong cells, Engagement in two shapes, Giving strength against Phone with its heading on two lines, a stray Assign column on some rows, and resizing or dragging making it worse.

- **Why it looked like that.** The header cell had 10 px of padding on its right and the row cells had none, so a right-aligned heading sat 10 px left of its numbers; with no padding a right-aligned Lifetime ended exactly where a left-aligned Owner began (0.0 px apart, measured). Donor took `2fr` of whatever was left, Stage and Owner `1fr`, the rest fixed, so widths depended on the window. The two score headings were set to wrap (SWEEP-2). Admins had an 80 px column of their own with a reassign button on assigned rows only. A column could be resized to 60 px whatever it held, and phones showed as typed.
- **What changed.** `COLUMN_SPEC` in `DonorColumns.jsx` gives every column a default, a min and an alignment (unknown fields take their format's). Every track is a fixed px width and a last `1fr` track takes the slack, so the default seven columns fit at 1440 and more scroll sideways with the checkbox and Donor pinned (`position: sticky`). One cell box for header and rows (14 px each side). Headings one line with a tooltip. Owner: a face and a name, or a quiet Assign; on Team an admin clicks the name to change it; the separate Assign column is gone. Engagement: "42 Warm" or "— Quiet" / "— Not scored", the old sentence in the tooltip. Giving strength: the badge, centred; "—" when there are no gifts. Phone through `shared/phone.js`. Donor is two lines on every row (a dash when there is no email), Last gift too ("No gift" over a dash). The editor's reset reads "Reset columns".
- **Test.** `tests/table1-columns-clean.test.js` (the brief asked for it). On main's client: 16 passed, 35 failed. After: 52 of 52. Planted: `CELL_PAD = 0` turned the five overlap checks red. Two test-mechanics traps found on the way: a Playwright mouse move to a negative x is dropped (so a resize "to the narrowest" did nothing on either build), and a screenshot left the header under the sticky top bar, where the drag landed on the bar. The check now counts a move that changed nothing as a failure.
- **Phone width.** Already a card list below 768 px (BUILD-41); the test checks that the squeezed table never shows at 390.
