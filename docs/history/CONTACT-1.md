# CONTACT-1 · A person's address, email and phone in one click (24 Nov 2026)

From a pilot customer's call: to see an address or an email she opened Edit record, and on some people the rail's Contact section was missing altogether ("I'm not even seeing it on some of these"). For an invoice or an envelope she needs the address.

**Item 1 (shipped on its own).** The section was drawn only when the record had `email`, `phone`, a contact person or the rail read's address. Three ways that missed a reachable person: the address came only from `GET /donors/:id/rail`, so a person with just an address had no Contact section until that read answered and none at all when it failed (About offered "Add email or phone" instead); the workbook import fills `mobile` and `email2`, which the client's `adaptDonor` dropped and the section never read, so a mobile-only person had none and their mobile showed nowhere on the profile; and a person with nothing showed nothing. Now `shared/contactDetails.js` reads the person's own columns (the address line is the same function the rail read uses), the section is always drawn, and an empty one says "No contact details yet · Add".

**Items 2 and 3.** The profile is Jonathan's, so the proposed Contact tab was a mockup first (drawn on staging over a demo household, nothing written), and was built only after his yes, "as drawn" (`docs/needs-jonathan/CONTACT-1.md`). The tab (`client/src/components/profile/ContactTab.jsx`) sits after Conversations and writes nothing. The mailing block is `shared/ackLetter.js addressLines`, the block the window envelope prints, so Copy address copies exactly what an envelope would carry, and it refuses to offer a copy of a partial address (no city, or no state and no ZIP) and says why. Three things the walk caught that the mockup had drawn loosely:
- **The joint addressee named someone who lives elsewhere.** The household's `addressee` is everyone in it, so a three-person household with one grown child at another address offered an envelope naming all three at an address only two of them live at. `GET /households/:id` now gives each person `sameAddressAs` (the household page's one same-street-city-ZIP rule) and `addresseeHere` (`addresseeOf` over only the people at that address, this person first), and the tab offers that.
- **"Do not" listed a bounce.** A bounced email or a wrong number is not a do-not; it sits on its own row as a brass flag, and "Do not" lists only the do-not flags and Deceased.
- **The rail never said "No calls".** The import writes `do_not_call` and the household view shows it, but the rail read's flags left it out. It is a flag now, on the rail and the tab.
The mockup's "Prefers: Not said yet" row is drawn only when a preference is known: nothing in Steward records one yet, and a row that can never be filled is not a fact.

Found on the way, for a later brief: `adaptDonor` does not carry `phone_unreachable` either, so the rail's "Marked wrong. Never dialed or texted from Steward." line under a phone never draws (the "Phone marked wrong" flag under Careful still does).
