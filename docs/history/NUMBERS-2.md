## NUMBERS-2 · Imported totals are never erased, and one definition each (2026-10-25)

Real orgs are about to import years of history, and any recompute (a gift edited, deleted or refunded, a merge, even the batch after an import) set a person's total to the sum of their gift rows. A donor imported with $50,000 and no gifts read $500 after one edit to their first new gift (before screenshot, on main). The imported total is now its own figure (`donors.imported_*`, with its source, the day it came in and the last day it covers). Lifetime is that plus the gifts dated after it. A gift dated inside the covered period is never counted twice. The profile says "Includes $50,000 from your old system (before Mar 2025)". `recordGift` recomputes instead of adding. A merge folds two imported totals. `first_gift_date` is kept by the same statement. Existing stored totals above their gifts became imported totals covering the days before each person's first gift, so no one's Lifetime moved on deploy.

One average (refunds off the total, not counted as gifts, credit rows out) and one retention (FEP, the org's fiscal years, gifts above zero, plus a same-dates-last-year version) now feed every screen, with the words in the "?" and the help article `how-steward-counts`. Home, Fundraising and the profile now use the org's own year: `orgTz()` never carried the start month, so everything was July. Part 4 closed a dozen more of NUMBERS-1's suspected disagreements and marked the rest, with reasons, in `docs/numbers-1/inventory.md`. `numbers2-history` failed on main (on main the $50,000 read $0 after a recompute, averages read $349.92 where the rule gives $295.83, and an October year was ignored). `numbers1-foot` gained an import-a-total step and still passes. ask2-plans pins the new average.
