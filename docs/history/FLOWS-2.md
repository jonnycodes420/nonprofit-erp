# FLOWS-2 · The last dead ends, filters that filter, and a merge you can trust (2026-11-05)

From Muse's Walk A, evening run on 5 Nov. Seven findings, five items.

- **Home's "13 follow-ups".** Not reproduced: on main (4c8ac25), at 1440, 1180, 900 and 390, on the demo and on a fixture of 13, the count opens the rail's list of 13 under "Open follow-ups · 13" and each name opens the person (HOME-6 already pinned "N follow-ups opens N"). Left guarded rather than changed: `tests/flows2.test.js` §1 checks the count and the list, and §1's guard clicks every control on Home and Fundraising and fails on any whose click changes nothing on screen.
- **Fundraising's Raised.** It went to Reports' Giving summary, a page about the year, not the gifts behind the figure. `/fundraising/overview` now sends `period.source` (the `gifts` figure source over the span Raised was summed over, to today; cache key `fundraising-overview-3`), and the Raised and Gifts tiles open those rows in place (`components/fundraising/RowsPanel.jsx`, the panel a `<Figure>` opens).
- **Lost & Found.** Not in Fundraising's nav. Overview now has a second part, Lost & Found (`lostfound`, registered in `FR_LEGACY`), which draws Re-engage's own `ReEngageView` from the same whole-org read, opened on its "over 24 months" window (`components/fundraising/LostAndFoundPart.jsx`).
- **Saved views.** LIST-3 saved the sort; the view still kept only the condition filter. The list's own filters (search, Stage, Owner, Tag, designation, household, who) are not conditions, they are the chips over the list, and none were saved. New column `donor_list_views.chips` (JSONB), added in `server/db/ddl/part7.js` and not beside LIST-3's in `server/donors/listViewsSchema.js`, because the schema hash covers only db.js and server/db/ (a column added outside them never runs on a database already at the old hash), cleaned to a fixed set of keys on POST; picking a view restores every one and clears any it was saved without.
- **Gives monthly.** It read "a recurring plan at any interval" (`recurring: "1"`), so a person on a quarterly plan who gives every two months stayed in. One rule now, `shared/numbers/givesMonthly.js`: a monthly plan still running, or gifts on 6 or more days in the last 12 months whose median gap (rhythm.js's `median`) is 21 to 45 days. The Donors filter and a Group's "Gives monthly" both read it, and its sentence is the filter's tooltip.
- **Merge.** The compare screen already started on the kept record's values (ADDRESS-1's `keptFirstChoices`), except where the kept record is blank. What the walk saw was the headings: both sides were "Rosalind Twofold", so the kept one could not be told apart, and from a profile the kept record is often the other one (more gifts). The sides are now headed Kept and Merged in, each with its id, the day it was added and its lifetime (`components/merge/KeptHeads.jsx`); a row that starts on the merged-in side says why; the sentence above the rows names the kept record by name and id. The default kept record and the button's words are unchanged (address1-street pins them).
- **Org name.** Settings → Organization showed the name as text only. The one name field was /welcome's org-basics step (Open setup → "Set it up yourself"), and for an org that has finished onboarding WelcomePage draws its fields on the first render and its effect then sends you to /dashboard (WelcomePage.jsx, `if (auth?.org?.onboarding_complete) navigate("/dashboard")`): the field rendered once and vanished. `components/settings/OrgNameCard.jsx` edits it in Settings through PATCH /orgs/:id and reads the org again (refreshOrg).
- **Files header.** The Files tab's empty line read only the profile's uploads (`donor_materials`); a file attached to a note is listed below it by the timeline, so the tab said "No materials uploaded yet" over a file. The header now counts both ("Files · 1") and the empty line says where the attached files are.
