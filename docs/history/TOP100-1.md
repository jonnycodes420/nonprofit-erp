# TOP100-1 · A Top 100 list with a rank and a plan of care for each person (2026-11-09)

Allie Barnett (Justin's Place, a beta customer) was building her Top 100 and a plan of care for each person in Excel. Her sheet is the spec: one row per person with rank, wealth score, giving, owner, plan and next step.

- **The list** is `portfolio_lists` (one per org, "Top 100", renamable) and `portfolio_members` (rank 1..N). It sits under Major gifts → Top 100 (`client/src/components/majorGifts/Top100.jsx`, routes in `server/majorGifts/top100.js`). People are added from the profile's Plan of care, from the Donors list's bulk "Add to Top 100", by name on the page, or by pasting Rank and Name/Email columns from a spreadsheet ("Bring in ranks", `POST /top100/ranks`, preview first). The import pipeline was not touched, because WEALTH-1 was changing its mapper at the same time; the paste-in is the importer's "Rank" column for this list.
- **The rank** moves by drag or by typing a number. Every change takes the list's row lock and rewrites the moved ranks in one statement; `uq_portfolio_rank` is DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED, so no commit can leave two people on one rank. Six reorders by two people at once land with ranks 1..6.
- **Owner and Stage are not stored on the list.** The brief named an owner column on `portfolio_members`; it reads `donors.assigned_to` instead, because BUILD-30 makes assignment the one owner, and a second owner on the list would disagree with the profile and the board. Wealth Score is `donors.wealth_score`, WEALTH-1's column; this build writes none.
- **A plan of care** is `care_plans` (goal, ask, ask-by day) plus touches that ARE tasks (`tasks.care_plan_id`, `tasks.touch_type`). They show in Tasks and on Home, and any Done finishes them. A database trigger writes the one timeline line on done and removes it on undo, so the five ways a task is ticked all log. Plan health is `shared/careShape.js planHealth`: Behind, On track, No plan, All done (the fourth state, every touch done and nothing more planned, was not in the brief and needed a word).
- **Templates**: "Quarterly touches" is built in (a touch at 0, 91, 182, 273 and 364 days); she can save any plan as her own (`care_templates`). The Journeys step editor did not fit (a journey step is a thread, one open at a time; a touch is a task with its own owner and day), so it is a simple list.
- **Home and the morning email** put due and overdue Top 100 touches first, by rank, written "#3 Hollis Brandt: visit, due Thu" (`server/majorGifts/top100Lead.js`; the lead is a sort key in `shared/threadRank.js`).
- **Atlas** reads "Who on my Top 100 hasn't been touched in 60 days?" and "Make a plan of care for #4" in code. The second writes nothing: it names the touches and opens the person's plan of care, where one press adds them.
- **Who sees it**: `prospect.canSee` on every route (admins and the major gifts permission). Never donor-facing.
- Tests: `tests/top100-plan-of-care.test.js`. Planted: a reorder that does not shift the others (6 red), and a touch that makes no task (14 red).
