# PEOPLE-1 · Mark someone deceased with care, and link a partner like a spouse (2026-11-04)

**Why.** A beta customer (4 Nov): a donor's spouse died and the only way to mark someone deceased was an import, so the person kept showing up in asks and suggestions; and some of her donors are partners, not spouses, with no Partner link, so their giving did not pool.

**What changed.**
- The profile's More menu has "Mark as deceased" (Their record). The dialog takes a date of death (a year, a month and year, or a whole date; never after today), a private note, and "Remind me each year on the anniversary" (off by default). `POST /donors/:id/deceased` (`server/donors/deceased.js`) writes the importer's own field (`donors.deceased`, `deceased_date`) and, in one transaction: ends active journeys (`cultivation_plans.end_reason = 'deceased'`, open and pending steps skipped, the step's thread closed), stops active sequences (`stop_reason = 'deceased'`), closes their open next step (any type: the walk found a "Send thank-you note" step kept him on Home) and every open task whose words are an ask ("Closed: marked deceased", `tasks.closed_reason`); other tasks stay. The reminder is a yearly task to the relationship owner, "Anniversary of <name>'s passing: a note to <spouse/partner>", never a send. One row in `deceased_marks` remembers what closed.
- "Undo: they're not deceased" (`POST /donors/:id/not-deceased`) restores the flag and the date it had, reopens the asks it closed (a next step only when none has opened since), withdraws the reminder, and says the journeys that ended stay ended.
- The same flag now also holds in the places that did not read it: the journey step runner (an importer-marked person's journeys end there, `journeyStart.advanceCultivationPlan`), sequence enrollment, Home's moves (a pledge to collect; a household move names and leads with the living), Home's thank-you queue (drafts kept, not offered), the profile's Next move (`/insights/donor/:id`), the Impact report's re-engage list, the weekly email's lapsed list, move suggestions and the suggested ask. Already holding before this build: every send gate (`donorMailDecision`, all 17 kinds), Re-engage and lapse lists, Drift, at-risk, the call list, thank-you and milestone drafts, journey starts, the birthday card, care threads.
- The profile shows "In memory · died <date>" under the name, and its header drops Lapsing and Current. A spouse or partner's profile lists them as "<name> (died <date>)"; the household stays.
- Partner is a relationship type, next to Spouse in the picker. It pools wherever Spouse does: one household (`householdsFromLinks`), scoring's `pooledWith`, and, new for both spouse and partner, one giving unit for the lapse rule (`shared/numbers/lapse.js UNIT_LINK_TYPES`; the TRUTH-1 key reads the same types), so a gift from either keeps both off Lapsed and Re-engage. Data health files a linked pair under family, never duplicates. An import whose Spouse/Partner column is headed Partner links the pair as Partner (otherwise Spouse); the link goes with the import's Undo.

**The walk** (headless Chromium on the local stack; the Chrome extension was not connected) found four things, all fixed before the PR: the open profile did not re-read the record after the dialog (no banner, Lapsed still shown), so closing the dialog now re-reads it and the task list; the profile's Next move still offered an ask; an open "Send thank-you note" step and two unsent thank-you drafts kept him on Home; Ruth's household panel said "Harold Lee · household" without the date.

**Tests.** `tests/people1-deceased.test.js` (the one new suite: donor data and email). Red on main 23 of 34. Planted: see the PR. `tests/live1-alive.test.js` counts sixteen doors (`person_flag`).

**Not done.** The send gate reads its flags by address: a living spouse who shares one email address with a person marked deceased is refused as deceased too. Left as it is (the safe side); for the next brief to decide.
