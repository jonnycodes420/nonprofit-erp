## PAGES-1 · Every donor-facing page is customizable: giving, volunteer and membership (2026-11-06)

**Why.** Jonathan, 6 Nov: anything a donor or volunteer sees has to look gorgeous and be easy to make your own. On the giving page editor the left panel could not be scrolled to Trust panel and below, "Cover photo: No photo" showed over a photo on the page, and a blank story left a large empty brown block. Volunteer pages had far less to work with, and membership had no page of its own to build.

**Editor fixes.** The Cover photo section now names the photo the page shows: a page with no cover of its own is led by its Hero block's photo (Donate.jsx always drew `cover || hero.image`), so the rail shows that one and Replace, Remove and the focus act on the Hero block; `layoutFor` counts that photo too (it had picked Simple, which draws no photo), and the rail says when the Simple layout hides a photo. With no photo and no logo the page drew `.g3-fill`, a box in the brand colour with nothing in it; now the brand box shows only with a logo, the editor shows a dashed "Add a cover photo" and the public page nothing, and a blank story is a dashed "Add your story" box in the editor and nothing publicly. The rail's scroll defect was not reproduced: on main the rail scrolled to its end under a real wheel at 1280×720 in Chromium and WebKit. The shell was hardened anyway (the page root is `position: fixed; inset: 0` instead of `100vh`, each rail has `min-height: 0`, `max-height: 100%` and `overscroll-behavior: contain`) and the test holds it.

**One page system.** A membership page is a `giving_pages` row of page_kind `membership` (`membershipPage.js`), like VOL-PAGE-1's volunteer page: the same editor, draft, publish, History and Revert, a public read `GET /org/:slug/membership-page/:page/public` and the page at `/membership-page/:org/:page` (VolunteerPage.jsx with `kind="membership"`). New blocks in the registry: Header with logo, Goal bar (spots filled on a volunteer page from its shifts, members of a typed target on a membership page, counted from Memberships), Membership levels (the form: each level with Join through the existing membership checkout) and Footer (name, footer text, contact, EIN line; a placeholder EIN is held back). Hero, rich text, image, gallery, video, stats, quote, people and FAQ are now on volunteer and membership pages too. A giving page keeps its fixed header, form, goal bar and trust panel around its blocks. Every block renders nothing publicly when it has nothing true to show (`blockHasContent` in PortalWidgets.jsx); before, an empty rich text block drew a blank white card.

**Theme.** `shared/pageTheme.js`, stored per page as `design.theme`: accent colour (the org's button colour by default), five curated font pairings (portalTheme's), buttons Filled, Outline or Pill, corners Square, Soft or Round, sections Light or Dark. The editor's "Page theme" section names every pair under WCAG AA (4.5:1) with its ratio: button words on the accent, an outline button or accent text on the section's ground.

**Templates.** Giving: Story first, Campaign, Simple (each also sets the layout). Volunteer: One-day event, Ongoing roles, Group service day. Membership: Story first, Campaign, Simple. A template's photo is one of Steward's from the org's mission group and its prompts are hints (WALKB-1), so each looks finished with only the logo and colours.

**Size.** PortalEditor.jsx is 1,192 lines: History moved to `PageHistoryPanel.jsx`, the block options and the editor palette to `PortalEditorBlockOptions.jsx` (scoped out of brand-allowlist with PortalEditor, the same editor).

**Left.** The FAQ and Stats validators refuse an empty block, so a template cannot carry them empty, and a freshly added FAQ block shows "Save failed" until a question is typed (unchanged from main). Public block pages do not install PERF-4's app mark; the test times them to their own ready marker on the same 150 ms round trip.

Test: `tests/pages1.test.js` (API and browser legs).
