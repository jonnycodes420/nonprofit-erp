## ATLAS-3 · Atlas answers with the people, uses what you know, and the More menu does its job (2026-11-05)

- **Why.** Muse's walks (4 and 5 Nov): a list question came back as one line ("22 people match … Sterling Nakamura first"), "Open list" showed in one ask of three, and "Tell me more" cleared the dialog. "Tell me about Marlowe" ignored the About note. Atlas drafted a thank-you "for his $10,000 gift", a gift not on record, and answered "link Stephen and Margaret" with "I never merge people". Brief me took about 25 s, repeated "this organization calls somebody like this a sponsor" (a line of its own rows) and cited "(1 row)"; Call script opened the email panel; Impact summary downloaded silently or failed silently.
- **A list answer keeps the people the Ask answer already carried** (`server/atlas/answers.js` listAnswer): the shown names with each one's last gift, the count, and every id behind it (read once through `/figures/:source/people`). Open list (`POST /atlas/actions/:id/list`) makes one static group through the Groups routes on the first click and reopens it after, so the set never changes under her. The ids stay on the server; the dialog gets the names.
- **The answers live with the request** (`voice_memos.read.answers`, keyed by the Atlas line), so no schema change. Tell me more keeps a thread in the dialog (`client/src/lib/atlasThread.js`) and the next ask carries `followsId`; the server reads who that request was about (`priorContext`) and resolves "her" to them.
- **Two step kinds**: `about` (the person answer: giving, About note, last three conversations, next step) and `link` (the relation from her words, else one click: Spouses, Partners, Family, Same household, Works at; then `POST /donors/:id/relationships`). A read of "merge" whose words say link, connect or related is a link; "merge" itself is still refused as before.
- **A thank-you naming an amount the record does not have** (no gift or pledge of it from them, none in the same request) is a gift tap instead, with "Record it first", and no email is written.
- **The status line** is the server's own: the dialog sends a key and polls `GET /atlas/progress/:key` ("Step 2 of 4: Writing the email to Undine"), in memory for a minute (`server/atlas/progress.js`).
- **Brief me** (`server/donors/briefFast.js`): kept per person by a hash of exactly the lines the model is handed; streamed as NDJSON with first words written in code from the record; one call on the faster model (BRIEF_MODEL, default claude-sonnet-4-6, as Atlas's read moved in AGENT-5); the org's word for a donor is an instruction, not a row; a sentence about what the organization "calls" someone is dropped. Citations read "from 3 gifts" (`shared/briefCite.js`).
- **Call script** is its own route (`routes/atlasMore.js`), five parts, facts read in code, a script whose dollar figure is not on record falls back to the template. **Draft an email** asks Atlas for the draft, so it lands in the review queue in the org's sign-off (voice samples) and is sent only from there. **Impact summary** shows on screen (`GET /donors/:id/impact-summary`, the PDF's own reading) with the PDF to download.
