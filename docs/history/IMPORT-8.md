# IMPORT-8 · An import gets who can be emailed right, counts every gift once, and says what it did (2026-11-04)

**Why.** Walks and real imports on 3–4 Nov. A 1,005-person People + Gifts workbook with a "Do not email" column (36 Yes) and 16 About-them notes reading "Asked for no more mail, email only" previewed "16 opt-outs": the reader's "please remove" scan (`PLEASE_REMOVE_RE` over every cell of the row, `shared/sheetRead.js`) matched "no more mail" in the notes, blocking 16 people who want email and adding a suppression for each; the 36 were written as Do not email but the preview never said so. A Donors sheet with "Last Gift Amount" and "Last Gift Date" beside a Gift History sheet read as a second gift sheet (any named table with an amount is "gifts"), so each person's last gift landed twice ("Method not given" on the Donors one).

**What changed.**
- A communication choice comes only from a column whose header names one (the plain words and the Salesforce, Bloomerang, DonorPerfect and Neon spellings; Bloomerang's Communication Restrictions read code by code). In a Mailing list column the cell's own words still count ("Unsubscribed", "please remove me"). A note anywhere else that reads like a preference is listed ("16 notes mention mail or email; nothing was changed"). The preview says each choice with its column ("36 marked Do not email (column 'Do not email')").
- The workbook screen's mapper (`buildWorkbookDonors`) and the old wizard's CSV builder no longer set deceased, do not contact, do not mail, do not email or do not solicit from a Notes cell or a cell comment: each is listed ("N notes read like …; nothing was changed") and applied only when she ticks "Mark them as the notes say" (`noteFlags: "apply"`). Every other text-read flag was audited: a Status column's exact values (`parseExclusionValue`, anchored) and the hidden-row and fill-colour legends (applied only as answered) read explicit values; "Anonymous" is a name cell whose whole value is the word; "wrong number" / "returned mail" notes mark the phone or address to check (IMPORT-5, Jonathan's addendum) and block nothing; history notes only propose (`prefsIn`).
- When a workbook has a gift sheet, an amount on the people sheet (Lifetime, Last gift amount, a year's total) is a check, never a gift: "Donors sheet's Lifetime column: $29,405; the gift sheets add up to $29,400. 1 person differs; …". A people-only file keeps reading its amount as one gift and says so.
- The workbook screen asks the server who is already in the org before writing (`/donors/import-combined` with `dryRun` + `matchPeople`), by the write's own rule (`existingFor`, `loadExistingPeople`): "240 already in Steward, 360 new". The rule gained one key: a row with no email or ID, or whose only namesake here has no email, is that person when one person in the org has the name.
- Undo names who stayed and why ("Undone, except for 1 person. … Ansel Ashgrove (a note).") and offers "Remove them too" (the ordinary delete, to the trash). The receipt no longer unmounts Undo after it runs.
- The receipt names the rows combined ("5 rows matched another row by email: …"); "How the counts add up" names each difference between gift rows, gifts, the deposit question and the set-aside list; the deposit question and the set-aside list are recounted after people matching someone deleted are held back.
- Tags, About them, Spouse/Partner and Household columns land on the person (tags, About them, a partner named in the file offered as one household, one household per Household name, Salesforce's "… Household" account). A file name never makes an event or dates gifts. A phone is dropped only when it is the org's own (a signature phone) or on more than 10 people. A source system is named only when one of its own columns is there. 30 deleted-person questions collapse under the bulk choices.

**Tests.** `tests/import8.test.js` (the one new suite: donor data and email). Red on main 14 of 15 (the one pass is a precondition). Planted: the About note read as an opt-out again turned §1 red (3); the people sheet's amount read as a gift again turned §2 red (3). `import-workbook-v3` now says "she applied the note flags" for its 800-row check and holds that unanswered they set nothing; u05's key (a Notes cell "please remove me from your list") is 0 opt-outs.

**Timings** (local, 20 ms on every statement): the 1,005-person workbook previews in 0.8 s and commits in 17.2 s (main 1.0 / 22.3 s; IMPORT-7 18.9 s); a 25,000-person, 25,000-gift workbook 5.5 / 101.7 s (main 7.7 / 117.2 s). The people-sheet check reads who is on each line once (it had been a search over every person per row).

**Not done.** A partner named only in the org (not the file) is not offered. Prod: `scripts/ops/import8-note-optouts-readonly.sql` counts people a note blocked; `scripts/ops/import8-note-optouts.js` (dry run first) turns email back on for those whose block came only from a note. Workbook-screen imports did not record which flags came from notes, so those cannot be told apart after the fact.
