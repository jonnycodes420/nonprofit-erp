## STAFF-MAIL-1 · The morning email is the only staff email that sends unasked (2026-10-30)

Jonathan, after reading the Staff mail list in the console: "the only automatic email should be the Thread … everything else should be a switch they flip", and the morning email "needs a settings tab" for what is in it and when. Before this, with Staff mail on, gift alerts, task assignments, step reminders, Week in Review, the monthly report and connection alerts all went to everyone by default, the last three with no per-person switch at all. Now `userWantsEmail` reads NULL as off for every kind but `thread_nudge`, a kind with no column fails closed, and the three without a switch got one (`notify_week_in_review`, `notify_monthly_report`, `notify_connection_alerts`). The morning email reads the person's own `brief_sections`, `brief_hour` and `brief_days`; the two morning timers became one (`processThreadNudges`, no shared window) and the org's weekend toggle became each person's "every day". A one-shot migration at first boot (`staff_mail_1_opt_in`, one transaction under an advisory lock) only ever turns things off and never writes `org_creo` or `org_b72demo`: gift, task and step alerts off, and a person who had turned off the daily task reminder gets no tasks or meetings section. Nothing is set on, so a person with the Thread email off stays off (her old tasks-only email ends with it) and the org weekend toggle is not carried into "every day". Found on the way: the donor-portal change alert passed `prefKind: "notify_portfolio_gifts"`, a column not a kind, so it went to everyone whatever they had chosen; §12d of `tests/mail-suppression.test.js` scans every `prefKind` and was red on it. A timed step due today was left out of the morning email because its own reminder carried it; with that reminder off by default it now stays in unless the person turned the reminder on.
