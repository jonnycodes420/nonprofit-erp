## IMPORT-4 · A layered import that understands the file and keeps everything (2026-10-27)

- **Why (26 Oct).** A real nonprofit's event workbook (a People sheet with Invited, RSVP, Attended, Table and Mailing list, and a Gifts sheet whose Date column was blank) reached the column mapper, which offered "Import 443 donors + 0 gifts": all 106 gifts ($124,290) refused as "unreadable date", every engagement column defaulted to "Don't import". `gifts.date` was NOT NULL, so an undated gift could not exist anywhere.
- **Scope.** CLAUDE.md caps a build at six items; the brief had about thirty. Shipped, in the brief's order: (1) undated gifts land and count by rule, (2) a combined workbook read as one list of people linked by its ID, (3) every column mapped, kept or skipped with a reason, (4) events, attendance and communication choices, (5) roles (a person with no gift is a contact), (6) the truthful preview and button, and whole undo. The rest is listed in the PR as the next builds.
- **Undated gifts** (built as a parallel fork): `gifts.date` nullable; `recordGift` takes a null date only with `undated: true` and starts nothing; Lifetime, count, average and an event or campaign it is linked to count it; This year, goal periods, retention, lapse and every window do not, and say "N gifts with no date not counted"; first, last and Donor since read dated gifts only; NULLS LAST on every gift date sort; the profile row has "No date" and "Set date"; `POST /imports/:id/date-undated` is "Use the event date" in one click. Help's "How Steward counts" has a "Gifts with no date" section. The checker learned the rule (`undated`) and numbers1-foot two step kinds; three planted defects each went red.
- **The reader** (`shared/sheetRead.js`) reads layers: People merged across sheets (ID, then email, then exact name with no clash), placeholders (a phone or email on 3+ last names) never copied, ZIPs that lost a zero restored; Roles; Money; Engagement and communication choices with their source; everything else kept as a custom field under its own header. A "Gave at dinner" column is skipped only when it foots person by person. Found while building: a totals row "TOTAL 178240" was read as a subtotal because the regex ran over the money cell (IMPORT-3 bug), and "Locker #" was claimed as a donor ID.
- **Test** `import4-layers` (70): the combined workbook (450 people, 106 undated gifts $124,290, 8 pledges $78,950 open, 172 attended of 406 invited, 4 opt-outs, 1 do not contact, three existing people matched and restored exactly by undo), the guest list and gift sheet in either order, and 14 fixtures each footing its money, columns, people, checker and undo. On main: 6 passed, 26 failed. IMPORT-3's messy key for file 27 changed (its unknown column is now kept) and file 28 was added for the one column question that still blocks. Seed unchanged.
