## PROFILE-4 · A short header that scrolls away, and tabs that stay with you (2026-11-24)

Jonathan, on a pilot customer's donor (24 Nov): "the center white box with the name needs to be smaller, all of the info underneath it is hard to get to and you only get a small window. Cut that white window in half... make it so it scrolls with the rest of the info." He approved a mockup the same day, with one change: the right rail keeps its own green (`RAIL.bg`, `T.railGreen`), not the brighter emerald the mockup drew.

**Reproduced first** on staging (f4be415, Harborlight, 1440×900, real clicks from the Donors list): the white band measured 277px from its top to the tabs, and PROFILE-3's `dp3-pin` held the band, the four numbers and the tabs at the top of the middle column together, so after a 600px wheel the tabs were still 277px down and the timeline had a 460px window. Imported donors carry a long "Includes $X from your old system" note under Lifetime, which is likely most of the ~470px on the pilot's records.

**What changed.** The header is one row: a 44px face, the name in the display face at 26px with its tags beside it, and the actions right-aligned in the same row (the row wraps, the name block has a 160px basis, so a very long name wraps beside the actions and the actions only drop below on a narrow column). Under the name, one quiet line: "Donor since 2017", then email · phone · city; "Add email or phone" only when there is neither an email nor a phone. The year is read by `profile/donorSince.js` from the same `/people/:id` answer the rail's role line reads (it is in the profile bundle). The four numbers are one strip with thin dividers (`ProfileNumbers.jsx`), values at 18px, indented to the name's edge; a cell's note sits beside its value on one line and ends in "…" with the whole note as its hover title when the strip is short of room, and only a cell with a note gives way. Each value is still its `<Figure>` and opens its rows; each label still opens its sentence. At 390 the strip is two by two.

**The scroll.** Only the tab row is sticky now (`dp4-pin`); the band scrolls away above it inside the middle column, which stays the one scroller beside the rail (PROFILE-3's columns are unchanged). `profile/useStuckTabs.js` watches a zero-height sentinel above the row: when it passes the top of the column the row shows the small face and the name at its left and a light shadow. A tab switched to while stuck scrolls the column so the new tab starts just under the row; the panes have a minimum height of the column less the row, so a short tab can still sit there. The `max-height:700px` rule that unpinned PROFILE-3's tall pin went: a 46px row can stick on a short laptop. No tab panel had its own fixed height or inner scroller (the only `maxHeight` box under a tab is the recipients list inside an email-as-sent view).

**Measured after** (local production build, 1440×900): 181px for a person with two tags and a contact line, 241px for a 54-character organisation name (it wraps beside the actions, as the brief's "actions stay in the same row" requires). The rail is the same colour, width, order and scroll. **Tests:** `hotfix1-profile` §P4 (header under 200px at 1440, the rail on `T.railGreen`, the tab bar at the top with the name after 600px), red on main and green on the branch; `tests/lib/profileColumns.js` now puts the numbers on the name's edge, not the back link's.

**Follow-up, found on staging after the merge** (e10b748, Harborlight, Margaret Chen, 1440×900): once the tab row stuck, the face and name beside it pushed Notes and Files past the row's edge, and the row's scrollbar is hidden, so two tabs were simply gone. Stuck, the row keeps only the open tab's count (the rest are back the moment the header is; stale1 §P2 reads the open Files tab's count while stuck), closes the gaps from 24px to 18px, and lets the name give way first: it ends in "…" with the whole name on hover, down to the face alone, before any tab leaves the row. Every tab fits beside the name at 1440 and 1280, for a 54-character name too. At 1024 five tabs were already out of sight before this build, stuck or not; that is the next brief's. The same walk showed every note in the numbers strip cut short; the cells now sit 16px apart, and Lifetime's note (the long "from your old system" one) is the one that gives way, so Last gift keeps its date and Last contact its kind and date. `hotfix1-profile` §P4 adds "every tab still in view beside it": red on main (`hidden: ["files"]`), green after.
