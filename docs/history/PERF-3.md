## PERF-3 · Home on a phone under 3 seconds: the first download (2026-11-02)

Brief: Home on a phone under 3 s. perf-app's 3.64 s phone budget failed on main (3.84 s). Reached 3.54 s, not 3 s; what is left is in `docs/defects.md`.

**Measured first.** perf-app's phone (412 px, DevTools slow 4G: 562 ms, 1.4 Mbps; CPU 4.07x), its own 2,000-person org, the local preview, median of 5, every request with its start, end, bytes and priority, cold and warm (a second visit, the cache primed). Main: cold usable 3,864 ms, first paint 3,056, LCP 3,864, **CLS 0.489**, 314 KB on the first look (217 KB of it JavaScript), 30 requests; warm 1,540 ms, CLS 0.513, and both fonts downloaded again on every visit (`/fonts/` answered `max-age=0`). The waterfall named three things: the moves deck's chunk (Insights, 12 KB) was asked for only once App ran (3.16 s) and the LCP waited on it; the deck's 64 px stand-in, then the deck itself, pushed the Thread off the phone's screen (0.41 of CLS, plus 0.08 from Ask's suggestions arriving a frame after Home); and on a warm morning the fonts were the critical path.

**Fonts.** DM Sans's weight axis now starts at 400, the lightest weight Steward draws: 37.0 KB to 24.1 KB (latin) and 18.2 to 12.1 KB (latin-ext), by `fonttools varLib.instancer wght=400:1000 --no-optimize`, which keeps every remaining delta as it was: rendered at 400 to 900 it is pixel-identical (re-normalising to 900, or re-optimising, moved points by under 1/1000 em and was not kept). Glyph subsetting saved 100 bytes on DM Sans and 56 on the serif (already latin subsets) and changed the serif's rendering, so it was not done. Each file is copied under `/assets/` with a hash in its name (`scripts/prerender.mjs`), so vercel.json's year-long immutable cache holds it. Only DM Sans is preloaded; the serif loads when a headline asks. Until a face is in, text draws in a system font sized to it: Arial at 102% (bold 100.71%), Roboto 103.57% (bold 107.08%), Georgia 98.01%, Noto Serif 92.22%, measured on Home's own sentences, with ascent and descent the typeface's own over that, and each fallback covering exactly the characters its typeface draws, so a glyph DM Sans lacks still falls to the system font once it is in. The stacks are written inline in 111 files, so `vite.config.js` adds the fallback names at build time (the marketing site, which loads its own, excepted). Tried and not kept: no font preload at all (3.91 s: DM Sans then landed at 3.86 s and its re-layout became the last long task).

**The first look's shape.** The preloader adds the lazy parts Home's first look draws on `/dashboard` (`data-home-need`), found from the build manifest by a `/* first-look */` mark on the import (`lib/bootReads.js`); a mark that names no chunk fails the build. The deck's code is asked for with Home's reads, and App's first load waits for it and for the two reads that set the first look's shape (the deck and `/home/lead`), never more than 0.8 s, so Home draws once with the deck and Ask's suggestions in place. `api.js takeParked` hands a parked boot answer over synchronously, so the deck and the lead draw from the first frame. CLS: 0.489 to 0.004 cold, 0.578 to 0.004 warm, 0 at 1440.

**Fewer bytes.** Dashboard.jsx no longer computes what it drew nowhere (`goalCardsSection`, `driftSection` and its empty state, `goalDriverHint`, the AI briefing, the old queue's actions and their state): App's chunk 88.4 to 84.1 KB gzipped. react-dom is the production build (129.5 KB raw); the first look's largest parts are react-dom, App.jsx (44 KB raw), Dashboard (29), TopBar (21), AskRail (18), the shared components (72) and `@sentry/core` (14); no date, chart or markdown library is on it. `@vercel/analytics` and Speed Insights (5.5 KB raw, about 2 KB on the wire) stay in the entry: Speed Insights is meant to start early.

**Headers.** Vercel already sends Brotli for every JS and CSS file, but its on-the-fly Brotli is larger than its gzip for react-dom (65,909 against 64,118 bytes); Vercel offers no way to serve pre-compressed files from a static build, so nothing changed there. Hashed assets were already `immutable`; HTML navigations now say `no-cache` (vercel.json, on the CSP's paths, never on the Railway rewrites), and `scripts/ci/local-preview.js` honours a rule's `has` headers as Vercel does. app.html preconnects to the API origin the build uses; the app still calls the Railway address because `VITE_API_URL` on Vercel is Jonathan's switch (`docs/needs-jonathan/SECURE-3.md` item 1), and the preconnect follows it when it moves. The preflight cache was already two hours (SECURE-1).

**After.** perf-app, median of 5: phone 3,540 ms (main 3,836), warm 1,324 (main 1,528), first look 295 KB (main 314; JavaScript 228, from 217, the deck's 15 KB now on it), CLS 0.004 cold and warm. Home at 390 and 1440 after the fonts load: 0 pixels differ from main and the page text is the same.

**Locked** (`tests/perf-app.spec.js`): the phone 3,640 ms (3,540 + 0.1 s), a second visit 1,460 ms (+10%), every byte of the first look 310 KB (+5%), its JavaScript 240 KB (+5%), and CLS under 0.02 cold and warm, worst of the runs. Run against main's build, the phone, warm, bytes and CLS checks all failed (3,836 ms, 1,528 ms, 314 KB, CLS 0.489 / 0.578).

**Why not 3 s.** On the local preview (HTTP/1.1, six connections) the fifteen first-look files queue, each waiting a 562 ms round trip, and the link carries 295 KB in about 2.7 s, 110 KB/s of its 180; then parse and render take about 0.6 s at the phone's CPU. The nine small shared chunks exist because pages outside the app import the same modules; merging them would grow the giving pages, and PERF-2's two chunk plans did. `docs/defects.md` keeps the P2 open with this waterfall.
