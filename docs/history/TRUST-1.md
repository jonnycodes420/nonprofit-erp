## TRUST-1 · One org's request never writes in another org, and a campaign reaches who the setting says (2026-11-04)

- **Why.** The last two open P1s and a P2 a donor would read. `POST /sequences/process` (any org's admin) ran `autoEnroll` -> `ensureAtRiskSequence`, which looped `SELECT id FROM orgs` and inserted an ACTIVE at-risk sequence in every org without one. And a campaign reached only people set to Always include, because `donorMailDecision` refused kind `campaign` unless `auto_email` (SEND-GATE-1); the walk org's builder said "315 donors · 0 can get this email".
- **Jonathan's rule (27 Oct).** Nothing goes to a donor automatically; a send happens only when someone at the org clicks and OKs it. A campaign is a send someone clicks, so `auto_email` governs automatic sends only and a campaign follows SEND-GATE-2's rule for its type.
- **Tenant scope.** `processSequences`, `autoEnroll`, `ensureAtRiskSequence`, `ensureMilestoneSequences`, `processDunning` and `processPledgeReminders` take `{ orgId }`; their request routes (`/sequences/process`, `/recurring/process-dunning`, `/pledges/process-reminders`) pass the caller's org. Only the hourly jobs (routes/jobs.js) call them with none. The last two were found by the sweep: both ran the all-org pass and only filtered the response (FIX-45). The at-risk and milestone sequences Steward makes are made `paused`.
- **The sweep, the rest.** Super-admin only, all-org by design: `retryFailedNotifications`, `reconcileStripeVsGifts`, `processTrialReminders`, `processNetworkGate`, `numberCheck.runAll`. Jobs or boot only: `refreshReconcileDenominator`, `refreshStoredDrift`, `recomputeAllScores`, `checkAllRecordBands`, `runVolunteerRemindersTick`, `processSmartMoves`, `processScheduledCampaigns`, `processSavedReportSchedule`, `processBoardPackSchedule`, `processCardExpiry`, `syncAllGmail`, `runDataHealthNightly`, `convertWorkflows()`, `backfillLinkedTasks`. Already scoped when a route calls them: `processTrackedSequences`, `processWorkflowSweeps`, `processPledgeInstallmentReminders`, `processMembershipRenewals`, `processGivingSources`, `processGrantMilestones`, `refreshCardsOnFile`, the QBO/Xero syncs, `runJourneySweep`, `processJourneyAutoSends`, the auction, photo and geocode queues, and every `...ForOrg(req.user.orgId)` trigger.
- **The campaign rule.** `donorMailDecision` for `campaign`, after the marketing refusals, asks `mailTypes.holdReason` (the same function `sendGate.decide` asks): type on; Everyone and not Never, or Only donors I choose and Always include; not skipped. `donorMailDecisions` reads the type row and skips once for a list. A campaign pressed while Campaign emails are off still goes to the gate and is held (it "waits"), so Going out's Held list is unchanged; the builder counts those people as "wait until Campaign emails are on". The count line joins its reasons with " · ".
- **The merge.** `shared/giftSentence.js` `withoutMissingGift`: for a person with no gift (or no lifetime total), each sentence naming a gift field becomes "Thank you for being part of this." Called by `legacyCampaignBody`, `renderMergeFields` and the block renderer's `mergeText`. The Thank-you and Year-end starters keep their follow-on clause in the same sentence.
- **The picker.** "A group" also offers All donors, Everyone with an email, Lapsing or lapsed and Major, so an org whose one group is Volunteers is not left with only that.
- **Cleanup for Jonathan.** `scripts/ops/trust1-steward-sequences-readonly.sql` lists every Steward-made at-risk or milestone sequence still on; `scripts/ops/trust1-turn-off-steward-sequences.js` turns them off, dry run first. No rows change at deploy.
