## REDTEAM-1 · Steward attacked the way an outsider or the wrong role would, and what that closed (2026-11-06)

Only the build's own local stack, its own databases and made-up orgs were attacked. Every finding got a test that failed on main first.

- **The matrix skipped 398 routes.** `scripts/lib/routeInventory.js` read the auth chain only from `server.js` (rebuilt by `readSource`), so a route written straight into `routes/*.js` or `server/<domain>/` had no chain and was filed as public: no auth-wall check, no cross-tenant probe. It now reads every route module, falls back to the live chain's named middleware for a route registered in a loop, and the matrix fails on any route it can't read (`PUBLIC_UNREAD` lists the push webhooks). Covering them needed org B fixtures for 85 routes (`tests/lib/matrixMoreIds.js`). Every one of the 1,481 routes is probed.
- **Org B's ids in the body** (matrix §8, `tests/lib/bodyCrossProbe.js`). Every authenticated write (736) gets org B's ids in every spelling, all at once and one row at a time, with its path on org A's own rows; then no org A row may point at org B's and org B's counts may not move. The one-at-a-time bodies were added after a planted defect (both fund checks on a gift removed) stayed green under the all-at-once body: the first refusal hid the fund. Nothing crossed.
- **Three routes answered 200 on another org's record**, the matrix's one-answer rule broken, nothing leaked: `GET /donors/:id/address-history`, `GET /donors/:id/bundle`, `DELETE /ask/pins/:id`. All are 404 now.
- **Spreadsheet formulas in exports.** The audit log, the screening file, survey results (typed by the public) and Going out's sent list had no guard; six other exports missed a leading tab or CR. One `server/shared/csvCell.js` for all ten.
- **A material's link could be `javascript:`**, opened with `window.open` on the profile, where the session token lives: one staff member could take over a colleague's session. A web address or nothing, on the server (`webAddress.js`) and in the client.
- **Bad filter values were 500s** (a quote in a date or limit): `/going-out`, its CSV, `/pipeline/officer-activity`, `/annual-fund`, `/finance/audit-log`. Not injection (all bound), now defaults. Every filter in the live router gets quotes and SQL-shaped values in `tests/redteam.test.js` §6.
- **Checked and sound:** the coordinator allowlist across every GET, every admin action the screen hides from staff, /ops and /admin for an org admin, bad and huge amounts, an edit carrying org_id or donor_id, another org's fund, unsigned/forged/replayed Stripe events, double submits (idempotency key), giving-page fee math (server-side), Atlas answers (only its own options), remote photo fetch (safeFetch).
- **Scanners.** Semgrep 1.184.0 (p/javascript, p/nodejsscan, p/owasp-top-ten, p/expressjs): 43 ERRORs, three real, none reachable by a request: a workflow input pasted into a shell (`error-digest.yml`; `ci.yml` and `ship.yml` moved to env too), GCM decipher without a fixed tag length (both callers already checked), and the INTERVAL built from an integer (now a bound parameter). The njsscan rules read and found false here are excluded in `scripts/ci/semgrep-gate.sh`; seven checked SQL lines carry `nosemgrep` with their reason. The default 5 s rule timeout had skipped 651 file/rule pairs; with 60 s, 26 more ERRORs showed on main: 24 `tainted-sql-string` on dynamic WHERE builders (routes/groups.js, routes/search.js, routes/volunteer.js, server/donors/{people,records,routes}.js, server/reports/fundraising.js, server/tasks/routes.js, server/boot/helpers-4/5.js) not read one by one in this build, and two read false (`API_KEY_PREFIX`, the org guard on `PATCH /orgs/:id`). Semgrep is a CI job (`semgrep`) that `test` waits for, as a RATCHET: `--baseline-commit` (the merge base on a PR, the previous commit on a push), so a NEW ERROR finding fails and only changed files are read (153 s locally).
