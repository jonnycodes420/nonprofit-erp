# ASKS-2 · What a real customer's file showed on its first day

12 Nov 2026. A customer org (6,445 people, Salesforce history back to 2013) showed three things on its first day. Reproduced on staging (Harborlight, read only, build 2427f37) before any fix; every fixture in the suite is made up.

## W-ASK-jump · a step up more than doubled the ask
`suggestedAskCents` rounded the anchor UP to a friendly rung ($35,712 to $50,000) and then stepped one more rung ($75,000), 2.1 times the largest gift. Staging showed the same shape on Harborlight: "Ask $10,000: largest gift $5,420, last 3 averaged $5,160 and rising" (1.85 times). The step now starts from the natural ask of the anchor (`naturalAskCents`) and lands on the first step rung at least a quarter above it, never above 1.5 times the anchor (`stepUpCents`, shared/smartAmounts.js). The step rungs are 1 / 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 / 2 / 2.5 / 3 / 3.5 / 4 / 4.5 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 7.5 / 8 / 9 per decade, kept where natural, so above $10,000 they are $5,000 apart up to $50,000. The profile's screening step reads the same function. Planned change: sweep1-home §6 ($2,700 rising) asks $3,500, was $5,000.

## W-DATE-gone · "Create tasks for Nov 1" on Nov 12
The Year-end card's task label was a literal. It is now computed from the day (server/insights/taskSpread.js): on or before Nov 1 it names the season's first working day; after it, tasks are due from today and spread like ATLAS-7's first calls (the spread moved into the same module; groupAsk.js reads it). Every other card and Insight action was swept: none named a date. The stored Insights answer is keyed by day and a data fingerprint, so an answer built on the old code would have stayed "fresh" after the deploy; the engine's version is now part of the fingerprint (insightsData.js ENGINE).

## W-NOTGIFT-bulk · one decision, 160 clicks
"Rows that may not be gifts" is grouped by the reason and the memo's pattern (shared/numbers/notGiftGroups.js: the person's name, years, seasons, months and numbers taken out). A group of two or more rows takes the row's three decisions for all of it, with a live preview (Lifetime giving before and after, and who would become a contact) and one Undo (insight_reviews.batch_id). The single-row decisions moved whole into server/insights/reviewDecide.js so a group does exactly what a row's button does; the group route re-checks each row's membership and never touches an id outside the group. A group's Not a gift takes the donor role off people left with no gift (donorType.js), and Undo gives it back (insight_reviews.roles_prev). Raffle rule unchanged.
