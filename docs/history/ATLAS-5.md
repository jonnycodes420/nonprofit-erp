# ATLAS-5 · Atlas reads dates right, starts empty, and gets out of the way (8 Nov 2026)

From the fleet's lead of 8 Nov. Each item was reproduced first, with real clicks on a production build (NODE_ENV=production, the built client through local-preview), on a made-up org shaped like the fleet's description (Lantern Cove Rowing: two Walters, a Sterling, a Juniper with a lunch note).

- **Dates (W-1108-Atlas-20).** `whenFromWords` added a further week to "next <weekday>" whenever the day was fewer than three days off, so "next Tuesday" on a Sunday was Tuesday a week later, and on a Saturday the coming one. The rule now lives in `shared/atlasDates.js`: "this X" is today or the coming X, "next X" / "on X" / "X" the first X after today; "in N days", "end of month". Atlas's today is `todayIn(org timezone)`; the suite's table runs at 9:30 PM New York, when UTC is the next day, which is how a UTC today would show itself. Every proposed date says its weekday.
- **The empty box (01X).** The box was empty; the example was its placeholder, but in Chrome (which listens) it sat under "What I heard", so it read as words Atlas had heard. The placeholder now says "For example: …" (typing) or "Your words show here as you talk." (listening), and Send waits for her own words.
- **Letting go (08X, 08).** A proposal had Save and Edit and no Discard, so an unwanted one waited for ever; a "Which Walter?" left unanswered was in no list at all. Proposals now discard (one UPDATE, only while unsaved; Save refuses after), every waiting bell line has Dismiss, and her open questions are read into the bell from the requests themselves (so the old ones show too) with Answer and Dismiss. The bell panel closes with Escape, × and a click outside, and gives focus back to the bell.
- **Dedupe (02X).** A read that asked about the same person twice got two identical answers; answers are now one per person, and a person's notes are listed once by id.
- **The whole message is the note (03X).** A note took the model's words, so "QA A5" was dropped from it and came back as a request Atlas couldn't do. A note is now cut from her message like a conversation (`shared/atlasNote.js noteText`); only "and also …" splits it.
- **Can't know (07).** "Steward can't answer "sterling's favorite color" yet." is now "I don't have Sterling's favorite color. I can add it as a note if you tell me."
- **Speed (23).** The read goes to the smaller model; a person's summary, finding someone and questions for Ask are read in code with no model read; the AI switch and the org's voice are read once per request.
- Old rows: `scripts/ops/atlas5-old-rows.js` (a dry run) lists reminders saved on the late Tuesday and notes saved without the end of her message.
