## ARCH-1 · The rules of the house, and the plumbing that makes builds collide (2026-11-01)

MAP-1 drew the map; this build wrote the rules and took away what made builds collide. `docs/architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md` is the house: domains (server code goes to `server/<domain>/`), one modular monolith (a layered core, plug-ins for integrations and readers, a queue for background work; not microservices), one core with many faces, no org-specific code, queries in bulk with messy-25k as the yardstick, one home per number (`shared/numbers/`, NUMBERS-3), the size rule (no new file over 1,500 lines), tests that set their own clock, and where scripts and generated files go. CLAUDE.md's first line sends every session there. `docs/decisions/house.md` holds six decisions that cross every area. The ratchet, `scripts/ci/arch-check.js`, runs in CI's guards job and the pre-push hook against `docs/architecture/baseline/`, counts only go down (raising one is a hand edit with its reason): big files, gift-summing or date-windowing SQL in routes, hand-rolled fiscal years, scripts in the scripts/ root, real-clock reads in tests, org ids in app code, and images over 500 KB; each of the eight plants went red. The collisions: `docs/HISTORY.md` became `docs/history/` (one file per brief; `scripts/ci/history-index.js`), `NEEDS-JONATHAN.md` became `docs/needs-jonathan/` (`scripts/ci/needs-jonathan.js`), `run-all.sh` finds its suites itself (`tests/lib/suites.js`; a skip is `// battery: skip — <reason>` in the suite's own file; 188 before, 188 after, same names, 189 with SWEEP-1's), and the route inventory and suite timings are generated into `.generated/`, never committed. CI: a docs-only PR runs lint and the ratchet only; perf budgets warn in CI and stay strict locally (`tests/lib/budget.js`, `PERF_STRICT=1`); the tree-check skip on main is deliberate. Six CI flakes fixed at their cause, two of them real bugs: a profile read during the post-import score recompute could 500 on `donor_scores_pkey` (one recompute per org at a time, with an advisory lock), and day and month windows were cut at the database's midnight, so an evening gift in New York counted in the next month (`orgTime.orgDayWindowSql`; parity4-started §6 at 11 PM ET on a month's last day and across the 1 Nov DST change). Dead weight: 113 one-time scripts deleted (`docs/architecture/scripts-removed.md`), 20 with no recorded prod run kept in `scripts/once/`, 52 moved to `scripts/ops/` and `scripts/ci/` with every caller updated (`scripts/README.md`); 14 unimported files and 143 dead exports removed; 3 routes deleted under amendment A1 (`docs/architecture/routes-reviewed.md`); docs/ went from 567 MB to 10 MB. `/annual-fund` counted donors by fiscal year beside calendar-year money (`computeRetentionRate` takes `startMonth`; numbers2-history §4, red on main). Nothing on a screen changed: the built bundle's path strings are identical. Left in `docs/defects.md`.
