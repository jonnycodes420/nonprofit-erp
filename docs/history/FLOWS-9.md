## FLOWS-9 · Refunds counted one way, a preview that tells the truth, three repeats closed (2026-11-13)

Night two on staging had FLOWS-7 and FLOWS-8 deployed, and three of FLOWS-7's items still failed. This round, every item was first reproduced on staging with Playwright clicks (read-only in Harborlight; every write the app tried on its own was blocked), and each repeat says why the first fix missed.

**Why the three repeats missed.**
- *Morning email team row (Volunteers-19).* FLOWS-7 made the team row count tasks by Tasks' rule, and it did: the row's "Dana Reyes · 32 open · 8 overdue" is exactly `/tasks/counts?staff=Dana`. The fleet compared it with Tasks' **Mine** (52 / 11), which for an admin also holds the 20 unassigned tasks, because Tasks' **One person's tasks** filter never rendered: `Tasks.jsx` read `useAuth()` (which is `{ auth, … }`) as the auth itself, so `isAdmin` was always false. Nobody could open the view the row names. Fixed, and the email now says under the row what each line is and that Mine also holds the unassigned. The footer read nothing: it said "the morning email is on" to anyone with an address. It now reads her setting, and with it off says this is a preview and the email is off, with the switch beside the preview in Settings.
- *Video draft (Fundraising-19).* FLOWS-7 resolved only a well-formed `/v/<16+ char token>` on this host, and called a recording "there" when it had an asset id. A cut token on this host was asked about by nobody; a link on another of Steward's hosts was asked by HEAD and passed; a row whose recording was gone from the store passed. Now every video page on any Steward host is resolved against the video row (this org, playable: the asset is in the store) or read, and the not-here page is broken. A draft that fails is never sent by "Send all reviewed". On staging today Margaret Chen's draft opens (its seed rewrote token and link together), so the brief's existing row no longer carries the defect; `scripts/ops/flows9-old-rows.js` lists any that do, per org.
- *Periods (Fundraising-05).* FLOWS-7's `MoneyPeriod` labels do render on staging ("FY 2026–27 · Jul 1, 2026 to Jun 30, 2027" beside "Annual Fund 2026 · Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2026"), but a date on each card did not tell anyone the cards disagree. One line now says so under the cards when they differ (`server/reports/periodsDiffer.js`).

**Refunds (Fundraising-01, Finance-27X).** Staging, FY 2026–27: Giving summary 649, Fundraising card 651, Gifts by fund 650. Three rules: `COUNTED` (refunded in full is out), a bare `amount > 0` (`givingBetween`, `GIVERS`, the givers source, By year, the report builder, `/annual-fund`), and a count of rows (What they support counted the gift and its refund row, 2). Gifts by fund's 650 was a split gift counted once per fund line in the footer. Every one now reads `shared/numbers/giftCount.js`; the gift dialog lists refund rows marked "Refund" and the original "Refunded in full", and its count line says the gifts. An average is over each gift at its net. The soft-credit box's "$25 beside Lifetime $0" was a stale read after a refund: the profile now reads it and What they support again after one. Taking a refund row back un-marks its gift (`refunds.js REMARK_IN_FULL_SQL`).

**Giving page preview (Fundraising-36).** The preview showed $150 / $500 / $1,500 because the page had `smartAmounts` on, a flag the editor never showed. The preview now shows the amounts she typed, says in a line what the live page offers instead when the flag is on, and the editor has the switch. Cover fees did not toggle because the preview's capture-phase click handler both cancelled the click and, by opening "The gift" in the editor, re-rendered the page before the click reached the box, so React put it back unticked and never called onChange. A control's click is now left to do its work and the section opens after it. The preview also no longer counts form views or records a typed email as an abandoned gift start.
