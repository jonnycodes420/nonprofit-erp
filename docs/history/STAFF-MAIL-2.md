## STAFF-MAIL-2 · The weekly email is the default (2026-10-31)

Jonathan, after STAFF-MAIL-1 shipped: first "it should be naturally switched off until turned on", then the plan this build shipped: everyone new starts with one weekly email and nothing else. `users.notify_weekly` was added with no default, so every existing person reads it off, and then given `DEFAULT true`; `notify_thread_nudge` got `DEFAULT false`. No row was written at deploy. "Your week" (`runWeeklyEmailForOrg`, server.js) goes Monday from 7 AM in the org's time zone on the existing morning tick, once per person per week (`digest_sends` type `weekly_email`), behind the org's Staff mail gate before anything is reserved. It is built from parts that already answer elsewhere: the morning email's ranked steps and task list run to Sunday, unpaid pledge installments and events this week, and last week from the `gifts` figure, Home's `unthanked` figure from Home's own start date (`reportHooks.thanksSince`), and donorStatus's lapsed rule for who lapsed. A week with nothing in it sends one line and the link to Home. Its footer and Settings → Your email (renamed from My email, as the brief names it) both offer "Turn on the daily email"; the server turns the weekly one off on that turn and says so, and Undo restores both. The weekly email's unsubscribe link turns off only the weekly email.
