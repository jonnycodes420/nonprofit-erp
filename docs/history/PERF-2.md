## PERF-2 · Home on a phone, faster (2026-11-01)

Brief: Home on a phone under 3 s (SWEEP-6 measured 4.5 s). Reached 3.4 to 3.5 s, not 3 s; what is left is in `docs/defects.md`. Measured first, with Playwright at 390 on DevTools slow 4G (562 ms, 1.4 Mbps, the CPU calibrated to a Moto G at about 4x) and at 1440, cold cache, on two orgs: the three real-shaped files imported (`scripts/ops/sweep2-walk-org.js`) and messy-25k (25,033 people, 90,523 gifts). Before: usable 4,437 / 4,691 ms on the phone, 468 / 644 ms on a desktop, 261 KB of JavaScript on the first look, 51 requests, and on messy-25k a 1.9 MB (467 KB on the wire) deck read. After: 3,4xx ms on the phone for both orgs, 410 to 600 ms on a desktop, 214 KB, 31 requests, a 38 KB deck read. perf-app (its own 2,000-person org): phone 3,537 ms, desktop 464 ms.

The measurement found that the phone was not only bandwidth-bound. Home's first look waited on four things in turn: the scoped reads, asked for only once `/dashboard/my-stats` had come back (one more round trip, about 600 ms); the fonts, which a browser fetches only when text first uses them, so they left Google's two hosts after Home's first render and made the phone lay the page out again; the app's code, whose preloader sat after the render-blocking stylesheet and waited 750 ms for it; and the boot reads, which the entry started only once react-dom had downloaded. And on the local preview (HTTP/1.1, six connections a host) every small chunk waited its turn.

What changed. Out of Home's first look: `MorningBrief.jsx` (out of MeetingPanels, with Add to a donor's record; the after-meeting form loads on "Write it down"), `GoalRow.jsx` (out of Dashboards), `ThreadMenus.jsx` (ThreadDismissMenu and PutItOnMyCalendar out of LogConversation; the log form loads on its click), `MailboxBanner.jsx` (the banner and the one `/mailbox` read, out of InboxConnect), the moves deck (lazy, its own "Reading your donors…" card holding its place), the score breakdown panel (ScoreWhy loads it from its modal), the confirm dialog (MailTypes asks for it when it asks), the changelog's product words (with the entries), and the pinned answers, which every Home downloaded with Ask's whole panel: `PinsIfAny` was written for this and drew itself instead of the pins, and nothing drew it. Fonts: DM Sans (one variable file) and DM Serif Display are Steward's own (`client/public/fonts/`, OFL), their faces written into app.html's head and the latin files preloaded; App adds its stylesheet only where the shell did not (two copies of the faces downloaded each font twice). The preloader in app.html sits ahead of the entry, adds its links on the next turn (added at once they sometimes beat the entry to the connections), and starts the boot's five bundles itself; `api.js` prefetch takes those answers by address (`lib/homeReads.js` `bootBundles`/`bundlePath`, one list for both). Its CSP hash in vercel.json moved with it. Reads: the seven boot reads ride in one bundle; Home's scoped reads ride in the first, the server resolving `{scope}` by Home's own rule; the idle guard's `/me/mfa` joined the shell's bundle; the two Atlas bells (one hidden by CSS) share one read. The deck's first read (`/insights?view=deck`) carries each tab's figures, its first two moves, how many it carries and the moves Home records as shown (the same 50 a tab as before, so What worked counts the same), and not the ladder (1 MB on messy-25k, drawn only by its own panel); every move loads on her first touch, hover, focus, Next or tab (`&moves=all`). Home's bundle reads: the same statement counts on the 287-person and the 25,000-person org (30 / 77 / 11 / 68 / 137), so no query per row; the slowest read is `/donors/counts` at 150 ms on messy-25k, one query, left in `docs/defects.md`. In TEST_MODE the bundle's Server-Timing carries its statement count.

Tried and not kept: `experimentalMinChunkSize` (renamed the entry chunks and grew the first look to 258 KB), a manual "home-core" chunk (Rollup pulled react and the API client into it, so every page would have loaded it), `fetchpriority="low"` on the preloads, and the fonts ahead of the scripts (both made the fonts download twice under DevTools' disabled cache).

Locked: perf-app's phone budget 4.8 → 3.8 s (strict locally, a warning in CI), Home's first-look JavaScript at most 225 KB (214 + 5%, bytes, strict everywhere), none of Home's bundled reads asked twice, and the preloader's hash present in vercel.json's script CSP. The phone budget and the JavaScript budget failed on main's code; the twice check and the CSP check failed on planted defects.
