## EDITOR-1 · One Steward editor for pages and email (2026-10-29)

Jonathan: the giving page editor (GIVE-3) is the trademark editor, and email did not work that way (campaigns and one-person templates showed the same words twice, in a form and in a preview; the Cover photo panel spanned the giving page editor). Now anything a donor sees opens in ONE shell, `StewardEditor.jsx`: top bar (Back, name and type badge, Phone / Desktop, the draft's state, History / Revert / Send me a test, one primary), a left rail of folding sections, one preview at a time (Side by side is gone), and a right rail only when the type needs one; below 900 px the rails are sheets. The preview is where things are edited. Giving pages: the headline and story are typed in the page (`InlineText`, plain text, never re-rendered under the caret), the photo is clicked for its focus with Replace and Remove over it (`PhotoTools`), each block shows up, down, duplicate, remove and a "+" under it, and a picked text or quote block is typed in place; the Details/Page/Design modes became rail sections (Details, Layout with the form's position, Cover photo, The gift, Where the gift goes, Goal, Trust panel, Other ways to give, After they give, Blocks, Design). Event pages: the card on the event screens opens the page in the shell, its preview being the page `/e/:slug` sends (the handler became `eventPageHtml`, shared by `GET /events/:id/page/preview` with `editable`), the name and description typed in the page, the cover, film, address, times and the night's photographs in the rail. Email: `EmailEditor.jsx` (templates, one-person emails, starters, journey steps) and `CampaignEditor.jsx` (campaigns) render the server's own email with `editable: true` (`renderEmail` marks `data-st-edit` regions and draws every field as a chip, `data-st-token`, filled for the person previewed), read the typed words back with chips as `{{field}}`, put a chip at the caret from "+ Field", preview as any real person ("Preview as", `as`, org-scoped), and wire handles into the sandboxed frame from outside (no script runs in it). The receipt's and year-end statement's tax lines stay one locked block: never marked, no Remove. New email blocks: divider and gift summary (the person's own gift; nobody with no gift sees a box). Campaigns keep their own blocks (`emailBlocks` on POST/PUT), an older HTML campaign is converted on open (`htmlToBlocks`: paragraphs, headings, lists, a link-only paragraph as a button, pictures as photo blocks, inline links with their address), and unsaved blocks preview through `POST /email-preview`; "Edit in editor" from a campaign to its template is gone, the campaign's email is edited where it is. History for an email lists its saves and publishes from the audit trail (`GET /email-templates/:id/history`).

Part 3. The campaign audience said "All donors", "Nobody yet", "1,183 left out" and "Send to 0" at once; `segment-preview` now answers `audience` (one line whose counts add up: "1,183 donors · 0 can get this email: 21 have no email address, 1 has an address that bounced, 1,161 have automatic email off.", each count opening its people), and while Donor mail is off the people it would reach are their own group ("3 can't get email while Donor mail is off for …") and Send to N stays disabled with that sentence. Send it later starts empty and a time that has gone is refused by the server. There is always a photo: 40 Unsplash-licence photos in eight mission groups (`assets/stock/`, `LICENSES.md`, `shared/stockPhotos.js`, `routes/stockPhotos.js`, `StockPhotoPicker.jsx`), stored per org through assetStore on first use and marked `stock: true` until replaced; a new giving page, event and photo block starts with one from the org's mission group. Steward makes no starter pages or emails at signup, so nothing needed the "Starter: make it yours" mark. Found on the way: CAMPAIGN-2's countdown, matching challenge and recent gifts had no case in the page widget validator, so a page carrying them (the demo's year-end page) could not be saved again ("Unknown widget type."); fixed, `editor1` §7.
