What this does not change

  • The ink right rail stays, on its contrasting ground. It gains the contact block.
  • All four figures stay, and each still opens to the rows behind it, footing to the cent.
  • Giving by year, the next step, proposals and every tab are untouched.
  • The roles themselves are unchanged. Only how they are drawn changes. The + menu holds the same "Volunteer" and "Staff and board" actions.

The one judgement call

"Donor since 2021" needs a first-gift year. Where a record has no gifts the line reads "Prospect · Volunteer", and where a person has no role at all it renders nothing rather than an empty line. Say if you would rather it always show something.
