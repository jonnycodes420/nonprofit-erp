The playbook

The journey is a document you write, not a chart you configure: one plain sentence per step, in the order they happen, editable in place. The ruler above gives the shape at a glance. The bet is that a fundraiser does not want a Gantt chart — she wants to read what she has promised to do, and change a word of it.
