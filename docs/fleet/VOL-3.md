# VOL-3 · For the fleet

**Surfaces changed:** Volunteers header (the four numbers are now figures that open their rows: Active volunteers,
Pending applications, Schedule conflicts this week, Shifts short this week, each with a link under it to its screen).
Volunteers → People → All volunteers (a Status column and a Status filter; the old "Active only" filter now reads
"Served in the last year, or on a shift to come"). The title sentence ("N active volunteers on the roster…"). A
person's record → the new **Volunteer** tab (status with Pause / Mark as former / Make active and Undo; "Can give"
with Edit for hours a week, days and roles; a shift dated after today says "not counted yet"). Reach → Applications
(only what came through the volunteer page; an approved one whose person is gone says so and has Delete). An event's
page → Volunteer team (its shifts, who is on each, Add a shift). Erase this person (now takes their applications).
