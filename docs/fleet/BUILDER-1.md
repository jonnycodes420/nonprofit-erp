receipt, a campaign starter, a designed email), a journey step's email, and Communications → Campaigns → New
campaign / a draft campaign. Each is now three panes: Elements / Blocks / Fields on the left, the email on cream in
the middle, settings on the right (a small picture of the whole email, then Edit and Style for the part clicked, or
"This email" with name, subject, preview line, who it's from, background and Checked against). The top bar has
Undo and Redo. The path line under it reads "Email › Body › Text" for what is selected.
