title: Pledges due at year end, one total on every import, and a giving form that waits for payments
product: crm
---
When you import pledges and choose "Same as the event date", the first installment is now due December 31 of the event's year, then every December 31, so nobody is overdue the week after the dinner. Pledges you already imported haven't changed. If you want to move them, Data health lists them with their dates before and after, and you can undo the move. Each import receipt now gives one total, split into gifts and first payments on pledges, and an event counts each person once. Your organization's main giving form shows "Not open yet" until payments are connected, the same as your giving pages.
