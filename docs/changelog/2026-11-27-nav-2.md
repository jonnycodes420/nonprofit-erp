title: A shorter sidebar, and anything else one click away
product: crm
---
New organizations start with a shorter sidebar: Auctions, Peer-to-peer, Memberships and Major Gifts stay off until you use them. Settings, then Menu, now lists everything that is not on your sidebar with a sentence on what it is for and an Add button, and anything you no longer use can come off the same way. Until Major Gifts is on your sidebar, its pipeline, portfolios and plans are in Fundraising under Major gifts.
