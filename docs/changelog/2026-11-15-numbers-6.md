date: 2026-11-15
title: Each person's giving totals stay right after every change
product: crm
---
The giving totals kept on each person, which the donor list, groups, exports and staff emails read, are now updated by one step after every change to a gift, including the sample data, emptying the trash, and a postdated gift on the day it arrives. A ledger line no longer outlives a gift deleted with its person.
