title: Remove anything you added, and a picture on every page and item
product: crm
---
Every list you can add to now has a Remove in its More menu: ticket types, sponsorship levels, guests, the waiting list, tables, auction items and winners, giving, volunteer and peer-to-peer pages, teams, membership levels, volunteer roles and shifts, groups, funds and campaigns. It asks once and says what happens. Anything with people or money on it is retired or archived rather than deleted, so every past guest, gift and report keeps it, removing a guest never refunds them, and Undo puts everything back for ten seconds. Auction items take up to five photos, and every page has a cover photo control at the top of its editor that keeps the subject in frame on a phone.
