title: Every count matches the list it opens, and Last contact means the last contact
product: crm
---
The number beside Tasks is now the tasks due today or overdue, says so when you point at it, and moves the moment you add or finish one. Last contact on a profile is the latest call, meeting, email, event they came to, or note you mark as a conversation, and the header, About and Engagement all say the same day. A follow-up you set when you log a call is yours, so it shows in your Tasks and on Home, and Re-engage now has a Lost and Found tab for people whose last gift was more than two years ago.
