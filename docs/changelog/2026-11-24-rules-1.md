title: Volunteer hours count once served, everywhere, and a contact is never called a donor
product: crm
---
A shift logged for a future day no longer adds to a person's hours on their record, the Donors list's hours column, report columns, year-end statements or hours milestones; it counts once its day comes. Search calls someone who has not given yet a contact, the same as their record. The LYBUNT report counts in one unit and says what it is, and the "Lapsing or lapsed" email audience says how it differs from Re-engage.
