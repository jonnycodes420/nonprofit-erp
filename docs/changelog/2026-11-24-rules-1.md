title: Volunteer hours count once served, everywhere, and a contact is never called a donor
product: crm
---
A shift logged for a future day no longer adds to a person's hours anywhere (their record, the Donors list, reports, year-end statements, milestones) until its day comes, and search calls someone who has not given yet a contact, the same as their record. The LYBUNT report counts in one unit and says what it is, and the "Lapsing or lapsed" email audience says how it differs from Re-engage. You can mark someone as a former volunteer right from the line under their name, or from More, with Undo.
