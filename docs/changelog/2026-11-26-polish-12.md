date: 2026-11-26
title: Giving charts stay readable, and the Top 100 is now a choice
product: crm
---
On a donor's Giving tab, the years under the chart no longer run into each other: with many years they shorten to "FY17" or show every other year, and each bar still names its full year when you point at it. A fall from $24,000 to $50 now reads "99.8%" instead of "100%", and a name ending in "Inc." no longer gets a second period. The Top 100 list on Major Gifts is now a setting under Settings, Major gifts: turn it on if you keep one, and Major Gifts opens on Pipeline when it is off.
