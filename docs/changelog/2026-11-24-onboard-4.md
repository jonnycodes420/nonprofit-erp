Every organization now starts Steward with a call with Jonathan, who sets it up with you. The website's one button is Book a call: a short form, then a time on his calendar, and you can send your donor file ahead so he has looked before you talk. Pricing depends on the size of your donor file and comes with hands-on setup, and you see it in writing before you sign.
