The "?" in the top bar now opens a help assistant you can talk to. Ask how to do something, or why something looks wrong, and it answers from Steward's help articles and what it can see in your own organization, like when PayPal last synced, with the articles it used and a button to the right screen. It only reads and never changes anything, and "Talk to Jonathan" sends him the whole conversation whenever you would rather ask a person. The help center on our website has the same assistant for anyone thinking about Steward, and eleven new help articles cover Auctions, Peer-to-peer, Memberships, To confirm and more.
