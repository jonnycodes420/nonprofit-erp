date: 2026-11-04
title: Profiles open quickly right after you record a gift
product: crm
---
Recording a gift no longer makes the next profile you open wait while Steward rereads your whole file. Next moves and Insights are prepared in the background and appear a moment later, so every other screen stays quick for everyone on your team. The starter groups and the Retention report also open much faster on large files, with the same numbers as before.
