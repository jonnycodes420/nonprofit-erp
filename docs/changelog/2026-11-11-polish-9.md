date: 2026-11-11
title: Donors see your public name at checkout, and your Board names its years
product: platform
---
When a donor pays, Stripe's checkout now shows your organization's public name and the fund's public name, never the name you use inside Steward. The Board view names each period by its fiscal year ("Giving, FY 2026–27"), an old import from before Steward checked imports says so and can be archived, and editing a gift shows the new total the moment you save.
