title: Bring in your old spreadsheets
product: crm
---
Upload an old grant tracker, board report or budget from Grants or Reports, in Excel or CSV. Steward shows you what it found before anything is saved: grants go into your pipeline, and a table of numbers is kept under Reports with the original file attached, so last year's numbers sit beside this year's. Any column Steward can't place is kept with the file, never dropped.
