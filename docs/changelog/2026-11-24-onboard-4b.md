title: A guided setup on Home, and you can send us your donor file
product: crm
---
A new organization now sees five steps on Home: make it yours, bring your people, see who to call, connect what you use, and invite your team. Each step ticks itself when it is really done, and you can skip any and come back to it. To bring your people you can import a file, start from your Stripe or PayPal history, or send the file to us: you see it go from Received to Being mapped to Ready for you to check, and nothing goes in until a person presses Load.
