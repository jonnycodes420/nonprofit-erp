title: Moving someone to the Trash takes their money off Finance, and deletes ask about what is attached
product: crm
---
When you move a person to the Trash, their gifts now leave every fund balance on Finance, Restore puts them back to the cent, and if receipts were issued to them Delete asks whether to void those first. Deleting a grant, a transaction, an ask, a program, a journey or a volunteer's hours now names what is attached, moves it to the Trash and offers Undo, and a grant with a gift or pledge asks whether to keep that money on the funder. Settings → Trash keeps its count right after a restore, and its search narrows the list as you type.
