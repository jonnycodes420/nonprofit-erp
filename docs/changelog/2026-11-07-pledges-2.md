title: Pledge payments apply the way a bookkeeper expects, and you can record a recurring plan by hand
product: crm
---
A pledge payment now pays the oldest installment due first, and anything over it carries to the next one. A payment above what is left on the pledge asks whether to record the extra as its own gift or reduce the payment to the balance, and writing off or deleting a pledge asks first and can be undone. You can now record a recurring plan for a check every month, a bank draft or a payroll gift, add its payments, and see when the next one is expected and whether it is behind.
