title: Nothing that needs you slips
product: crm
---
When a donor or a funder writes to your connected mailbox and nobody has answered after a business day, Steward puts "Reply to" them on your Thread. Newsletters, receipts and out-of-office replies are left alone. The people who missed an event become one "Missed you at" task with a draft for each of them waiting in Drafts, and the calendar shows the monthly gifts expected each day. A person with a monthly gift can't be deleted by mistake: Steward tells you their plan is still running and offers to archive them instead.
