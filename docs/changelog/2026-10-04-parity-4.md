title: Money that slips, lists in plain words, and a seating chart
product: crm
---
Recurring now shows the cards your donors' banks updated by themselves, and Fundraising lists gifts people started on your own forms and did not finish, each with a short note you can edit and send. Ask Steward for a list the way you would say it, such as "donors who gave last year but not this year", and it shows the filters in words, the people, and saves it as a group in one tap. Events have a seating chart you drag guests onto and a check-in that scans each ticket's QR code with a phone.
