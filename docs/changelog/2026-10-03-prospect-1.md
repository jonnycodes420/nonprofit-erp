title: Room to give: see who could give more, and why
product: crm

Ask "Who could give more?" from Home and Steward lists the people whose own giving shows room to grow, each with the reasons from their record, the colleague who knows them best and a step to plan a visit, start the major donor journey or set the ask. You can prepare a file for any wealth screening provider and bring their results back, stored as ranges with the provider's name and date, and look up a foundation's public tax filing on its record. Only admins and staff you give the major gifts permission can see any of it.
