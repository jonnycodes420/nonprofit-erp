title: Every email opens in the email designer
product: crm
---
Every email in Templates now opens in the same designer, with your brand, blocks and photos: the receipt, the year-end statement, the one-person thank-yous and the campaign starters too. A receipt keeps its tax lines in a block that can't be removed, and what you save there is what the next draft says. A donor who gives every year is no longer tagged as returning after a gap, and the Sequences link on Home opens Sequences.
