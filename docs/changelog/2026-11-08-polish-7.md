title: Words that match what happened
product: crm
---
A note to a foundation's own address now opens "Dear friends at" the foundation, and a contact is greeted by name only when the note goes to them. Writing off a pledge says "Wrote off $450", a campaign that reached nobody no longer counts as sent, and the audit log reads as sentences, never raw data. A person who is Current because of a household gift says whose gift and when, and the brand preview shows your own name and logo.
