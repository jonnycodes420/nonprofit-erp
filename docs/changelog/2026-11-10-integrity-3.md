date: 2026-11-10
title: Undoing an import never deletes anyone for good
product: crm
---
Undoing an import now puts everyone back where they were before it. People the file added go to the Trash, where you can restore them for 30 days, and people it brought back from the Trash return there with their original details: who deleted them, when and why. Someone only leaves Steward for good when you empty the Trash or after their 30 days, and the Audit log keeps a line for every person an undo or the Trash moves. The Trash page and the Everyone breakdown now always show the same count.
