title: Your evening counts as today
product: crm
---
Every window that looks back over days now follows your organization's own calendar. A gift banked, a thank-you sent or a task finished at 8 PM is counted on that day, not the next one, in Finance, Journeys, volunteer hours, surveys, grant heads-ups and the rest. The same holds at the end of a month, the end of your fiscal year and the days the clocks change.
