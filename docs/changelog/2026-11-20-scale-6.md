title: Donors opens quickly on a busy day, and totals move as you save
product: platform
---
Filtering Donors by giving level, lapsed, lapsing or retained now answers in a fraction of a second on a large list, even when your whole team is working at once. The counts above the list come back faster too. When Steward is very busy it now says so in a few seconds and tries once more for you, instead of leaving a page waiting. And after you record a gift, mark a next step done or assign owners, the team totals and the Tasks count change on the screen you're on, without a reload.
