title: Volunteers can be paused or marked former, and everything you added is editable
product: volunteers
---
Each volunteer now has a status, Active, Paused or Former, with the day it was set and who set it, and Mark as former comes with Undo while keeping their hours and history. The volunteer's own tab on their record lets you change their roles, days and hours a week, shifts dated after today no longer count as hours given, and an event page now shows its volunteer shifts and who is on them, with Add a shift right there. The numbers across the top of Volunteers open the people behind them, and erasing a person now takes their volunteer applications too.
