title: Ask Why and What, and ask about one person
product: crm
---
Home and every donor's record now have two buttons, Why and What. Each opens a list of questions your own records can answer, and every answer offers the next questions to ask. You can now ask about one person in plain words, like "what should I ask Flavia for?", and Steward answers from her own gifts and conversations, with a suggested ask and a call you can plan.
