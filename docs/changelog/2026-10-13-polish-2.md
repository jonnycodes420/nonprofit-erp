title: Steward speaks plain American English
product: crm
---
Every screen, email and PDF now uses American spelling, one date style ("Oct 8, 2026") and one name for each action, so logging a conversation is always "Log a call". Report names say what they mean ("Gave last year, not this year"), the import screens name the system you're moving from, and thank-you drafts open with the donor's name, close with yours, and say where the gift goes ("where it's needed most" for your general fund, or a fund's public name). Imports can be undone, and the Imports list now shows only your files, with a clear button to bring in another.
