title: Atlas shows its work as it writes, and Donors opens faster
product: crm
---
When you ask Atlas for a draft, it now tells you what it is doing ("Writing the draft to Marlowe…") and shows the first words of the email as they are written, instead of a spinner until the end. The Donors list, a person's profile, the people counts and the retention report now read only what they need, so they stay quick when your whole team is busy.
