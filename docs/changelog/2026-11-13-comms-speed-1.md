title: Choosing people for an email is quick, and leaving a page stops its work
product: crm
---
When you pick the people for an email by hand, you now search by name or email and see the first fifty right away, instead of waiting for your whole list to load. Pages that show everyone at once, like Re-engage and Lost and Found, load several times faster. If you leave one before it has finished, Steward stops loading it, so the next page you open isn't slowed down.
