title: A real email builder for every email
product: crm
---
Every email now opens in a builder: add text, images, buttons, signatures and more from the left, click any part of the email to change its words, size, spacing, border or background on the right, and undo anything back to your last save. Personal fields have their own tab that shows what each one says for the person you are previewing, and a field you pick always lands in your email, with a note saying where.
