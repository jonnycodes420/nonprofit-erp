title: Big spreadsheets come in whole, and a big undo shows its progress
product: crm
---
A one-sheet spreadsheet of 25,000 people now comes in whole: every row is read, and a file too big to read is turned away before anything is written, with its row count. The receipt says what each count is, sheet by sheet ("rows in the People sheet", "gift rows written", "set aside"), and the sheets add up to the file. Undoing a big import now runs in the background with a progress bar, so it finishes or says why it stopped. A file with thousands of gifts now imports in seconds rather than an hour.
