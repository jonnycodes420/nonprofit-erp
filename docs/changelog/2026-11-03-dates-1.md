title: Add many donors to a journey at once, journeys on a date every year, yearly tasks, and a note you see first
product: crm
---
Check several donors in your list, or select everyone matching a filter, and add them all to one journey: you see who will start and who won't, and why, before anything starts, and one Undo takes the whole batch back for 30 days. A journey can now start on the same date every year for the people you pick, such as a year-end impact report every December 1, and a task can repeat every year, including on someone's birthday. Each person also has a short "About them" note at the top of their About box, which you can fill in from a spreadsheet when you import.
