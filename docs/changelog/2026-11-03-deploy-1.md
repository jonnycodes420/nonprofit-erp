date: 2026-11-03
title: Updates to Steward no longer interrupt your work
product: platform
---
When we release a new version of Steward, the old one now finishes what you asked of it before it stops, and the new one waits until it is fully ready before it answers you. A question you ask in the moment of an update gets its answer instead of an error. Background work that was interrupted by an update picks up again on its own.
