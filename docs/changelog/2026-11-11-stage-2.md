title: The test copy of Steward says it's the test copy
product: platform
---
Steward's staging copy, where every change is checked before it reaches you, now carries a strip at the top of every page that reads "Staging · not what customers see", and its browser tabs start with "[Staging]". The copy you use never shows either. A nightly check also compares the two copies' settings, so a missing setting is caught before it can cause trouble.
