date: 2026-11-04
title: Recording a gift updates one person's scores, not everyone's
product: crm
---
When you record a gift, merge two records or link a spouse, Steward now updates the engagement and generosity scores of only the people that change touched, instead of rewriting every score in your organization. Opening a profile during an import shows that person's score right away without holding anything up, and big imports refresh everyone's scores once, at the end. Steward stays fast while an import is running.
