title: Help on every page, and a person when you need one
product: platform

Press the question mark at the top of any screen and the help article for that screen opens beside your work. Ask Steward answers how-to questions from the help centre, without ever seeing your donors, and when the help does not cover it you can ask a person, who replies within one business day. The whole help centre is at stewardapp.dev/help.
