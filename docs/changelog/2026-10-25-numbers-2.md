title: History from your old system is kept, and every number means one thing
product: crm
---
When you import donors with their giving totals from another system, Steward now keeps that history as its own figure, labeled with the file it came from, and a donor's lifetime is that history plus every gift in Steward since. Average gift and retention now mean the same thing on every screen, report and board pack, and "this year" follows the year your organisation set in Settings. "How Steward counts" in Help has the definitions.
