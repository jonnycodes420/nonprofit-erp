title: Steward is ready for many more organizations
product: crm
---
Steward's background work (mailbox and calendar reads, reminders, digests, connection syncs and the recent gifts an import brings) now runs from one shared queue, with each organization's sync as its own job. A slow mailbox or a big import at one organization no longer holds up anybody else's, and a job that keeps failing stops and is looked at rather than retried forever. Each organization also has a daily AI allowance, and Steward says plainly when it has been used, with everything else working as normal.
