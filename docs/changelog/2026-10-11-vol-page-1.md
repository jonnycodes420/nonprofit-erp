title: Volunteer pages that look as good as your giving pages
product: crm
---
Build a volunteer sign-up page in the same editor as your giving pages and emails, on the same brand and with the same photos. Drop in role cards, a shift picker that shows open spots and a waiting list, a sign-up form with your own questions, group sign-ups, and impact numbers that come straight from your records. Each sign-up lands on the person's record and in Volunteers, and the confirmation emails wait for you to send them.
