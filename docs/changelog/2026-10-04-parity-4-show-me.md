title: Show me, in plain words
product: crm
---
Ask Steward for a list the way you would say it, such as "donors who gave last year but not this year" or "monthly donors in Lexington", from Home or Reports. Steward shows the filters it used in words, how many people match and who they are, and one tap saves the list as a group that stays up to date or exports it. When a question needs a filter Steward does not have yet, it says so plainly instead of guessing.
