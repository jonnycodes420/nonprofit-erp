title: Atlas only tells you what it read
product: agent
---
Ask Atlas how much someone gave in a month, a quarter, a fiscal year or between two dates, and it answers for exactly that period, naming each gift by its date and amount, with their lifetime shown separately and labeled. Atlas now checks every dollar amount and date in its answer against the records it read, and if one doesn't match, it gives you the plain sentence from your records instead. It also answers who your executive director is, who signs your receipts and your EIN from your settings, and answers each part of a two-part question on its own.
