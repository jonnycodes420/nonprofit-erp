title: One next move and one ask per person, the same on Home and their profile
product: crm

Each person now has one next move and one suggested ask. Home, their profile's Next move, and the Giving tab's Next line and suggested ask all show the same one. A donor who has gone quiet is asked for what they gave last, and the ask names that gift. A step that comes from a household member's gift says it is the household's and names whose gift it was. Someone moved to the trash no longer drives anyone's next move.
Two gifts from one person on the same day now make one thank-you draft for the day's total, and marking it sent thanks both. On Home, a draft you have looked at reads "read", never "opened".
