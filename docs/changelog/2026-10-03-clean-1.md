Donors now has a Data health page that lists possible duplicates, addresses to tidy, people who moved, emails that need a look and people you have no way to reach, each with its reason and one button to fix it. Merging two records moves every gift, note, task and connection to the one you keep, shows that the lifetime giving adds up to the cent, and can be undone for 30 days. Steward also prepares a change-of-address file for any USPS-licensed NCOA provider and reads their results back so you can approve each move.
