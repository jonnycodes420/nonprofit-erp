date: 2026-11-04
title: Every total keeps up when you change a gift
product: crm
---
When you edit or delete a gift, the donor's profile, its right-hand panel, the thank-you waiting for that gift, your fund balances and your fundraising totals all change at once, with no reload, and a thank-you not yet sent is rewritten for the new amount. A part-paid pledge now shows the same amount still to come, due and overdue everywhere, and Home's annual goal counts the gifts to the goals under it, as Fundraising does. The "some year but not this year" report now shows what each person gave in the last year they gave, never $0, and a gift you soft-credit to someone says so on the giver's gift.
