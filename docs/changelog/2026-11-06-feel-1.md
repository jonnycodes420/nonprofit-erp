title: Home holds still, and every number agrees with itself
product: crm
---
Home no longer jumps while your moves load, and a gift with no fund now reads Unrestricted (no fund) everywhere. A volunteer shift shows the same count on the Calendar and on the schedule, fund totals and the donor export follow every gift you record, and gift types and event summaries read in plain words. Skipping the next step in a note no longer closes a different follow-up, and a $0 gift no longer offers a receipt.
