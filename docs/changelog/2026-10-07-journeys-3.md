title: Journeys start on the moments that matter
product: crm
---
A journey can now start when a membership is about to end or has lapsed, when a monthly gift fails, is cancelled or its card is about to expire, when a volunteer reaches 10, 25, 50 or 100 hours, when someone comes to their first event, when a grant is awarded or when a fundraising page reaches its goal. Each comes as a ready-made journey that stays off until you turn it on, and every step is a draft or a task that waits for you. Someone can be in more than one journey at a time, and Steward spaces their steps so nobody hears from you twice in one week.
