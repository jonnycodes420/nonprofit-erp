date: 2026-11-07
title: Events, volunteers and tasks stay current, and nothing is left behind
product: crm
---
Marking a guest as attended, no-show or back to registered now changes what their profile says about the event, and removing a guest or an event takes those entries away too. Adding a guest past an event's capacity, or signing a volunteer up for a shift that overlaps another, now asks you first and names the clash, and a logged shift can be edited. Event figures, task counts after a snooze, volunteer hours, the Files tab and the merge and household searches now show the latest answer the moment you act, with no reload.
