title: Event attendance and tasks stay current, and nothing is left behind
product: crm
---
Marking a guest as attended, no-show or back to registered now changes what their profile says about the event, and removing a guest or an event takes those entries away too. Event figures on Fundraising, task counts after a snooze, and the volunteer panel's hours update the moment you make a change, with no reload.
