title: Campaign pages, GivingTuesday and year-end
product: giving
---
Every campaign can now have a public page of its own: your story, your photos, a live goal bar that says exactly what it counts, the donation form, a countdown to the closing date and a list of recent gifts that names only the donors who chose to be named. Start one from the GivingTuesday or Year-end template and you get the campaign, the page and a plan of dated reminders for the weeks before and after, from drafting the appeal to thanking everyone within 48 hours. Every step is a task on somebody's list here; Steward sends none of them. If a supporter or a board member has promised to match gifts, enter it once and the page shows how much of the match is still unclaimed.
