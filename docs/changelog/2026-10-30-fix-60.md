title: A pledge counts as giving, and every number is the right kind
product: crm
---
Somebody who pledged recently, or whose pledge is on schedule, no longer reads Lapsed; if a payment is past due they read Pledge payment overdue instead. Every pledge, from any import or screen, now has its schedule of payments, and year-over-year reports compare the same days of each year and say which. Budgets follow your fiscal year, labels are in sentence case across the app, and the numbers on the Events list open the rows behind them.
