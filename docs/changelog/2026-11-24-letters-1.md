Upload your thank-you or tax letter as a Word file and Steward keeps your letterhead, logo and layout, then fills in each person's name, gift and deductible amount. Pick an event or a campaign and a date range, see who gets a letter and who is left out and why, and download one PDF or an editable Word file. Add a picture of your signature and it prints above your name in its own colour, and campaigns made by accident from a gift now show as drafts you can finish, merge or delete.
