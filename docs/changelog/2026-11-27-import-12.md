title: Exports from Salesforce, Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, Neon and Little Green Light come in whole
product: crm
---
Drop both files from your old system at once, the people file and the gifts file, and Steward reads them as one import, joined on the old system's own ID. Refunds now come off the gift they refunded, soft credits land as recognition rather than money, and tributes, anonymous gifts and pledge payments keep what your old system said about them. Gifts from businesses and foundations in a Salesforce export are no longer set aside.
