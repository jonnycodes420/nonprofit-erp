title: One answer to "are they on time?"
product: crm
---
A donor's profile no longer says "On track" beside a Lapsing tag. Steward now works out when each person's next gift is due in one place, and the tag, the sentence under it, the Directory, Drift and Re-engage all read that one answer. When someone is past their usual time, the sentence says by how much ("Now 6 weeks past their usual time"), and a gap is said in whole months, so it never reads "9 months" for someone who is not yet lapsing.
