date: 2026-10-23
title: Mail that can't be delivered stops trying, and each kind of mail has its own switch
product: platform
---
When an email can't be delivered, for example to an address that bounced, Steward now marks it as failed with the reason and stops trying, and you can see it in the send log. Staff mail, donor mail and billing mail can each be turned on or off for your organization, and sign-in mail always goes. Undoing a merge now brings back soft credits sized to the gift as it stands today.
