title: Search shows people as you type, and saved views keep their order
product: crm
---
Search, from the top bar or with Cmd+K, now shows matching people while you are still typing, even on a slow connection. A saved view on the Donors list now comes back in the order you saved it. A logo saved in Settings, Branding now shows on your giving page, and an appeal sent to someone who has never given no longer thanks them for giving before.
