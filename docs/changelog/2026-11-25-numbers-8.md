title: Figures that agree: fund cards, gives through, LYBUNT and thank-yous
product: crm
---
A fund's card and the window it opens now show the same balance and the same waiting gifts, and each line says what it counts. Choosing a fund in Transactions shows every year, so its lines add up to its balance. A person whose gifts come through an organization now has a Gives through section with the amount, and the organization lists the same person at the same amount.
The LYBUNT count on Fundraising opens people when it says people and households when it says households. Home's "Gifts not yet thanked" says the day it counts from, and the report builder's Thanked filter uses the same rule. Previewing a thank-you for someone who has never given now says so and offers your newest donor, instead of thanking them for a gift they never made.
