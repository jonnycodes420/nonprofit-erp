title: Starter emails that thank the right gift, cancelled plans you can find, and journeys that truly pause
product: crm
---
The starter emails in New campaign now thank a donor for their last real gift, never one that was refunded. A cancelled offline monthly plan stays on Recurring giving under Canceled with the day it was cancelled, and pausing a journey now holds everyone in it at their current step until you turn it back on. Re-engage also says why its count changed since the day before, naming who crossed a year without a gift and who gave again.
