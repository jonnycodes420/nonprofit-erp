title: Big imports finish, and a donor's labels agree everywhere
product: crm
---
A workbook with 25,000 people in it now imports in one go in under two minutes, with one progress bar and one Undo. A donor marked Major, Lapsing or Lapsed reads the same on their profile, in your Donors filters, in groups and on Home, and the ask Home suggests for them is the same one their profile suggests. When two gifts arrive on the same day from one file, the last gift shown is always the same one, and gifts recorded as soft or matching credit no longer count toward a pledge being paid.
