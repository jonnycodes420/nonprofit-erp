title: Checks entered in QuickBooks now count in Steward
product: crm
---
If your bookkeeper enters checks, cash and bank transfers in QuickBooks, Steward can now read them in, so those donors no longer look like they never gave. You choose which income accounts are donations, review every gift before it is saved, and Steward skips anything it already has from Stripe, PayPal, a giving page or its own sync. It only reads QuickBooks, never writes to it, and a whole read undoes in one step.
