title: Finance and Reports say plainly what they show
product: crm
---
Finance now puts your giving and your ledger under their own headings, and when nothing has moved through Steward yet it says so in one card instead of a row of zeros. Top donors shows the dollars your top donors gave, the bookkeeper's report shows the real processing fee and net, and Reports gives your all-time total, the same figure Atlas gives. Every fiscal year is named one way, as FY 2026–27, and blank figures, empty reports and Grants with no grants now say what to do next.
