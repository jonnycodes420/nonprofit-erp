title: Tell Steward what happened, and the paperwork is done
product: agent
---
After a meeting, press Tell Steward on Home, on a donor's page or in the menu, and talk for thirty seconds or type a few lines. Steward logs the conversation, puts the follow-up on the Thread with its date, notes what they are interested in and drafts the email, and every one of those can be undone with one button. Nothing is sent, and amounts, contact changes and anything personal wait for your yes.
