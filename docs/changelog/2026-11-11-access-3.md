title: Home for someone who sees only their own people, made for them
product: crm
---
Staff who see only the people assigned to them, such as a Campaign cabinet member, now open Home to the date, their greeting and the Thread straight under the Atlas box, with no empty space where other people's panels used to sit. The right side is now "Your portfolio": how many people are theirs, what those people have given this fiscal year, how many have a next step overdue, their top five givers, and the next three touches on their Top 100. When their export is turned off, the Donors list no longer shows Export CSV.
