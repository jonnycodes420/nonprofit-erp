title: You choose who gets automatic emails
product: crm
---
Every person now has an Automatic emails switch, and it starts off. Steward only sends receipts, thank-yous, reminders and other automatic emails to people you have turned it on for, one at a time on their profile or many at once from the Donors list. When Steward holds an email because the switch is off, the profile says so, with a Send button that sends that one message after you confirm.
