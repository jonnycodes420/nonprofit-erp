title: Logging a touch writes one follow-up, and every task says if it's done
product: crm
---
Logging a call that keeps the same next step now moves that step to its new day instead of leaving a finished copy beside it, Mark done on a profile finishes the step in one click, and leaving Next step empty writes nothing. On a person's Tasks tab an open task is an empty box with its due day and a finished one shows a check and the day it was done. The Re-engage count follows your filters ("0 of 331"), Giving rhythm agrees with the Current tag when someone gives through a household or a foundation, the merge screen names teammates, each Home move says why it suggests a note or a call, and Plan a follow-up is always on the profile, even with a step open.
