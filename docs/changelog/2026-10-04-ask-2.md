title: Ask anything about your own file
product: crm
---
Type a question in plain words, such as "How much did we raise in September?", "This year vs last year by fund" or "Who are my top 20 donors this year?", and Steward answers in one sentence where every number opens the gifts behind it. Follow up with "and last year?", "only monthly donors" or "who are they?", and pin an answer to Home or add it to a dashboard. When Steward can't answer something, it says so plainly instead of guessing.
