title: The More menu on a donor's profile fits on your screen
product: crm
---
On a smaller laptop, the More menu on a donor's profile used to run past the bottom of the screen, so Merge, Edit, Export and Delete were out of reach. It's now grouped into Reach out, Plan, Know them and Their record, sits in two columns on a wide screen, and scrolls inside itself when it needs to. Linking someone to the business they give through is easier to find too: "Link to a person or business" is in the menu, and About always offers "+ Link another".
