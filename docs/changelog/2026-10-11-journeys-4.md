title: Journeys that start from everything a donor does
product: crm
---
A journey can now start when someone makes or finishes a pledge, falls behind on one, gives through a giving page for the first time, starts their own fundraising page, wins or donates an auction item, registers for an event, comes, or doesn't come, finishes a call with an outcome you pick, or opens an appeal and doesn't give. Each one shows who would have entered it in the last 90 days before you turn it on. Event guests are also a list you can use anywhere: in a group, a journey or a send, including the people who registered but didn't come.
