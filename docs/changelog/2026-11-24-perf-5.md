title: Home downloads less, and What's new loads when you open it
product: platform
---
The list of everything we have shipped no longer downloads in the background a few seconds after Home opens. It loads when you open What's new, and the dot that tells you something is new still shows as before. That is about 58 KB less to download each time Steward opens, which matters most on a phone.
