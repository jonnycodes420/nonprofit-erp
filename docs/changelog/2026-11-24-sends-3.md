title: Steward does only what you ask: Atlas lists, pledge reminders, test emails and undo
product: crm
---
Opening a list of people from Atlas now shows them in People without making a group; Save as group is its own button. Pledge reminders go only when an installment is actually past due, "Send me a test" works even while your organization's email is switched off (it only ever goes to you), and photos in your emails are sent as JPEG so every inbox can show them.
Marking a journey step done from a person's record can now be undone from Recent changes, and undoing an import asks before it voids any receipts. A phone number marked wrong now says so under the number.
