title: Run your week from Tasks
product: crm
---
Tasks now opens on Today, with Upcoming, Overdue, Later, No date and Done beside it, and each one shows its count. Home's "Due today" is the same number. Type a task the way you would say it, like "Call Bill Harmon Friday 2pm about the gala", and Steward fills in the person, the day and the time for you to check before it saves. Each task shows who or what it is about. When you finish a call or a meeting, Steward asks how it went and what comes next. Tasks can repeat every week, month or quarter, and you can snooze one with a reason or move, reassign or finish several at once, with Undo.
