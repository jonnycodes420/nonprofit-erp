Home now opens with your greeting, the date and one sentence on how giving compares with the same point last year. Under it, Your moves shows one person at a time, grouped as Keep, Grow, Major and Planned, with what is happening, why it matters, what to say and the ask; one button makes the task, and Not now or Doesn't fit moves you to the next. In Review you can now mark a row as partly a gift, so only the value someone received leaves your giving totals and their receipt says so.
