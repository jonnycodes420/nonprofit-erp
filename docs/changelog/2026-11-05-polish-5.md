title: Every sentence reads the way a person would write it
product: crm
---
Words no longer run together when a page is read aloud, copied or translated, so "worth $500" and "Emails sent to Marlowe" read as written. Totals inside sentences are whole dollars, an amount you type starts as 10,000.00, and every date on a screen is your organization's own day, so an undo at 8 PM is dated that evening. A handful of rough lines are fixed too: the next move on a profile now says what to do ("Visit Marlowe between Nov 5 and Dec 5"), the Tax receipts badge says what is actually being sent, and the Grants page logs funder email from a small Log emails link.
