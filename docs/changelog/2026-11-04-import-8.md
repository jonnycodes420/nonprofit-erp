date: 2026-11-04
title: Imports get email choices, gift totals and Undo right
product: crm
---
When you import a spreadsheet, Steward now takes "do not email" and similar choices only from a column that names them, and shows each choice with the column it came from. A note that sounds like a preference is listed for you to check and changes nothing. A lifetime or last-gift amount on your people sheet is checked against your gift sheet instead of being counted as a second gift, and the preview tells you how many people are already in Steward. If you undo an import, Steward names anyone it kept because they have activity since the import, and lets you remove them too.
