date: 2026-10-31
title: Soft credits stay out of your giving totals
product: crm
---
A soft credit or a matching gift credit thanks someone for a gift, but it was never money you received. Your board view, the pace line on Home and the annual report now leave those credits out of giving this year, and someone whose only recent activity is a credit now shows as lapsed, not current. Steward also now checks every one of these numbers against an answer key built straight from your gift rows.
