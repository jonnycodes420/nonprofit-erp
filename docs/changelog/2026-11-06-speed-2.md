date: 2026-11-06
title: Merging two records takes a moment, not ten seconds
product: crm
---
When you merge two records for the same person, Steward now moves everything in a few steps instead of checking each kind of record one at a time. A typical merge finishes in well under a second, and the side-by-side screen before it opens faster too. Nothing about what a merge keeps, moves or lets you undo has changed.
