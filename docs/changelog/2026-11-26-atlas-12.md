title: Atlas gives one number for one question, asks which person you meant, and logs the call you tell it about
product: agent
---
Ask Atlas about lapsed, LYBUNT or SYBUNT donors in any wording and it gives the same count Reports shows, with the sentence that defines it; say "calendar year" and it counts calendar years instead. A name that matches two people now asks which one, showing what each has given, and adds them together only when you press Both.
"How much is left on the goal?" and "Are we on pace?" answer with Home's goal line, and a follow-up like "and the Gala?" stays on that campaign. Tell Atlas about a call ("she said she'll give in January, remind me Jan 5") and it proposes the call note and a dated follow-up for you to save. A brief now opens with a few plain sentences, and says when no conversation has been logged.
