title: Add a street address by hand
product: crm
---
You can now type a donor's street address and apartment or suite when you add or edit them, and the profile shows the full mailing address as soon as you save. Every field on the edit form now has a label above it. When you merge two records, each field starts on the record you are keeping, and the button names the record you will end up with.
