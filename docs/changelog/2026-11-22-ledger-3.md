title: Fund balances post with their deposits, and each fund shows both of its numbers
product: crm
---
Finance now has a Post to the books step: gifts recorded in Steward that are not on the books yet are listed by the deposit they came in with, with each deposit's count and total, and you choose which to post. Each gift posts to its own fund, and Undo takes exactly that posting back off. Every fund card now shows what the fund received this fiscal year from gifts (the same figure as Reports' Gifts by fund) above its balance on the books, and any gifts still waiting to post, so a fund never reads $0 without saying why.
