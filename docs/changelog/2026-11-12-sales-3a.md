title: Your price and start date, in writing before you sign
product: platform
---
When you agree a plan with Steward, you now get a private page with your organization's name, the price, the day billing starts and what's included, and you sign it there. You can add a card on the same page and nothing is charged until the day billing starts. A copy of what you signed is kept in Settings, Billing.
