title: Atlas understands lapsed questions however you ask, and briefs start with what's open
product: agent
---
Ask Atlas about lapsed donors in your own words ("who's lapsed?", "how many people stopped giving?", "LYBUNT") and it gives the same count as the rest of Steward, with the list's name and what it means; when a question could mean two lists, you get both. "Who should I call to win back lapsed donors?" now lists only lapsed donors, biggest lifetime givers first, and top donors show what each person gave this year. "Brief me" now starts with open asks, gifts not yet thanked and your next meeting.
