product: crm

Every page now scrolls as one page, the donor profile and the event page included. A donor's profile has a new layout: their name, two tags and four numbers at the top, the next move and everything that happened by month below, and a green panel on the right with the next step, any open pledge, their household, how to reach them and how engaged they are.
"This year" on a profile is your fiscal year everywhere, labelled with its dates, so a sentence and a number never disagree about which year they mean.
