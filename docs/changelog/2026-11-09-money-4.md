title: Record a refund, see every recurring plan, and imports that add up in dollars
product: crm
---
Any gift now has Record a refund: say how much, when and why, and the gift stays on file while totals, the receipt and, for a card gift, Stripe follow along after you confirm the amount. Recurring giving now lists the monthly plans your staff record by hand beside card plans, and counts them in monthly recurring revenue. An awarded grant shows what has come in against its award, its application dates step aside once you have won it, and every import receipt now adds up in dollars as well as rows.
