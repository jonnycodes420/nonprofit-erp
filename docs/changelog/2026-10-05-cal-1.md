title: One calendar for everything
product: crm
---
A new Calendar in the sidebar puts your meetings, next steps, volunteer shifts, events, journey steps, campaign sends and pledge payments in one week view, with Day, Month and Agenda views and a toggle for each kind. Drag a block to move it or stretch it to change its length, and Undo puts it back; click an empty slot to add a meeting, a call, a next step, a shift or an event. The shift form is now one clean form with all-day, weekly repeat and notes, from the calendar and from Volunteers alike.
