title: Every name is a link
product: crm
---
When Steward names a person, a gift, a task, a meeting, a journey, an event, a campaign or a group, on the Thread, in what Tell Steward did for you, or in the emails Steward sends your team, it is now a link straight to it. Links in an email take you through sign-in and land you right there. A link to something you can't see says so and shows nothing of it.
