date: 2026-10-08
title: One bad address no longer stops Mailchimp
product: crm
---
If Mailchimp turns down an address, Steward now skips it, lists it on the card with Mailchimp's reason, and carries on with everyone else. The card shows when Steward last read Mailchimp and how many records it updated, and an unsubscribe in Mailchimp stops Steward's own email to that person too. Steward adds people to your Mailchimp audience only if you turn that on.
