title: A refund counted the same way everywhere, and a giving page preview you can trust
product: crm
---
A gift refunded in full is no longer counted as a gift anywhere, and a part refund counts once at what was kept, so the Giving summary, Fundraising, Gifts by fund and a donor's profile all give the same number. The giving page editor's preview now shows the suggested amounts you typed, says plainly when the live page suggests others, and its cover the fee box works, so $500 a month becomes $515.25. Archived events drop out of the Events count and every event list, Fundraising says in one line when campaigns and Raised cover different dates, and an admin can open Tasks for one person again.
