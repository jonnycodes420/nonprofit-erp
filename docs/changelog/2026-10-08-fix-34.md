title: One meeting, one row, and tasks that finish the job
product: crm

A booked meeting now shows once on the Calendar, with its prep, reschedule and done buttons inside the card, and Home's Thread shows one row per donor with the rest of their steps a tap away. A Thank-you task opens the thank-you note for that gift, and sending it marks the gift thanked and closes the task; an Email task opens a draft from your templates. QuickBooks waits until every fund has an account before it syncs anything, and PayPal lets you choose which fund its gifts go to.
