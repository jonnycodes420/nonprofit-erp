title: Thank-you drafts state the true lifetime, and every count agrees
product: crm
---
A milestone thank-you now states the donor's lifetime giving as it stands when the draft is made, and Send stops any draft whose total has gone out of date, naming the figure. Fundraising, the Giving summary, LYBUNT and SYBUNT, their group pages and the Groups starters now count the same people, and a person whose only gift this year is $0 counts the same way everywhere. An erased person no longer appears in any count or roster while their gifts stay in your totals, a gift's receipt can be voided from the profile with a note, and a refused delete now says why.
