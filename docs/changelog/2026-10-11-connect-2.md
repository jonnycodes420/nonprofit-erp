product: crm

Connect Givebutter, Square or Zeffy and every gift lands on the right person with its gross, fee and net, on the fund you choose, or waiting for you to choose. A gift already in from your giving page or an earlier export is never counted twice, and a row Steward cannot use is listed with the reason instead of stopping the check. Xero now receives one bank transaction per gift when you press Sync, and Constant Contact brings back who opened, clicked and asked to stop, so their wishes are honoured here too.
