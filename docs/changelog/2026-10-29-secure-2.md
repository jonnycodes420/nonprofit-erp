title: Two-factor your way, idle sign-out, and quieter logs
product: security
---
Admins can now sign everyone out after a set time with no activity, and Steward emails you when your account signs in from a browser it hasn't seen. Every export is recorded with who took it and how many rows, and a staff export of more than 1,000 people needs an admin. Email addresses and phone numbers are checked as you type them (with a "did you mean gmail.com?"), a campaign that only partly went out now says exactly how many went and why the rest didn't, and our logs and error reports no longer carry your donors' details.
