date: 2026-10-07
title: A booked meeting changes the whole record
product: crm
---
Book a visit in Steward, or put one on your Outlook or Google calendar, and the person's record catches up: the meeting becomes their next step, a prep task lands the business day before, a "How did it go?" task lands on the day, and the timeline and engagement score both show it. Move or cancel it on either side and all of that follows. Emails you send from Outlook are now logged too, and Settings shows exactly when Steward last read your mail and calendar, with a Read now button and a banner if it ever stops.
