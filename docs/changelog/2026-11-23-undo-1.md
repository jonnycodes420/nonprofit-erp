title: Undo a refund, and an Undo that takes back everything a change did
product: crm
---
A refund can now be undone for 30 days from the gift's row or from Recent changes: you say why, the refund comes off, and the receipt is put back as it was unless a newer receipt already went to the donor. Ticking a step done, Home's Done and logging a touch now show in Recent changes, and their Undo also puts back the journey the step moved on. Log a touch now says which next step it will close and lets you keep it open, and registering someone you had invited makes them registered with the places they bought instead of adding to the invite.
