title: A messy migration comes in the way your old system had it
product: crm
---
When a file sends the same gift twice, or sends an old gift again with a new amount, Steward brings it in once and asks before changing anything, with "Update to the file's amount" as your choice. Refunds now come off the gift they refunded in any spreadsheet, dates Steward can't read are set aside and listed instead of landing with no date, and a total line or a row with only an email is never made into a person. A workbook's Pledges sheet writes its pledges, and every receipt, Undo and the Imports list now give the same undo date.
