title: Drafts land on the person, and all your templates in one place
product: crm
---
When you ask the Agent to thank everyone who gave this month, each draft now shows up on that person's record as their next step, on the Thread and on their timeline, and you can review them all in a row instead of one at a time. Approving a thank-you marks the gift as thanked, and Undo puts it back. Communications has one Templates tab now, in four sections, and each card says whether it is a printed letter or an email.
