Steward is one plan with everything included, for your whole team, month to month. The price follows your records, the people and organizations with at least one gift, and signup shows your price and the one-time setup fee before you pay. Settings, Billing now shows your record count and how it is counted, your price, your next charge and whether the setup fee was paid, and a change of band never happens without your yes.
