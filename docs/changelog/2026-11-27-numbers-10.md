A fund's card, its dialog and the Transactions list now give one balance and say whether card fees donors covered are in it. A thank-you draft names each gift it thanks for, an event's First-time givers counts only people whose first gift came through that event, and a program officer shows the foundation's gifts as Gives through. Deleting a gift cancels its thank-you call with a line on the timeline that Undo takes back, a pledge that can't be deleted says so before you confirm, and the import summary adds up to the file, scheduled gifts included.
