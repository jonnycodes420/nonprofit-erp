title: Search, merge and the trash find the right thing
product: crm
---
Searching for a name now puts the exact match first, and two people who share a name are marked Donor, Contact or Volunteer, so Enter opens the one you meant. A merge you start from a record keeps that record, Re-engage keeps the Directory's filters, and the Notes tab says what someone has given today with older notes dated. The trash has its own place in Settings, with its count, and a link from the Donors list.
