title: Your donor list explains itself, and Data health shows only what needs you
product: crm
---
Hover the Stage, Engagement or Giving strength heading on your donor list to read what each one means, and hover a Giving strength number to see what it is made of. Re-engage now says plainly how long it has been since someone's last gift, and Data health shows only the checks that found something, with long lists of possible duplicates and families shown fifty at a time. Importing a file reads more naturally too, from "When was Finding Joy?" to counting an organization as one of the people it brings in.
