date: 2026-11-23
title: Editing a gift has every field recording one does
product: crm
---
Editing a gift now opens the same form you record it with, so you can fix its campaign, appeal, fundraiser page, soft credit or tribute after the fact, and an amount like $123.45 always shows as $123.45. Campaign, appeal, soft credit and tribute boxes show matches as you type and save only what you pick, and a name with no match asks whether to create it instead of being quietly dropped. Bank transfer (ACH) is a payment method everywhere, a gift can be marked Anonymous for public lists while staying on the donor's record, Cancel now clears a gift you started, and the giving page preview shows the amounts and frequencies you are editing before you publish.
