title: Home asks for the right amount, in words a donor can read
product: crm
---
A year-end ask now takes off what someone already gave this fiscal year and says so, and a donor who already gave as much is not asked again. Notes to foundations and businesses open "Dear friends at" the organisation, or its named contact, and Home's Ask box says plainly when you name a fund you don't have instead of answering with all your giving. If nobody owns a donor yet, Home offers to make your 25 largest givers yours in one click, with Undo.
