title: Bring your old reports with you
product: crm
---
Import the reports from the system you used before, from Reports or the Import menu: CSV, Excel and PDF files, or a zip of them. Steward says what each report is and where it came from, keeps every file under Past reports and in search, and can bring a report's totals in as history, labelled "from your old system" and never mixed into your own gifts. Your history no longer starts the day you switched, and the whole import undoes in one step.
