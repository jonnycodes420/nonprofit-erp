title: Bring your old reports with you
product: crm
---
Import old reports from Reports or the Import menu: CSV, Excel and PDF files, or a zip of them, from Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, Salesforce, Neon, Little Green Light, Zeffy, QuickBooks or a spreadsheet. Steward says what each report is and where it came from, keeps every file under Past reports and in search, and can pull a report's totals in as history, labelled "from your old system" and never mixed into your own gifts. The whole import undoes in one step.
