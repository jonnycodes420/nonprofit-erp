date: 2026-11-15
title: Refunds, split gifts, households and deleted gifts read true everywhere
product: crm
---
A gift refunded in full no longer counts anywhere on a profile, and the Raised list counts gifts, with each refund shown under the gift it came from. Transactions now shows a split gift as one line per fund, and a household that shares an address can get one year-end statement with everyone's gifts and the combined total. Deleting a gift now also cancels the thank-you call its welcome journey had scheduled.
