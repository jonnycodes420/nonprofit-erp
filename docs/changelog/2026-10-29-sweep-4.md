title: Events and pledges that say exactly what happened
product: crm
---
An event's page leaves out anyone you have deleted, counts each first-time giver once, and on a past event the seating chart speaks only of the people who came. Follow-up tasks for your guests now show how many they will make before anything is made, never make a second task for someone who already has one, and can be undone; a pledge reminder can be undone too. Every pledge now has its payment schedule, your public event page says "Not open yet" until payments are connected, and asking how an event did gives what came in and what was pledged there.
