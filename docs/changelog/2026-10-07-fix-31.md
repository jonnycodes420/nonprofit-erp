date: 2026-10-07
title: Grant deadlines and next steps are tasks, and stay in step everywhere
product: crm
---
Every grant deadline and every next step now shows in Tasks, on the Thread and on the Calendar as one thing: tick it, move it or undo it in any of them and the others follow. Proposals, LOIs and reports also get a "Start the proposal" task two weeks ahead, and done deadlines stay on the Calendar, crossed out, so you can see what was handed in. On Volunteers, the first number is now everyone on your roster, so someone you added today counts straight away.
