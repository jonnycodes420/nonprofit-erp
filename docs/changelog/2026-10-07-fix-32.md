title: Sign up and start straight away
product: crm
---
After you pay in Checkout, Steward signs you in and opens your start page in the same browser, instead of asking for a password you never set. If your account takes a moment to set up, the page says so and waits, and if it takes too long it offers a sign-in link by email. Exploring the demo now takes you straight to Home, and sample donors are no longer counted as your import.
