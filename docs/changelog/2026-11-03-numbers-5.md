title: Big workbooks import in one step, and every total counts money the same way
product: crm
---
Import a spreadsheet now takes a workbook with tens of thousands of people and their gifts and reads every sheet, with one progress bar and one Undo. Every total in Reports, Finance, grants and recurring gifts now leaves soft and matching credits out and takes refunds off, so they agree with each other and with each donor's profile. Home's suggested asks now match the profile's: a donor whose giving is rising is asked at their own level until Steward knows they have room to give more, and the card says so.
