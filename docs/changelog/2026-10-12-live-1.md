title: Every import now tells you what it found
product: crm
---
When you import a file, or Steward reads PayPal, a giving page source or QuickBooks, it now writes a short readout: who came back after a year away, who gave their biggest gift ever, who stopped a monthly gift and who is now late for their own rhythm, each line opening the people behind it and offering one next step. Gifts from the last 30 days in a CSV now get a thank-you draft and can start a journey, just like the same gift from Stripe, and the preview tells you how many before you save. Steward also remembers how you mapped a file, so next month's export with the same columns maps itself, and every readout is kept under Agent, in What changed.
