date: 2026-11-05
title: Buttons that finish what they start
product: crm
---
Adding people to a journey from the Donors list now shows every journey and, for the people you checked, who can start it and why not. Calendar has an Add meeting button that saves on Steward's calendar when no calendar is connected, and you can drag a task to another day. Year-end statements and receipts now show what was made before you download or email them, Mark done on Home marks a step done, clicking a donor's initials opens the photo picker, and yearly and birthday tasks keep coming back on the right day.
