title: Reports open faster, and stay current after a gift
product: crm
---
The Three-year and Year over year reports now open from a saved answer instead of adding up every gift each time, so they open in a fraction of a second even for large files. After you record a gift, SYBUNT, Annual, Three-year, Year over year and Giving summary update within a moment, so the next time you open them they are both quick and include the new gift.
