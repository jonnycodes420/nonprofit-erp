title: What follows a gift always happens, and never in silence
product: platform
---
When a gift arrives, Steward drafts its thank-you, starts the right journey and updates the donor's numbers. If one of those steps fails, Steward now tries it again on its own, and a step that keeps failing is flagged for our team instead of being quietly skipped. While a donor's numbers are being brought up to date after a change, their profile says "Updating…" instead of showing the old answer as current.
