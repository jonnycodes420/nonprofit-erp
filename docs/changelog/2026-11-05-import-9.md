title: Undoing an import puts everyone back, and imports say what they tidied
product: crm
---
If you bring people back from the trash while importing a file and then undo that import, those people now go back to the trash too, so your list is exactly as it was before. When a file's dates could be read two ways, like 3/4/25, the preview now asks you which you meant before anything is imported, and lists any dates written in a different format. It also tells you how many amounts it tidied, such as "$75.5" becoming $75.50, and shows each one.
