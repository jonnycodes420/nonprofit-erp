title: A shorter header on every person's record, so more of their story fits on screen
product: crm
---
The top of a person's record is less than half as tall. Their face, name and tags sit on one line with your buttons, and Lifetime, This fiscal year, Last gift and Last contact sit in one thin row under the name. The header scrolls away with the rest of the record, and the tabs stay at the top with the person's name beside them, so you can switch tabs without scrolling back up.
