date: 2026-11-27
title: Search finds people by surname, and the email builder works in Safari
product: crm
---
Search now finds a person by their full name, their surname alone, or their surname and first initial, and lists everyone who shares a surname. The email builder now works in Safari: clicking, dragging blocks and the plus between blocks all respond, and Undo takes back what you typed. Picking a meeting time keeps the dialog open, and Imports and Atlas now say how long you have to undo something.
