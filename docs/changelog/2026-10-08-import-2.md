title: Bring your notes and history from your old system
product: crm
---
Everything your team wrote about your donors can come with you now: notes, calls, meetings, visits, letters, open tasks and attached files, each on the right person's timeline with its original date and author. Steward shows you exactly who each row belongs to before anything is saved, asks you to confirm any "do not solicit" it finds in the notes, and undoes the whole import in one step. Until someone has any history on file, their record says "No history yet" instead of calling them cold.
