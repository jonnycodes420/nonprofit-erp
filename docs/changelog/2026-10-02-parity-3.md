product: volunteers

A person's record now carries their birthday, and a journey can start on it each year. Filters and groups by giving level open in a fraction of a second on a freshly imported file. Shifts now have roles, colours and venues, a calendar by day, week and month, and a roster you can drag people onto. Reminders and thank-yous are written for you, and you send them in one tap. Each person now has a full volunteer record: hours you can filter and download, qualifications, and notes the volunteer can see.
