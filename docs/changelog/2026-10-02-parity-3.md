Your volunteer page now takes applications you approve in one tap, and each person's record shows their hours, qualifications and notes. Shifts have roles, a calendar and a roster you can drag people onto, and reminders and thank-yous are written for you to send in one tap. The Volunteers screen filters by hours, shifts and giving, shows which volunteers have never given, and a person's birthday can now start a journey.
