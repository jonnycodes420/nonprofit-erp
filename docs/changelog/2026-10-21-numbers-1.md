title: Every number adds up, after every change
product: crm
---
A gift recorded from anywhere, including a voice note, now updates every figure at the top of a person's record at once: Lifetime, This year, Last gift, Average and Largest come from the same read. A campaign only counts gifts dated inside its own start and end, and choosing a campaign for a gift dated outside it says so instead of saving it. Steward also checks every number for every organization each night, and keeps only the counts.
