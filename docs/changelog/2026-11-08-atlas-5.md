title: Atlas gets dates right and gets out of your way
product: agent
---
Tell Atlas "remind me next Tuesday" on a Sunday and it now means the Tuesday two days away, in your organization's own time zone, and every date it suggests shows the weekday so you can check it before you save. The box opens empty, anything still waiting for you can be dismissed (including a suggestion you don't want and a "which one did you mean?" you'd rather skip), and the activity panel closes with Escape, its close button or a click outside. A note keeps everything you typed, a question Atlas can't answer gets a plain sentence, and common questions like "tell me about Juniper" now answer straight from your records without waiting on the AI.
