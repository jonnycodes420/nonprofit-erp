title: Checks land on the right donor
product: crm
---
When you snap a check, Steward now finds the person you already have, even when the check adds a middle initial, uses a nickname or names two people, and tells you why it chose them. Names printed in capitals read normally, a memo like "in memory of Walter" becomes the gift's tribute and is mentioned in the thank-you, and "pledge payment 2 of 4" goes on the right payment. If nobody matches, adding a new donor is a choice you make, never something Steward does on its own.
