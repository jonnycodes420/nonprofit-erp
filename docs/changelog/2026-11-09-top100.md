Major gifts now has a Top 100: your ranked list of the people who matter most, with Wealth Score, giving, owner, stage, the ask, the next step and the last contact on one row. Drag to reorder or type a rank, and give each person a plan of care: a goal, an ask and planned touches that land in your Tasks and lead your Home list when they are due. Export it or print it for board and coaching meetings.
