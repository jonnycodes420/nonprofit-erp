title: Screens catch up the moment you save
product: crm
---
After you save, delete or restore something, every screen that shows it now refreshes on its own, without reloading the page. The Trash count on the Donors list moves as soon as you move someone to the trash, and a person's Household card fills in when the household is made from Data health, another tab or a logged conversation.
