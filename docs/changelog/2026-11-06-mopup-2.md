title: Split gifts in your bookkeeper's files, and moving people to trash in bulk
product: crm
---
A gift split across funds now shows as one row per fund in the bookkeeper's gift file and on each deposit, so every fund is coded right and the deposit still adds up. On the Donors list you can tick people, or everyone a filter matches, and choose More, then Move to trash; anyone with gifts stays unless you say otherwise, and Undo brings the whole batch back. An organization whose file told us what it gave before Steward is no longer described as a first-time giver.
