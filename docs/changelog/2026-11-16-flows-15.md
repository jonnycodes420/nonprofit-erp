title: Appeals that thank only givers, and numbers that add up on screen
product: crm
---
A letter that thanks people for giving no longer goes to someone who has never given: they wait until you write a first-gift version (the heading too), or you tick them in yourself, and Preview as that person says plainly that it won't be sent to them. A campaign's weekly pace now matches the days left beside it, Atlas answers a question about money in dollars rather than as a list of people, and a fund has one name everywhere, with Restricted shown as its own badge. Billing's Records line now shows the sum that reaches the Donors tab, and the Stripe card says separately whether your giving pages take gifts and whether Steward reads your Stripe history.
