title: Everything in Communications opens
product: crm
---
Every name, number, bar and campaign in Communications now opens what it refers to: a person opens their profile, a count opens the people behind it, and a campaign opens the email as it went out, with everyone it went to. A donor's Emails tab lists every campaign, journey email and thank-you they were sent, and each one opens the way they received it. Audience now counts donors the same way a campaign sends to them, and the industry average with no source is gone.
