title: Imports, undo and deletes that cannot land in the wrong place
product: crm
---
Every change you make now goes to the organization the page was showing, and if you signed in to another organization in a different tab, Steward stops the change and asks you to reload. Imports read real files more carefully: businesses keep their names, each gift keeps its own event or category, and a file of contacts finds the people already in Steward instead of adding them twice. Pressing a button twice, losing your connection or closing the tab partway through an import, undo or delete now leaves everything either done or not done, and Steward tells you which.
