title: See what Steward can read in QuickBooks, and disconnect it yourself
product: crm
---
Once QuickBooks is connected, press Read from QuickBooks to see your company's name and its latest customers and payments. That way you know Steward is looking at the right books, and nothing is sent to QuickBooks while it reads. You can now disconnect QuickBooks from the same panel, and everything already recorded stays where it is.
