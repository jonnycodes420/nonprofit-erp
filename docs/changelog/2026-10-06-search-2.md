title: Search finds every gift, note and meeting
product: crm
---
The search box now finds gifts, monthly plans, pledges, journeys, meetings, emails, notes and imports, and each result opens on its own row. Type "$500" for gifts of that amount, "Oct 3" for that day's gifts and meetings, or a name followed by "gift" to jump straight to that person's giving. Results come in groups of three, with "See all" when there are more.
