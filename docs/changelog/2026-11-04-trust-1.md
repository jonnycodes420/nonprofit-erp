date: 2026-11-04
title: A campaign reaches everyone your email settings say it should
product: crm
---
With Campaign emails on for Everyone, a campaign you send now reaches everyone with an email address who is not set to Never, has not unsubscribed and is not marked do not contact. The builder's line says exactly how many will get it and why the rest won't. A person who has never given now reads "Thank you for being part of this." where a thank-you would have named their gift, and anything Steward sets up on its own starts turned off until someone in your organization turns it on.
