title: Recent windows in Ask, and greetings that sound like you
product: crm
---
Ask now understands any recent window, like "who came back in the last 90 days" or "how much did we raise over the past 6 weeks". A saved template that opens "Dear {{donor_name}}" greets people the way a thank-you does: their salutation, or their first names, both people on a joint record. Re-engage, Team and the map show today's totals when you open Donors, and the screen-reader step line on your giving form no longer shows on screen.
