title: Let Steward listen to the whole meeting
product: crm
---
Tell Steward now listens on your computer too, in Chrome, Edge and Safari, and has a new "Listen to this meeting" mode: pick who it's with, read a short brief, let everyone know you're taking notes, and press Start. Steward shows the words as they come, keeps your own notes beside them, and when you press Stop it writes a short summary and files the conversation, the dated next steps and a drafted follow-up email, with amounts and personal details waiting for your yes. No audio is ever kept, and the transcript can be deleted with one click. Checks made out to someone else now say so before you log them, and written cents like "17/100" are read correctly.
