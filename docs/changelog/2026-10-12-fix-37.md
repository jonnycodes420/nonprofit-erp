date: 2026-10-12
title: Imports ask before drafting thank-yous
product: crm
---
When a file has gifts from the last 30 days, Steward now asks whether they were already thanked before it drafts anything: yes, no, or only gifts above an amount you choose. Your first import and exports from systems like Salesforce or Bloomerang assume they were, so moving to Steward no longer fills your queue with notes for gifts you thanked last week. Big files also save right away now, with a progress line while Steward works through the recent gifts, and your first import's summary tells you what your history shows.
