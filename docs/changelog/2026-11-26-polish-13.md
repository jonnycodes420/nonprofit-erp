date: 2026-11-26
title: People counts agree everywhere, a deleted gift or grant takes what it made, and imports say how long you can undo
product: crm
---
Everyone, Donors, Contacts, Home and Re-engage now count the same people by one rule, so an archived person no longer shows up in some counts and not others. Deleting a grant also removes the funder organization it created, if nobody has used it since, and Undo brings both back. Deleting a gift removes its timeline entry along with its thank-you task and next step.
Imports now say "You can undo this import until Dec 3, 2026" on the confirm screen, on the receipt and in Settings, Imports, and an import past its window says the window closed. On Major Gifts, the Top 100 table scrolls inside its card with the name column held in place, and a refresh with Top 100 turned off opens Pipeline.
