title: Checks and gift letters, ready to confirm
product: crm
---
Snap a check with your phone, drop in a scan, or forward a pledge form or a donor-advised fund letter, and Steward reads it and puts a card in To confirm: who it is from, how much, and which fund, with the image right beside it. Nothing is recorded until you tap Confirm, and then the gift is logged once, the image is attached, and a thank-you draft that knows the donor's history is waiting for you. Steward never keeps the bank's routing or account number from a check, and the thank-yous screen now shows how long, on average, a gift waits for its thank-you.
