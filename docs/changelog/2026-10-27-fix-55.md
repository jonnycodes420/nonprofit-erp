title: Importing a spreadsheet is fast, and the preview keeps up with your answers
product: crm
---
The import preview now reads a large file in a second or two, and every answer you give updates the counts and the Import button right away. You can drop a spreadsheet on the import window, the Donors page or Settings, Imports, and the window always closes with X, Escape or a click outside. Pledges over several years show what is due this year and the total still to come, people you deleted can be kept out or restored all at once, and Settings, Imports has an Empty trash.
