title: Grants, as a system of its own
product: crm
---
Every funder now has a page of its own: its program officers, what it likes to fund, typical award size, when it takes applications, and every ask and award with your win rate. Your grants sit on a board from Prospecting to Closed that you can drag across, each with its deadlines, a checklist, its award paid in instalments that link to the cheques as they arrive, and its reports kept year after year. A shared library of your mission, history, programs, budget, board and bios drafts a first version of any section for you to edit, and email to a funder's program officer lands on their grant by itself.
