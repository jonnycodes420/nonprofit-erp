title: Unpublish a giving page, delete done tasks, and clearer labels
product: crm
---
A live giving page can now be unpublished from its menu. It goes back to Draft, and its link tells visitors it isn't taking gifts right now. Done tasks can be deleted, and they go to the trash with Undo. Settings now says plainly whether email is on and what sends on its own. Two campaigns or templates with the same name show when each was made, and you can rename one in place. Goal tiles name their dates, and the Home greeting follows your organization's time zone.
