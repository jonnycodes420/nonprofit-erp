title: A gift whose item name says "Total" is imported like any other
product: crm
---
When a spreadsheet has an item or lot name with the word "Total" in it, such as a class at a wine store, that row is now read as the gift it is, not mistaken for a total line. Real total and subtotal lines are still recognized and checked against the rows above them. A line that looks like a total but does not add up is listed for you to check, with a button to include it, and the count at the top says how many rows need a look.
