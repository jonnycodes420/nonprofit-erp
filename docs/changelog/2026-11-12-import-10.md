title: Spreadsheet imports read auction sheets, events and repeat files right
product: crm
---
A gift whose item name says "Total", such as a class at a wine store, now imports like any other, and a line that only looks like a total is listed for you to check. The Event box suggests the events already on your calendar as you type and asks "Did you mean" before making a second one, and opening a file you imported in the last 30 days asks before importing it again. Imported auction purchases now count as sold items with their winners, and people with no gift come in as contacts, not donors.
