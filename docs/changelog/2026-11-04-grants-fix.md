date: 2026-11-04
title: Every grant links to its funder, and Awarded records the award
product: crm
---
Any grant can now be linked to its funder's record from the grant itself, or from the note that asks for it: search your organizations by name or EIN, or add the funder as a new one. Moving a grant to Awarded now asks for the amount, the payment schedule and the restriction, and puts the promised money on the funder as a pledge you can see on their profile and in Fundraising.
