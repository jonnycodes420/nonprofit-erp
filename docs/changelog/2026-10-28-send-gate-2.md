title: One page that says exactly what Steward sends for you
product: crm
---
Settings now has a page called What Steward sends automatically. It lists every email Steward can send on its own, from receipts to pledge reminders, each with a preview and a switch, and every one starts off until you turn it on and choose who gets it. A new Going out page shows what will go in the next seven days, everything that went, and anything held back and why, and a small status at the top of every page says whether anything is on.
