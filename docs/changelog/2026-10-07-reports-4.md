title: Every number on Reports opens the gifts behind it
product: crm
---
Every number on the Reports tab now opens the gifts or people behind it, and they add up to the number on screen to the cent: retention, top donors, LYBUNT and SYBUNT, the three-year and annual reports, and the giving summary. Household totals, giving page totals and membership counts open too. Each number has a small "?" that says in one sentence how it is counted, and any list of rows can be downloaded or saved as a group.
