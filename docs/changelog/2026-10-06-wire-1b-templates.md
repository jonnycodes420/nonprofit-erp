Communications has one Templates tab now, instead of two that held the same kind of thing. Your letters, thank-yous, campaign emails and designed emails sit together in four sections: thank-yous and receipts, appeals and campaigns, events and volunteers, and grants and funders. Each card says whether it is a printed letter or an email, and whether someone here has reviewed it yet.
