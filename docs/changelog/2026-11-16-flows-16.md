title: Atlas saves follow-ups from any page, and clearer messages
product: crm
---
Ask Atlas to follow up with someone right after asking about them, on any page, and the reminder lands on their record. A question asked with a date keeps that date after you pick which person you meant. Skipped journey steps now say who skipped them, when and why, and error messages are always written in words.
