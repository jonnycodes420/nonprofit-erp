title: Filters for guests and introductions find everyone
product: crm
---
When you filter the Donors list by "Guest of", "Introduced by" or "Brought people in", Steward now searches everyone you know, not only the people who have given, and the switch above the list says Everyone. Guests usually haven't given yet, so before this they could be missing from the list.
