title: See who opens its list, and possible duplicates are real ones
product: crm
---
On Home, See who on "What your history shows" now opens the list of exactly the people its count names, and each name opens that person. Possible duplicates no longer match people on a blank or placeholder email or phone (such as "n/a" or 000-000-0000), a phone many people share, or a shared address alone, so the count after an import is the people worth a look.
