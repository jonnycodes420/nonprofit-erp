title: Fixes reach you sooner, and the night release no longer waits on a timer
product: platform
---
Behind the scenes, changes to Steward now line up and are tested one after another instead of restarting each other, so a fix waits less before it reaches you. The overnight release goes out the first time anything runs after 3:15 AM, and a weekly check tries the next version of our build machines before it is forced on us.
