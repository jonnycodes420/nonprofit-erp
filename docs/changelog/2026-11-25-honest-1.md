title: Connections say what is actually flowing
product: platform
---
Each connection in Settings now says one of four things: Working, with the last thing that came through and when; Connected but nothing flowing yet, with what is missing; Needs you, with the one step; or Not connected. Stripe with your giving pages off now says no one can give yet, and QuickBooks says how many gifts are waiting and which funds still need an account, with a checklist to map them in place.
When a giving tool goes quiet for longer than it usually does, Home shows a line and your admin gets one task. Tools with no account to connect sit under Bring in a file, and the footer's Privacy link now opens the policy in effect.
