title: A view-only role, and one sign-in for more than one organization
product: security
---
You can now invite someone as a Viewer (read only): they see every page your staff see except Team, billing and connections, and they can't change anything. Atlas answers their questions but won't log or draft for them, and an admin decides in Settings, Team whether viewers can export. Someone who belongs to more than one organization can now switch between them from their account menu without signing out, and the organization they are in shows at the top of every screen.
