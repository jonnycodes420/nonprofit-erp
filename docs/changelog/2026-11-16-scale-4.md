The three-year comparison and the annual report open in about two thirds of the time on a large file, with the same numbers to the cent. Steward's nightly checks now run quietly in the background, one organisation at a time, so they no longer compete with the people using the app, and quick follow-up work after a new gift never waits behind a long overnight job.
