date: 2026-11-01
title: Events count your guest list the way you do
product: crm
---
An event's numbers now count each household once, the way your guest list does: a couple who came together is one, with the number of people shown beside it ("172 came, 252 people"). Opening a number lists each household by name with its people under it. The check-in screen now opens on the name search, with the full guest list folded underneath until you ask for it.
