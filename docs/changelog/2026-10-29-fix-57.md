title: Your own signature, and names where there were emails
product: crm
---
Settings has a new My profile page where each person sets how they sign: name, title, phone, an optional photo and the signature block, with a preview. Emails and letters drafted for you now close with your signature, and a thank-you task signs with the person it is assigned to. Cards and activity show people's names instead of their email addresses, and a receipt never goes out with a blank where your address should be: it waits on the Going out page until you add your mailing address.
