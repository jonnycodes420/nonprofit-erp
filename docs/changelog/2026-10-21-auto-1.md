title: Journey emails that send themselves, in your words
product: crm

An owner or admin can now let one email step in a journey send itself, using words someone on your team wrote and saved, on weekday mornings between 9 and 11 where you are. You see who is getting it the night before, can skip anyone or pause everything in one click, and Steward never sends it to clients, anyone unsubscribed, anyone you wrote to this week, or anyone who just gave. Every journey starts with this off.
