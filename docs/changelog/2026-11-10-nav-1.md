date: 2026-11-10
title: Choose what your sidebar shows
product: crm
---
An admin can now choose which entries your team's sidebar shows, and in what order, in Settings, Menu. Every entry starts on, and each person can still hide more for themselves. An event's page now shows its auction, and Events has a list of all your auctions. Settings, Major gifts lets you pick which Major Gifts tabs your team sees.
