date: 2026-11-10
title: Choose what your sidebar shows
product: crm
---
An admin can now choose which entries your team's sidebar shows, and in what order, in Settings, Menu. Each person can still hide more for themselves. Auctions now live with your events: open an event to see its auction, or Events, Auctions for all of them. Settings, Major gifts lets you pick which Major Gifts tabs your team sees.
