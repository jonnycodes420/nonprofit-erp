title: Lost & Found is now a rescue plan
product: platform
---
The free Lost & Found audit now judges every donor against their own giving rhythm, the same way Steward does, and sorts them into three groups: call this week, call this month, and probably gone. Each group is ranked by what a year of their giving is worth, and every donor comes with one fact from your file, such as "$1,200 every February since 2022. Nothing for over a year." It still runs entirely in your browser, and the PDF has the same three groups.
