title: Journeys, events and volunteers behave the way you expect
product: crm

Only a journey that is on can take people now. Drafts and paused journeys are listed with the reason, and adding several people at once shows who can start and who can't. A journey tied to a date starts its first step on that date, a giver's next expected gift follows their own usual gap, and a preview or test email for someone who has never given reads the way they will see it.
