title: Getting set up, step by step
product: crm
---
While you are getting started, Home shows Getting set up: your agreement, your import, Stripe, your giving page and the places you take gifts, your first call and your first gift, each with one button, ticking itself as each thing really happens. When it is time to start calling, Who to call tomorrow lists the first five people on your moves with their last gift and why, and Log the call is right there. Once your first month is done it leaves Home, and Settings, under Account, keeps it.
