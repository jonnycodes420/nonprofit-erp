title: Giving, volunteer and membership pages, built the same way
product: giving
---
Membership pages are new, and they are built in the same editor as giving and volunteer pages, from the same blocks: a header with your logo, a photo or video, your story, numbers, a gallery, a quote, a goal bar, questions and answers, the form and a footer. Each page can have its own accent color, fonts, button style, corners and light or dark sections, and the editor tells you when two colors are hard to read. Every page type starts from three ready-made layouts that look finished with just your logo and colors, and a blank part of a page never shows on the public page.
