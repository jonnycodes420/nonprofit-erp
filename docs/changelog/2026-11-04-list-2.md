title: The Donors list shows exactly what you filter for
product: crm
---
Every filter on the Donors list now narrows the list, and the count above it counts the people shown. Your filters stay in the address, so a reload or the Back button keeps them, and each column sorts by its own value with people who have never given at the bottom. Search in the top bar finds people by household, organization or a name typed without its accents, and Enter opens the first match. Right after a big import, the Donors list opens quickly.
