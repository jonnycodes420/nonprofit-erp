title: Nobody disappears when an import is undone, and one email belongs to one person
product: crm
---
Undoing an import now removes only the people that import added or brought back from the trash, even when two imports run at once, so a long-time donor can never vanish from your list. Retention lists show a household once, the Owner column always names the current owner, and a donor kept Current by their household or trust now says so by name.
Data health now lists any email address on two people and any person and household holding the same gifts twice, and adding someone with an email already on file tells you who has it.
