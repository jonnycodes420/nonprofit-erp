title: A workbook of people and their gifts imports whole, and an import names the rows to check
product: crm
---
A workbook with a people sheet and a gifts sheet joined by an ID (such as Constituent ID) now imports its gifts. The preview says "60 people and 136 gifts ($8,005.00) joined on Constituent ID", names each sheet it set aside, and lets you correct what each sheet is. Before you import, the preview lists rows over $100,000,000, exact copies of another row (skipped unless you choose to import them), gifts far above a person's usual giving, and gifts dated after today with their dates.
The gift form refuses a gift over $100,000,000 in the same words, and asks before saving a gift far above both the person's usual gift and your largest gift. A gift dated after today has no Refund button until its date.
