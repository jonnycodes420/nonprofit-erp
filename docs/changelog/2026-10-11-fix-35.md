title: Thank-you calls include everyone who gave
product: crm
---
Do not solicit means don't ask for money, and thanking someone for a gift isn't asking. A donor marked do not solicit who gives now shows up in Calls to make for a thank-you call, while appeals, asking lists and re-engagement asks still leave them out. Anyone marked no phone calls stays off the call list.
