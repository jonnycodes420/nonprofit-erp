title: Street addresses in reports, Major Gifts in the menu, and auction sheets
product: crm
---
People reports, the Donors list and the donors export now include Street address, Address line 2 and a Full mailing address ready for labels and mail merges. Major Gifts has its own place in the menu under Raise, opening on your Top 100, with Portfolios, Pipeline and Plans beside it. You can import a gala's auction purchases from a spreadsheet: each purchase becomes a gift linked to the event, and each receipt says what was paid, what the item was worth and what is deductible.
