date: 2026-10-11
title: Gmail and Outlook updates arrive in seconds
product: crm
---
When a donor replies or you book a coffee in Gmail, Google Calendar or Outlook, it now shows on their profile within seconds instead of within five minutes. Steward still reads only mail and meetings with people already in Steward, and still checks on its own every 30 minutes. If instant updates ever pause, the Connections page says so and Steward goes back to reading every 5 minutes.
