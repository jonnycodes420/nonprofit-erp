When Steward says an email went, it now means the email provider accepted it; if the provider turns a message away, the screen says so and what to do next, for volunteer and donor page links, reminders, recurring-gift notices, waitlist offers, sign-in codes, receipts and invites. A campaign page now opens with its title, the goal bar and a Give now button, on a phone as well as a laptop, with the story underneath. Fund transactions, volunteer groups, shift sign-ups and email campaigns are real links you can open in a new tab.
