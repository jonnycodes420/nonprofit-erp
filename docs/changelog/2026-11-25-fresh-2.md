date: 2026-11-25
title: Lists and counts keep up with what you just did
product: crm
---
The giving line at the top of a person's notes now changes the moment you save or delete a gift. Recent sends on the Communications overview drops a campaign you delete, Home's side panel updates a person's lifetime giving after a gift, and the Volunteers header moves when a volunteer is removed, all without reloading the page. When you ask Atlas to list the people from an earlier answer and a gift has come in since, it now says so and asks you to ask again for today's figures.
