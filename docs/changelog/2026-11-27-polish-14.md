Home, imports, the Thread, recurring plans and pledge schedules now show your organization's date in words, with the year when it isn't this year. Finance and Reports give the same all-time total and say where gifts with no date are counted, and the board pack and Home say "so far this fiscal year" with last year's full figure beside it. The Acknowledgment backlog opens on the same dates as Home, with a count, a total and pages of 50, and new organizations start with exports off for view-only people, with a table in Settings, Team that says what each role can do.
