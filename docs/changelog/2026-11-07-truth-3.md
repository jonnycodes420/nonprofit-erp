title: Every money figure you send or show is checked when it is opened
product: crm
---
A drafted thank-you now states the person's giving as it stands the moment you open, preview or send it, including any total brought over from your old system. Atlas adds up several people's giving for you and asks which person you meant when a name matches more than one. Finance shows what has posted to your books, what is waiting to post and what is history from before Steward, funds that are one fund in two names can be merged with an undo, and Tax Receipts lists every receipt you have issued.
