date: 2026-11-08
title: A Trash you can restore from, and deletes that ask first
product: crm
---
Settings, Imports now has a Trash you can search, showing who deleted each person, when and why, with Restore on each row or on a selection. People leave the trash on their own after 30 days. Deleting a person now asks first and names what goes with them, and their household and relationship links come back when you restore them.
