date: 2026-11-11
title: Shifts, reports and Home cards that behave
product: crm
---
A draft shift no longer shows as upcoming, a draft with nobody on it can be deleted, and a shift opens the same full window wherever you open it. Shift times take what you type, like 11, 11am or 23:00, and say what they read before you save. LYBUNT, SYBUNT and Lost always offer Save as a group, Home's cards no longer carry a snooze form onto the next card, and a meeting counts as held only once it has happened.
