title: Receipts show the donor's own address, and in-kind receipts describe the gift
product: crm
---
A receipt now puts the donor's own mailing address under their name, or nothing if you have none on file. Your organization's address and EIN appear only in the header. An in-kind or stock receipt now describes what was given and the date you received it, and says the donor determines its value, with no dollar amount. In-kind gifts ask for a Description of property, and any receipt can be reissued under the same number from Settings, Tax Receipts.
