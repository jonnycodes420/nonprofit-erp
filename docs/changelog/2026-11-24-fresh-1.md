title: Trash, filters and exports say what they did, and an update never breaks an open page
product: crm
---
Emptying the trash now clears the list and its count straight away, with no reload. The Notes or tags filter matches whole words and says plainly when nobody's notes mention a word, and Export CSV tells you how many people went into the file and its name. If Steward is updated while you have a page open, the page reopens itself once instead of showing an error.
