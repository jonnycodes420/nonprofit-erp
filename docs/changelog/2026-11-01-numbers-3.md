title: Every giving number comes from one place
product: crm
---
Ask, Atlas and Finance no longer count a soft credit as money, and Fundraising's recent gifts no longer list one. Gifts you marked as already deposited now show as deposited in the bookkeeper's file and the deposits file, and your donor portal and merge fields show the same lifetime and this-year totals as the donor's profile.
