title: Gifts at events, pledges in installments, and grant payments
product: crm
---
A gift can now belong to an event: choose the event on the gift form, on a scanned check or on a deposit line, or use Record a gift on the event's page, and the event's Raised and the guest's row count it. A pledge can be paid in installments: say how many, how often and the first date, and you see the schedule before you save. On an awarded grant, Record a payment received adds the funder's gift to the books and shows what is still to come.
