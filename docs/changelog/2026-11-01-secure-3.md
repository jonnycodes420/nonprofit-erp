title: Your sign-in and your email are locked down further
product: security
---
Your sign-in can now live in a secure browser cookie that page scripts cannot read, and nobody had to sign in again for it. Email Steward reads from your Gmail or Outlook, and the attachments it keeps, are now stored encrypted; you see them on a person's record exactly as before. The database also refuses every connection that is not Steward's own server, even if a key were ever exposed.
