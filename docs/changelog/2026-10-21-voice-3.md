The microphone now takes instructions and questions too: say "Remind me to call Tobias Friday" and Steward makes the plan and waits for your confirm, or ask "Who should I call tomorrow?" and the names show right there, even mixed into one memo about a meeting. When you meet someone today or tomorrow, their profile and the meeting on your calendar show what to have in mind before you go in. Listening to a whole meeting is gone, and no meeting transcripts are kept.
