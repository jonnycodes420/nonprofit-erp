date: 2026-11-16
title: One thank-you task per gift, and tick a task off wherever you see it
product: crm
---
Entering a gift now gives you one task to thank the donor, not two: when the gift already has its thank-you step, Steward no longer adds a second "just came in" task beside it. The box beside a task on a donor's profile, on its Tasks tab and in Coming up now marks the task done in one click, with Undo, and the change shows on Home and the Tasks page straight away.
