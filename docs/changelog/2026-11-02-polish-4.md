title: Re-engage you can trust, merging two records yourself, and greetings that always read right
product: crm
---
Re-engage now leaves out anyone holding a pledge, and a business and its owner count as one: link a person to the organization they own or work at, and a gift from either keeps both off the list. You can merge any two records yourself from More on a profile, or by ticking two people in the Directory, and undo it for 30 days. Every draft and email now greets people properly, never "Dear Dr.," or a business name as a first name, and many small screens read more clearly.
