A campaign now says On pace only when the money raised keeps up with the time gone and its own projection reaches the goal; otherwise it says Behind pace and by how many dollars. Every money card on Fundraising names the dates it counts, the morning email lists the same tasks Tasks counts, and an audience's reasons always add up to its people.
