title: Off means off, and Atlas asks before it saves
product: crm
---
When your organization's email is off, every place in Steward now says Off, and a journey or sequence step that comes due waits as a draft for you to send instead of going out. Atlas now shows you what it would save (a call, a follow-up, a draft email signed with your signature) and writes nothing until you press Save. A gift gets one thank-you task, an owner change is recorded once, and Skip the next step leaves your existing follow-up alone.
