A donor's profile now has one header with their seven key numbers across it, and one row of tabs under it for gifts, asks, conversations, emails, tasks, notes and files. The overview shows what happened lately, the ask with its stage, and the household, planned giving, membership and sequence in one small card. The dark column on the right now runs the full page and adds their giving rhythm, monthly gift, thank-yous still to send, what's coming up, how to reach them and where their gifts come from, and every number in it opens the gifts behind it.
