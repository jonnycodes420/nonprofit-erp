title: Meet Atlas, the helper that does the whole job
product: agent
---
Tell Atlas what happened in one breath, from Home's box or the microphone, and it does all of it without taking you off the page you are on: marks who came, adds people to journeys, sets reminders and drafts the thank-you. If it needs to know which Undine you meant, it asks every question at once, with one click each. Gifts and anything sent wait for your tap, and Atlas leaves a small note with Undo everywhere it acted, with a bell that shows what is waiting for you.
