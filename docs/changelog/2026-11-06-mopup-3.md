title: Split gifts reach QuickBooks split, and a fairer ask for donors you brought with you
product: crm
---
A gift split across funds now goes to QuickBooks as one line per fund, each on its own fund's account, with the processing fee divided the same way, so the deposit still matches the bank. The bookkeeper's report, filtered to one fund, now finds that fund's part of a split gift. For a donor whose imported file told us what they gave before Steward, the suggested ask now starts from that history and says so, and Home on a phone stays still while a new organization's moves load.
