Giving pages, event pages, thank-yous, receipts, campaigns and journey emails now all open in the same editor: settings on the left, and the page or email itself in the middle, where you type your words and add a personal field like a first name. Preview any email as one of your own people, and a new campaign tells you in one line exactly who can get it and why the rest can't. Every new page and photo block starts with a real photo matched to your mission, ready for you to swap in your own.
