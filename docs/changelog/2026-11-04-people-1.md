title: Mark someone deceased from their profile, and link partners
product: crm
---
You can now mark someone as deceased from the More menu on their profile, with the date if you know it and a private note. From that moment they are out of every ask, suggestion, list, journey and email, their giving history stays as it was, and you can choose a yearly reminder to write to their family on the anniversary. Partner is now a relationship next to Spouse, and partners are treated as one household, so a gift from either keeps both off your lapsed lists.
