title: Who brought whom: Introduced by and Guest of
product: crm
---
You can now note how a person came in: who introduced them, or whose guest they were at an event. Both people's profiles show the link, and the person who brings people in gets one line of credit, such as "Brought in 4 people · $12,400 given since", which opens the exact gifts behind it. You can also pick "Guest of" when you add a guest at an event, filter the Donors list by who introduced or hosted someone, and bring both columns in from a spreadsheet.
