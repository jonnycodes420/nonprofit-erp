title: Days left you can trust, every giver on the Donors tab, and drafts that remember
product: crm
---
A campaign or giving page now counts its days in your own time zone, and its end date reads "Last day" all that day instead of "Ended". Anyone who has given now shows on the Donors tab and in portfolios even if their record was saved as a contact or organization, so the Donors total and Fundraising's lifetime match. The Thank-you starter shows the person's real last gift instead of a field name, Tasks tells you plainly whether your morning email is on, and Draft an email opens with what your team wrote in their About note.
