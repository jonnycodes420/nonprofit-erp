title: One answer for every number, decided
product: crm

A gift is now the full amount the donor gave, with the card fee and what reached your bank shown beside it, and soft and matching credits have their own Soft credit figure instead of adding to anyone's lifetime. Lapsed, lapsing, giving levels and households each have one rule used on every screen, report and Ask answer, and you set your giving levels under Settings. "How Steward counts" in Help says each one in plain words, and any automations you had turned on under the old Workflows screen are now journeys you can open and change.
