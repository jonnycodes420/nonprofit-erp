date: 2026-11-23
title: A receipt no longer counts as a thank-you, and removing a gift can be undone
product: crm
---
Making a tax receipt no longer marks a gift as thanked: the gift shows its receipt on its own line and still waits for your thank-you, and every list of gifts not yet thanked agrees. Removing a gift no longer leaves a note on the person's timeline; it appears in Recent changes with Undo for 30 days, and the delete asks one question that says whether the receipt was ever sent. After a second gift the profile now tells you the next step, the quick gift form takes a tribute, and the form says when a gift goes on the books.
