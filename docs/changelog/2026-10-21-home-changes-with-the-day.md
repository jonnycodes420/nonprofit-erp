The top of Home now changes with your day. A lunch in two hours brings its brief to the top, a big gift that arrived overnight asks to be thanked, and in December your year-end appeal shows how far it has to go and who gave last year but not yet this year. Each card says in one line why it is there, "Not now" sets it aside, and on a quiet day Home suggests three people worth a note instead.
