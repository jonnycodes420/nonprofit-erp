title: One gift count everywhere, and postdated gifts handled one way
product: crm
---
Reports, Finance and the header now count gifts by the same rule, so a month's rows always add up to the total above them, and a split gift is one gift. A gift dated after today, typed in or imported, is kept as Scheduled: it is listed on the person's record and counts in totals on its date, so an import with postdated rows adds up. Thank-you letters can now name a donor's first, latest or largest gift, and the first-gift thank-you quotes the first gift.
