title: One link to sign your Steward order
product: platform
---
When you're ready to start with Steward, you get one link: read your order, sign it, add a card (nothing is charged until your billing start date), and book your setup call. The signing page no longer shows a blank paper signature block above the online one. Your signed copy has that block filled in with your name, title and the time you signed.
