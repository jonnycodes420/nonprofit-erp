date: 2026-11-06
title: Every change is tried on a practice copy of Steward first
product: platform
---
Before a change reaches your account, it now goes to a separate practice copy of Steward filled with made-up people, where it is walked through signing in, opening a donor, recording a gift, importing a file and running a report. Only when that walk passes does it go out to you. The practice copy never holds your data and can never email a real person or charge a real card.
