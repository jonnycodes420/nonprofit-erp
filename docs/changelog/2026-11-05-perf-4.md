date: 2026-11-05
title: Pages open faster after you sign in
product: crm
---
Donors, a donor's profile, Fundraising, Reports, Journeys and Settings now start loading what they show the moment the page opens, instead of waiting for the rest of Steward to load first. A profile no longer asks for the same information twice, and the Donors list downloads less before it appears. Nothing on any page has changed, only how soon it is there.
