title: Reports go to your board on their own, and every report leads to a next step
product: crm
---
Any saved report, the board pack or the new board deck can now be emailed on a schedule (weekly, monthly or quarterly, on your day and hour) to people you name, like a board chair or a funder contact, with no Steward login: each email carries the report as a PDF with its definitions. Every report now has "Do something with these people": plan calls, make a group, add them to a journey or start a mailing draft, each with Undo. Reports opens on Your dashboard, nine reports you already have, and "Add a report" lets you pick from every report Steward has.
