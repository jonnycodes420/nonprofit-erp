date: 2026-11-08
title: A pledge says the same thing everywhere
product: fundraising
---
Every place a pledge appears (the donor's profile, the Pledges list, Fundraising's figures and the Calendar) now says the same five things: what was pledged, what has come in, what is still open, and the next installment's amount and date. When you record a payment on a pledge, the form tells you what it covers before you save ("This covers the Dec installment and $50 of Jan."). A receipt whose gift was changed after it was issued now says so, with one click to void it and issue a new one, and an awarded grant with no payment schedule says "payments not set up" instead of asking you to record the award again.
