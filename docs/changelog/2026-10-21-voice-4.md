title: Tell Steward does it all
product: crm
---
Tell Steward about a lunch and a gift, and one tap records the gift, updates every number on their profile at once, and readies the receipt and thank-you to send together. The meeting on your calendar is marked as happened and its reminders close themselves, and "Do all of this" handles every card in one go, with one Undo for all of it. You can also say "Put her in the Major donor journey" or "Add Margaret to the Board prospects group", and Steward does it, or offers the closest names when one does not match.
