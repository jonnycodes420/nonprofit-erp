title: Refunds go with their gifts, and portfolios add up
product: crm
---
Undoing a deposit now removes any refund recorded against its gifts, and a refunded gift can be deleted together with its refund. The Giving summary's average is now always its total divided by its gift count, a household's suggested ask says when it comes from the whole household's giving, and a thank-you never quotes a refund as the gift. A gift refunded in full says it needs no thank-you, and the officer portfolios now end with an Unassigned row so they add up to your donor list.
