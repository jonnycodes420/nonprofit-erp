date: 2026-11-18
title: Log one touch to many people, and tasks in date order
product: crm
---
You can now log the same touch to many people at once: pick them on Donors, or tick several names in Log a touch, give it a title like "Invited to Spring awareness night", and it lands on each person's timeline, with one Undo for the whole batch. A new Donors filter, Touch titled, shows who has or hasn't had a touch, and Steward warns you before logging it twice. A person's tasks now list soonest first, and their next step is the soonest task coming up everywhere it's shown.
