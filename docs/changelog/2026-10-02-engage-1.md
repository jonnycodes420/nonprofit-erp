title: Who's warm, who's slipping, and what to ask
product: crm
---
Every donor now has two scores from 0 to 100, measured against your own donors: Engagement (how close they are, from meetings, calls, replies, events, volunteering and newsletter clicks in the last two years) and Generosity (how much they give), with a plain band of Close, Warm or Distant and a "See why" that shows every part and the rows behind it. The record suggests what to ask for from the donor's own gifts and shows the math in one line, and the Donors list can sort by engagement or show who is warm but has not been asked this year. After an appeal, "How did it do?" compares it with last year's, explains the gap, and lists who to call, with a button that plans the follow-up and sends nothing.
