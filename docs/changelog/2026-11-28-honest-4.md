date: 2026-11-28
title: Every connection shows its last real activity
product: crm
---
Each connection now says the last real thing it did, such as "Last gift read Nov 26, 2:14 PM", and what came through it in the last 30 days. A connection over example or sample data says "Example data" and isn't counted as a connection. QuickBooks now opens on what is waiting and why, each reason a link to fix it. After three failed tries in a row it pauses and waits for you to press Try again. Recurring gifts show when each card was last updated, and whether the bank or the donor updated it. The book-a-call page shows three weeks of open times at once.
