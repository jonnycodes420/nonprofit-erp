title: Emails that show a real donor, and audiences that add up
product: crm
---
Every email template now previews as one of your own donors, with their name, gift and total, instead of you or a made-up name, and the samples under Automatic email use your organization's name. A campaign to lapsed donors now reaches the same people the Audience tab counts, a receipt never shows a blank where your address goes, and a year-end statement no longer calls itself a receipt. The Sequences tab is one list instead of two, held emails on Going out say what to do next, and Communications reads in plain sentence case throughout.
