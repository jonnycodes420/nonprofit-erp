Hand Steward your donor files and your website address, and it shows you its plan before it does anything: what it will do on its own, what it has ready for your click, and the few questions only you can answer. After one Start it imports your people and gifts and checks them to the cent, fills in your profile, receipt details and branding, creates your funds and starter groups, writes thank-you drafts in your own words and builds three journeys, all paused. It never emails anyone, invitations and connections wait for your click, and one button undoes all of it.
