title: Screens that catch up after you act, and clicks that do something
product: crm
---
After you add a guest, mark someone attended or record a gift, the numbers beside it say "Updating…" until the new figure lands, instead of showing the old one. Meetings save and move in the demo, stopping one person's journey skips the steps left instead of marking them done (with an Undo), and Next shift shows a place on the waiting list. A logo upload saves straight away, Billing explains why its record count differs from the Donors tab, and the sidebar's Donors link works from a profile.
