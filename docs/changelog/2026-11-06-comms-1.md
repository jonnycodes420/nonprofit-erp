title: Clear drafts you won't send, and invites that arrive
product: crm
---
Any draft waiting for you, from a thank-you to a note Atlas wrote, now has an × to dismiss it, and Select clears a long list at once. A dismissed draft is kept, never sent, and comes back with one click from Dismissed at the foot of the list. Staff invites now arrive by email even if your organization hasn't set up its own mail, and a campaign that is waiting on Campaign emails tells you where to turn them on.
