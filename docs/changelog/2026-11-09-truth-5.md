title: Every amount says what it is
product: crm
---
Asking who gave in a period now lists what each person gave in that period, with their lifetime beside it and labeled Lifetime. A person who gives through a business or trust keeps their own row and amount on Gave last year, not this year, and an organization's profile shows what was given through it rather than each linked person's lifetime. Fundraising Overview can show this fiscal year, last fiscal year or any dates you choose, and a pledge made before installments now shows its schedule with each payment on it.
