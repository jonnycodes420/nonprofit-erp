title: Free event tickets work without payments, and every event screen says what it will do
product: crm
---
You can now make a free ticket ($0) and people can register for it on the event page with no payment account connected; a free registration records no gift. Paid tickets can be set up before payments are connected and say they go on sale once you connect them. Check-in at the door finds invited and waiting-list guests by name or email, a published volunteer shift can be removed (with its sign-ups cancelled and an Undo), and an event's Archive button says what the event holds.
