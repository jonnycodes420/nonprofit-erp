title: Privacy mode, for organizations that serve clients
product: security
---
If the people you serve are private, turn on "We serve clients whose details are private" in Settings, Privacy, and mark your clients on their page, from a list or group, or by uploading a list. Steward then never brings in email, documents or memos about them, while their gifts and thank-yous work as before. In everyone else's email, health details are replaced with a short note to open it in your mailbox, so they are never stored in Steward.
