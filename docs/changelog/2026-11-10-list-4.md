title: Sort the Donors list by your own fields
product: crm
---
Click the heading of any custom field column on the Donors list to sort by it, and click again to reverse. Numbers and amounts sort by size, dates by day, a choice list in the order you set its options, text from A to Z, and Yes/No with Yes first, and people with nothing filled in always come last. Save it as a view and the order comes back with it. A filter on a custom Number field, such as "at least 4", works again.
