date: 2026-11-05
title: Every number on screen updates the moment something changes
product: crm
---
When you record a gift, log hours, remove a group, add a task, record a pledge or grant payment, or undo an import, every total on the screen you are looking at updates at once, with no reload. The same is true when Atlas makes the change for you, and when Steward finishes work in the background, which reaches your screen within a minute.
