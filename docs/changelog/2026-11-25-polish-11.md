title: Fewer dead ends: next steps, Undo, campaigns and imports
product: crm
---
Logging a touch now asks before it closes the person's next step, including when that step is a task, and Keep it open is the default. Marking a volunteer as former keeps its Undo for a day, on their record and in Recent changes, even after you leave the page. Typing a campaign name that only starts another one still offers to create it, and each import in Settings says until when it can be undone.
