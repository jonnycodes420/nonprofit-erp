title: The Agent can see grants, memberships, campaigns, pages and auctions
product: crm
---
Ask the Agent who has a grant report due in the next 30 days, or tell it to draft a renewal to every member whose membership ends next month, and it finds exactly those people. It can draft a funder update from your own template, a thank-you to everyone who gave to an appeal and a note to an auction winner about paying for their item, and it can make a checklist task on a grant or a reminder to approve a peer-to-peer page. Every draft and task lands on the person's record and in Drafts to review, and nothing is sent until you send it.
