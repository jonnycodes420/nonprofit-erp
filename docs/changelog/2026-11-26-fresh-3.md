title: Finance, Volunteers and Events update the moment you save, and each tab keeps its organization
product: crm
---
Every Finance panel now updates as soon as a gift, pledge, refund or recurring plan changes, with no reload. So do the volunteer Schedule, Applications, a profile's volunteer panel and the Events desk. Schedule conflicts now counts the overlaps on the dates you are looking at, the same shifts the cards tag.
If you work in two organizations and switch in one browser tab, your other tabs stay where they were and nobody is signed out. A tab can follow the switch or stay. Someone who already volunteers and applies again is told they are already on the team, and a written-off pledge says plainly why it can't be deleted.
