title: Ask Steward anything about your own records
product: crm
---
Ask in your own words about gifts, conversations, events and guests, volunteer hours, recurring gifts, campaigns, asks, grants, tasks or memberships, and combine them ("how many people came to the gala and also gave this year?"). Steward works out the answer from your records, and every number opens the gifts or people behind it.
