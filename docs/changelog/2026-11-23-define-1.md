Lapsed, LYBUNT, Re-engage and your audiences now each show their name and a one-line definition under the number, so two different counts no longer look like one answer that disagrees with itself. A person counts as a donor once they have given, so someone you add by hand stays a contact until their first gift, and the All donors audience only holds people who have given. There is now one Volunteers audience, and two records that share an email address get one copy of an email, not two.
