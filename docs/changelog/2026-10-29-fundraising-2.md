title: Fundraising adds up, and your dinner tells its story
product: crm

Fundraising now counts only the goals running today, says when a goal starts instead of giving it a pace, and folds finished goals under Past with their results. A new Pledges view shows what is still to come, what is due this fiscal year and what is overdue, with every installment, and the same three numbers appear on the Overview, Home and each person's record. An event page now shows what the night raised, who came, who gave, and who came and did not give, each opening its list, and a giving page stays a draft with a friendly "not open yet" address until payments are connected.
