title: Log any touch in your own words, and edit the Ask right in the Top 100
product: crm
---
"Log a call" is now "Log a touch": pick what you did (a call, a handwritten note, an email, a visit, a meeting, a gift you sent, an event invite, a text, or a kind your team adds in Settings) and write exactly what it was. It shows on their timeline in your words and counts as their last contact, and if it matches a touch on their plan of care, logging it marks that touch done, early or not. Any task can now be marked done on any day, and in your Top 100 you can click the Ask, Ask by, Stage, Owner or Next step to change it right in the table.
