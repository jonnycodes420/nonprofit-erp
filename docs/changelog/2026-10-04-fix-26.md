title: One recipient count, scanning on any phone, and search on a phone
product: crm
---
The campaign builder now shows one count of who will get your email, and "left out" opens the list of people it will skip and why: no email address, unsubscribed, bounced, deceased or do not contact. Scanning tickets at the door now works on iPhones and in Firefox, not only in Chrome. On a phone you can search from the header, and the campaign builder and event pages fit the screen.
