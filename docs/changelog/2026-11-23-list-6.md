title: One Giving strength, and Clear filters clears everything on the People list
product: crm
---
Giving strength on the People list is now the same number as on the person's profile, and hovering it shows the parts it is made of. Clear filters now clears every filter, Owner included, and you can find people by words in their notes, About or tags with + Add filter, then Notes or tags. A saved view you change can be updated in place, and someone you marked a donor who has never given now reads as a contact on their profile, as they do on the list.
