title: Campaign pages, asks you have not made, and drafts that say only what is true
product: crm
---
Every campaign now has its own page, and its raised amount and donor count open the gifts and people behind them. You can ask for "major donors who haven't been asked" and get each one with a suggested ask and a button to plan it, and the Agent can plan calls to everyone who gave to last year's appeal but not this year's. A drafted email now only mentions a video, an amount, a date, a meeting or an event when your records hold it; otherwise you get the plain thank-you instead.
