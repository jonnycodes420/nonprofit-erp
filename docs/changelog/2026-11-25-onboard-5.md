title: Setup is one calm page at a time
product: crm
---
Setting up Steward is now its own page, ten short steps, one idea each: your logo and colors, your donors, your receipts and letters, your funds and campaigns, how you talk, where gifts come in, QuickBooks, email, your team, and who to call this week. Each connection shows the service's own logo, what it does for you and "Connected as …" once linked. Your progress saves as you go, so you can leave and come back to the same step; Home shows "Finish setting up" until you're done, and Settings → Account always has the way back in.
