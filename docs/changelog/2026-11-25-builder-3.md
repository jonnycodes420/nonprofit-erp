title: Work on an email together, share it for review, and set your house style once
product: crm
---
When a colleague has the same email open, you see their face at the top and an outline on the part they are working on, and both of your changes save. You can comment on any part of an email and mention a colleague, share a private view-only link with a board member, and download the email's HTML. A new Styles tab sets the fonts, sizes, link color and button shape for every email your organization sends.
