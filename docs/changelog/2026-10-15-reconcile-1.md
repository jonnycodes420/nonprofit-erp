title: Your old reports, checked against Steward
product: crm
---
When you bring in a report from the system you used before, Steward now checks it against its own gifts, donors, funds and, when connected, your books, and says in one sentence whether it matches to the cent or how much is missing. Where the two disagree, it shows why when it can: a fund it has no match for, people it has no record of, a gift dated just outside the period, a refund, a soft credit, a giving source not connected yet. You can add the missing person, map the fund, or use the old report's total as last year's number for your goals, each with one click and Undo, and the check runs again by itself whenever new gifts come in.
