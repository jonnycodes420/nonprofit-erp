title: Your inbox and calendar, on every donor's record
product: crm
---
Connect Gmail and Google Calendar, or Outlook and Microsoft 365, and every email and meeting with someone already in Steward lands on their profile by itself. Before a meeting you get a short brief from their own record, and afterwards Steward asks how it went and offers to record the pledge or next step you mention. Nothing about anyone outside Steward is kept, not even a count.
