Set your brand kit once in Settings (two colours, your signature, your address and your tax language) and every letter, email template and receipt uses it. Ten ready templates, from first-gift thank-yous to the year-end statement, start in plain, warm words, say "Not yet reviewed" until you have made them yours, and preview against any donor before you print a letter or make a draft to send. Year-end statements now add a short "Your year with us" with the hours someone volunteered and the events they came to, which you can turn off.
