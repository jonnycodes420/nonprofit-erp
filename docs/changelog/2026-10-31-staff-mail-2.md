title: One email a week to start
product: crm
---
Everyone new on your team now starts with one Steward email, "Your week": Monday at 7 AM, the week ahead (next steps, follow-ups, pledge payments and events) and last week in a few lines. Want it every morning instead? Turn on the daily email under Settings, Your email, where every other Steward email has its own switch too. If you are already on Steward, nothing about your email changes.
