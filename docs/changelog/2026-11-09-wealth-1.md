date: 2026-11-09
title: A Wealth Score on every person
product: crm
---
Every person's record now has a Wealth Score, 1 to 5, right under their address. Click it to set it or clear it. On the Donors list you can filter by it (equals 5, at least 4, at most 2, or not set), sort by it either way, save that as a view, set it for many people at once, and bring scores in from a spreadsheet. Reports has a new starter, Donors by Wealth Score, and Steward can suggest a score from someone's own giving, though nothing is set until you click.
