title: Surveys for your donors and volunteers
product: crm
---
You can now ask your people what they think: build a survey with short and long answers, one or several choices, a 0 to 10 scale and yes or no questions, in your own colours, and share it by link, QR code or on your website. Each survey is named or anonymous and says so at the top; a named answer lands on that person's record, and an anonymous one keeps no name, email or internet address and is on no one's record. Results show every answer counted and charted, each count opens the responses behind it, and "Send this survey" puts a draft for each person in Drafts to review for you to send.
