date: 2026-11-07
title: Two things at once no longer lose a journey step or double a task
product: crm
---
When two things happen to the same person at the same moment, Steward now handles them one at a time. A journey's next step always lands on the person's Thread, a yearly task ticked done twice makes one next task, and a receipt pressed twice makes one receipt and uses one number. Receipt numbers never repeat or skip.
