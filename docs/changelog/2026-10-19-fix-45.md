Confirming a check in To confirm now finishes as soon as the gift is recorded, the check's photo is in the donor's Files, and the deposit slip line says how many checks are logged and what is left. Undo stays for a minute, and Recent changes in your profile menu lists your last fifty changes with Undo, including cards you skipped in To confirm. Thank-you drafts greet both people on a joint record, a memo like "for the boathouse" finds the right fund, and donor search narrows the list as you type.
