title: Import keeps everything in your file, even gifts with no date
product: crm
---
Bring in an event workbook and Steward now reads it the way you would: one list of people across every sheet, who gave, who pledged, who was invited and who came, and who asked not to be emailed, each with where it came from. A gift with no date still lands on its person, marked No date, and you can date them all with the event's date in one click. Any column Steward has no field for is kept on the person under its own name, so nothing in your file is lost.
