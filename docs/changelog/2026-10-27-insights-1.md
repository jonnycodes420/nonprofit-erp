title: Steward tells you who to call, why, and what to say
product: crm
---
Home now opens with your donor ladder (second gift, retain, upgrade, major, planned) and a short list of suggestions, each with the facts, one sentence on why it matters, and exactly how to act: who reaches out, when, what to say and how much to ask, with the rule behind the ask. After an import you see "What your file says" first, and rows that look like purchases, tickets or reimbursements wait for you to decide before any total changes. Steward never sends anything for you; every draft, task and call list waits for your click.
