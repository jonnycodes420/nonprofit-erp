title: Import any spreadsheet, including your event night's sheet
product: crm
---
Drop in any spreadsheet, even one with no dates, a title row, couples' names or a total at the bottom, and Steward shows you what it understood before anything is saved. It asks only what it cannot tell, like the day an event's gifts were given, checks your own total row to the dollar, and keeps pledges, couples and monthly givers straight. One click imports it all, and one click undoes it.
