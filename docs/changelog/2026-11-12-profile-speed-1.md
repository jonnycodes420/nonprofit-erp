title: Profiles and busy pages open faster
product: crm
---
Opening a donor's profile now takes one trip to the server instead of two, so the header, the numbers and the right rail arrive together. Communications no longer loads every person in your file when it opens, and Donors, Reports, Settings and Events ask for what they show in one go. When several people on your team are working at once, no one page can hold up everyone else's.
