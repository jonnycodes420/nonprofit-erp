title: Drag, drop and reuse parts of your emails
product: crm
---
In the email editor you can now drag a part from Elements or Blocks into the email and see where it will land, move any part by its handle or with Alt and the arrow keys, and add something between two parts with the "+". Columns, boxes and your social links are new parts, and you can save any part to reuse it, or start from ready-made sections in your brand. Copy a part and paste it into another email of yours.
