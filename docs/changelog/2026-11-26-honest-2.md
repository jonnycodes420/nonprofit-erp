title: Settings has a Billing page, and connection badges say only what's true
product: platform
---
Settings now has a Billing page with your plan, price, next charge, card and invoices, and every plan link in Steward goes there. A connection's badge reads the same status everywhere, including Finance and setup, and the public Connections page uses the same three groups as Settings. Giving levels say what's wrong under the field, suggested amounts stay in order, and a draft never copies a note's label into a donor's email.
