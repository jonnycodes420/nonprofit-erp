title: Year over year and the Annual report open faster when several people read reports at once
product: crm
---
Year over year now reads every year in one pass, so on a large file it opens in a fraction of the time it took before, and the Annual report no longer slows the rest of Steward while it runs. Every number on both reports is exactly what it was. If a very large report ever runs out of time, it now says so plainly and suggests a smaller date range instead of showing an error.
