title: Home is calmer, even, and every name and number opens what it says
product: crm
---
Home now sits on one grid: your four groups of moves are four equal tiles in whole dollars, the setup checklist is a single line until you open it, and the automatic email setting lives only in Settings, Automatic email. The people drifting from their usual giving are a short list you can scan, five at a time, each with their last gift. Clicking a name on Home opens that person, "40 follow-ups" opens all forty, and an import's readout opens what the import told you.
