When you change a gift's date into this year, or put back a gift or pledge with Undo, the person leaves Lapsed on the Donors list right away, the same moment their profile says Current. Deleting a pledge now takes it out of the profile's suggested ask at once, and printing thank-you letters updates every screen that shows which gifts were printed, with no reload.
