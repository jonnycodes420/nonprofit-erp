date: 2026-10-06
title: Peer-to-peer campaigns from their own tab, and every grant deadline on your calendar
product: crm
---
You can start a peer-to-peer campaign straight from the Peer-to-peer tab, and add a fundraiser for a supporter yourself. A grant can now carry as many deadlines as the funder sets, including ones you name, and each one shows on the Steward Calendar, where you can drag it to a new day. If your Google or Outlook calendar is connected, you can choose to have your own deadlines and next steps appear there too.
