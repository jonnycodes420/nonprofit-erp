title: Your morning email, your way, and nothing else unless you ask
product: crm
---
The morning email is now the only email Steward sends your team without being asked, and each person sets it up under Settings, My email: what goes in it (Thread steps, tasks, today's meetings, yesterday's readouts), the hour it comes, and weekdays or every day. Everything else Steward can email you, from new-gift alerts and task assignments to Week in Review and the monthly report, is now off until you tick it on the same page. If you relied on one of those alerts, turn it back on there and it works as before. Everyone's morning email starts on weekdays; pick every day on the same page if you want weekends too.
