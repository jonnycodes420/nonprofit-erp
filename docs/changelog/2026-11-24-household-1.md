title: A couple is one row on your lists, and each of them keeps their own details
product: crm
---
A household can now hold one place on your Top 100, with both people's giving added together, the higher Wealth Score, and both photos side by side; adding one of a couple offers to add the household instead. The Donors list has a "Show households together" switch that turns each household into one row you can open, and an export can include both people with their own email and phone, an addressee line like "Debbie and Jeff Vogel", or one row per household for a mailing. If you merged a couple in the last 30 days, their profile now offers to split them back into a household, with each person's email, phone, photo and gifts restored.
