title: Staff who see only the donors assigned to them
product: security
---
In Settings, Team, each staff member can now see everyone or only the people assigned to them. Someone limited to their own people sees only those people in the Donors list, search, Atlas, Home, their morning email and exports, and everyone else simply isn't there for them. The Campaign cabinet choice on an invite sets this up in one step, with no export and no Wealth Score.
