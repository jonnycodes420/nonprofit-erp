date: 2026-10-02
title: Wallets, bank transfers, and amounts drawn from your own donors
product: giving
---
Your donation form no longer forces donors to type a card number. Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, a US bank account and PayPal now appear on it wherever your own Stripe account accepts them, and Settings shows you which ones your donors will see. A donor can tick "remember my card" and give again in one tap from an email link, and the suggested amounts can start from an organisation's own gift history, or from a returning donor's own last gift. The cover-the-fee box now adds your processing rate rather than a fixed one, every online gift records what the processor actually took beside what was charged, and Fundraising shows what the failed-card emails recovered this year with the gifts behind the figure.
