date: 2026-10-12
title: Old gifts brought in from your history no longer start journeys
product: crm
---
When Steward reads past gifts from QuickBooks, PayPal, Zeffy, Givebutter or Square for the first time, a gift older than 30 days is still recorded and counted everywhere, but it no longer starts a journey or a thank-you draft. A donor is never thanked today for a gift from last spring. Gifts from the last 30 days, and every live gift after that, work exactly as before.
