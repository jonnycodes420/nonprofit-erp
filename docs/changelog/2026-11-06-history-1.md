date: 2026-11-06
title: Each donor's giving by year, and the whole organization's year over year
product: crm
---
A donor's Giving tab now shows their giving one fiscal year at a time: the total, the number of gifts, the largest gift and how it changed from the year before, with a bar chart above it. A total brought over from your old system shows as its own "Before Steward" line, and soft credit sits on its own line under each year. Reports has a new Year over year report for the whole organization, showing donors, new donors, retained donors and retention for each year. The year in progress is compared with the same point last year, and every number opens the gifts or people behind it.
