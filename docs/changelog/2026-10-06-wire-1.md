title: Everything you do shows up everywhere it matters
product: crm
---
Ask "what has Maria done with us this year?" and Steward answers with her gifts, hours, events, membership, fundraising page and conversations, each one opening the records behind it. Memberships ending, auctions closing and giving pages ending are now on the calendar, and things that need a person (a lapsed membership, a missed shift, an unpaid auction win, a page waiting for approval) become a next step on your list. Merging two records now brings their meetings along too, so nothing about a person is left behind.
