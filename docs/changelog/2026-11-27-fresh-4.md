title: Every screen shows your change the moment you save it
product: crm
---
When you save a gift, a pledge, a recurring plan, volunteer hours, a shift, a meeting, a journey or a template, every number and list on your screen updates within a moment, without reloading. Renaming your organization now changes the name on Home straight away. Taking a ready-made block like Event invite out of an email takes all of its parts out, and clearing a volunteer's hours a week asks before it clears.
