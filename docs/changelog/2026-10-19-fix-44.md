title: Ask names the people it finds
product: crm
---
When you ask Steward for a list, such as your donors in one town or who gave over $500 and hasn't been thanked, the answer now names the people it found, starting with the three who matter most. If nobody matches, it says which part of the question found nobody. You can also ask who gave for the first time this month, and the list holds only people whose very first gift came this month.
