title: Save a dashboard, and send your board the same numbers every quarter
product: crm
---
You can now build a dashboard out of any number, chart or list in Reports, arrange the tiles, name it and share it with your team. The date range, fund, campaign and owner you pick are saved with it and live in its web address, so it opens, reloads and shares as the same numbers. Every tile still opens the rows behind it.
There is also a board pack: one PDF with giving this period against last year, your donors and retention, your top gifts, campaign progress, volunteer hours, and a page defining every number on it, under your own name and logo. You can build it from the default or from any dashboard you saved, and Steward can email it to your staff and board addresses every month or quarter on a day you choose. The schedule stays off until you turn it on, there is a button to send a test to yourself first, and it never goes to a donor.
