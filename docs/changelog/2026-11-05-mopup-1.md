date: 2026-11-05
title: A grant's money counts once, and dates start on your own day
product: crm
---
A grant marked Awarded before this week no longer counts its whole award as income on top of the payments you record against it, so Finance shows what came in. Date fields that fill themselves in, such as a new transaction, the deposit sheet and "Show again on", start on your organization's own day, even after 8 PM. Events now say Upcoming, Today or Past from their date, a fund under 1% of someone's giving says "under 1% of lifetime", and the Home move card no longer repeats the word Ask.
