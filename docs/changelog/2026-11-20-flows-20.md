title: Finish a call from a profile, undo a bulk touch later, and journey counts that agree
product: crm
---
Ticking a call or meeting task on a person's profile now opens the same "How did it go?" dialog as the Tasks page, and saving it finishes that task without touching the person's other next step. A touch logged to many people is now one line in Recent changes, with an Undo that works any time in the next 30 days. A journey's counts on the Journeys list and in "How it is going" now match, saved views apply again when you pick them after changing a filter, and the "Touch titled" filter suggests the titles you've used.
