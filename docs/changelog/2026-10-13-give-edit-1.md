title: Edit any giving page right from Fundraising
product: crm
---
Every giving page and your donation form now have the same four buttons on Fundraising: Edit the page, Open page, Copy link, and QR and embed. The editor changes the headline, story, photo, suggested amounts, which gift the form opens on, the fee box, where the gift goes, the goal bar and the thank-you, with a live preview at desktop and phone width side by side. Edits wait as a draft until you press Publish, the link never changes, and every published version is kept with who published it, so you can put an earlier one back in one click.
