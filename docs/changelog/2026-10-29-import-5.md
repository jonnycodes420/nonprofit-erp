title: Imports that never guess a date, and the guests who came but didn't give
product: crm
---
When a spreadsheet names an event but has no dates, Steward now asks for the date before it imports, then asks whether the gifts should take that date and when the pledges are due, and it never makes a date up. Each event page lists who came and gave, who came and didn't give, and who was invited and didn't come, and Home suggests a thank-you for coming to each guest who didn't give. An open pledge now sits at the top of the person's page with its next installment, and exports from Salesforce, Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, Little Green Light, Neon, eTapestry, Raiser's Edge, Givebutter, Donorbox, PayPal Giving Fund and QuickBooks import without column questions, remembering your answers for next time.
