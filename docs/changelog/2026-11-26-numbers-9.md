A fund now says, in one line, why its gifts on the books and its transactions differ (for example, a card fee a donor covered), and each part opens the gifts behind it. A letter that covers several gifts names each one and gives the donor's total deductible amount, the same figure as your letters list and the year-end statement. Last gift shows a refund ("$35 · $50 less a $15 refund"), Reports shows a household's lifetime as each person's share added up, someone linked to a business as Owner sees what they give through it, and "waiting to be thanked" is counted the same way on Home, Acknowledgments, Drafts to review and the Report builder.
