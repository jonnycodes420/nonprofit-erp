date: 2026-11-05
title: Saved views keep every filter, and Raised opens its gifts
product: crm
---
A saved view on Donors now brings back every filter it was saved with, including Stage, Owner and Tag, along with its sort and columns. On Fundraising, Raised opens the gifts behind the number, and Lost & Found sits in Fundraising's own menu. Gives monthly now means a monthly plan or gifts about a month apart, the compare screen for a merge says which record is kept, and you can change your organization's name in Settings.
