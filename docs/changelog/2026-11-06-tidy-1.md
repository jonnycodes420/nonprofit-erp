date: 2026-11-06
title: See your morning email before it comes, and send yourself a test
product: crm
---
In Settings, under Your email, you can now preview today's morning email exactly as it will arrive, and send a test copy to your own address (up to three a day). The page also says when your last morning email went out and how many items it carried. Lists that Atlas makes when you press Open list now sit in their own "From Atlas" section on Groups and tidy themselves away after 30 days, unless you rename one, keep it, or use it in a campaign.
