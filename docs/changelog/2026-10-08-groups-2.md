title: Starter groups, so Groups is never empty
product: crm
---
Groups now opens with the lists your records already hold, like "Gave last year, not yet this year" and "Gifts waiting for a thank-you", each with how many people are in it today. Tap a name to see who is in it and the rule in plain words, then add it in one click and it keeps itself up to date. Lists meant for asking leave out anyone marked do not solicit, and say how many they left out.
