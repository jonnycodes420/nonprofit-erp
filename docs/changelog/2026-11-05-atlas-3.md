title: Atlas answers with the people, and the More menu does what it says
product: agent
---
Ask Atlas "who gave last December but not this year?" and you get the people, each with why they matched, a name that opens their record, and Open list, which opens the same group every time. Ask about one person and Atlas tells you what your team knows: their About note, their last conversations and their next step; "Tell me more" now keeps the conversation going, and Atlas asks you to record a gift before it thanks anyone for it. On a profile, Brief me starts at once and keeps your last brief until something changes, Call script writes a call script, and Impact summary shows the summary with its PDF beside it.
