title: Your donor list, your columns, and a filter on anything
product: crm
---
Choose the columns on your Donors list with Edit columns, drag them into the order you like, and Steward keeps that layout for you alone. Show 25, 50 or 100 people a page, and filter by almost anything you keep: zip, city, county, giving, pledges, events, volunteering, opt-outs, tags and the fields you brought in from your old system. Any filter can be saved as a view or turned into a group, and asking Atlas the same question gives you the same people.
