date: 2026-11-08
title: A change you make shows everywhere at once
product: crm
---
When you save a change, every screen you have open now shows it the moment Steward confirms it, without a reload. Figures Steward works out in the background, like the counts on your moves, say "Updating…" until they catch up instead of showing the old number. Deleting a gift that has a tax receipt now asks whether to void the receipt first, instead of quietly doing nothing.
