date: 2026-11-06
title: Split a gift across funds, find recurring giving, and count pledges toward a goal
product: crm
---
A gift can now be split across two or more funds when you add or edit it: the lines must add up to the gift, and it stays one gift with one receipt, counted in each fund by its line. Recurring giving has its own place under Fundraising's Money in and under Finance, and searching "recurring" or "monthly" takes you there. Each goal can count pledges toward it (campaigns do by default), showing raised plus pledged as committed, and the profile's "Request a gift" is now "Send a payment link", beside a new "Record a pledge".
