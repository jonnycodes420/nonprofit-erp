In Drafts to review, Read the draft now opens and stays open, each draft shows its first line so you can tell them apart, and Open in my email starts the message in your own email with the words in it. I sent it and Not sending this each take the draft away at once, with Undo. Audience now shows how many of your donors have an email, can only get mail, or can't be reached, what your mail-only donors have given with a way to send them a printed letter, and why an audience is empty when it is.
