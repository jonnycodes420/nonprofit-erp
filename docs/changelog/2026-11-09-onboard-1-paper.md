title: Sign, save a card, and check your file before it goes live
product: crm
---
Getting started now takes one link: open it, read the one-page order form with your price and first charge date, type your name and title, and save a card. Nothing is charged that day, and the signed form is in Settings → Billing to view or download. Your first donor file can go in as a preview, hidden from every count until you have compared it with your own file (totals to the cent, each year, each fund and ten donors checked by hand) and pressed "These match, make it live".
