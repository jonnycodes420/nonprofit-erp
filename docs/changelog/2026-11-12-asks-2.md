date: 2026-11-12
title: Steadier suggested asks, task dates that are never in the past, and look-alike rows decided together
product: crm
---
A suggested ask now steps up from what a donor has actually given and is never more than one and a half times it, so a $35,712 donor is asked $45,000, not $75,000. The Year-end givers button no longer names a date that has passed: after Nov 1 it makes tasks due from today, spread over the coming working days, largest givers first. Rows that may not be gifts are grouped when their memos read the same, so 150 program fees are one decision with one Undo, and you see how Lifetime giving would change before anything does.
