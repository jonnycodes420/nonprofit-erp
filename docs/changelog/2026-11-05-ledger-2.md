title: Money figures that add up: the fiscal year, fund splits, grant awards
product: crm
---
Reports always names the same fiscal year, and it is the one the rest of Steward uses. On a profile, Restricted plus Unrestricted now equals Lifetime giving, with gifts to General Operating counted as unrestricted, and every fund they gave to is listed. A grant award is a promise, so it books in Finance as each payment arrives, never twice. Someone whose business gave recently is no longer shown as lapsed.
