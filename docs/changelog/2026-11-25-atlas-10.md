title: Atlas briefs you however you ask, and a role can be removed
product: crm
---
Ask Atlas "Can you brief me on" someone, or "What's the next step for" them, and you get the brief even with AI reading off: open asks, gifts not yet thanked, the next meeting, the next move your profile shows, and when they became a donor. Tell me more now shows more.
Each role on a profile can be removed, with Undo, and removing Volunteer keeps their hours. An application from someone who already volunteers closes itself. Data health now flags two records with the same name and different emails when both have been giving, and Atlas asks which goal you mean when several are running.
