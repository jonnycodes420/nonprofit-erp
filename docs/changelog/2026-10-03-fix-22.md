date: 2026-10-03
title: Fewer interruptions, and records that open
product: crm
---
Busy offices on one wifi no longer share a request limit: each signed-in person has their own, and Ask why answers up to sixty questions an hour. An attachment counted on an email now opens the message in the mailbox it is in, birthdays show on a person's record and on Home, and deleting something happens at once with Undo instead of a browser pop-up. Room to give sorts your whole list, and a foundation's figures come from the IRS's own public file.
