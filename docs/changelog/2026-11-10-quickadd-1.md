title: Add a person and their gift in one go
product: crm
---
Adding a person now takes you somewhere: Save opens their record, Save and record a gift opens it with the gift form ready, and Save and add another keeps the form open for the next one. In Record a gift and on each deposit slip line you can add someone new right there, and saving adds the person and the gift together, checking first whether they are already on file. Gifts now have Campaign, Appeal and Fundraiser fields that search what you already have (new campaigns and appeals can be made on the spot), and when you enter several gifts in a row the date, method, fund, campaign, appeal and fundraiser carry over as "Same as last", which you can clear.
