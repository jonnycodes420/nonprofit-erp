title: Every row of your file is accounted for
product: crm
---
When you bring in a list of people, every row now becomes a person or is shown to you with its line number and the reason it was set aside, so nobody goes missing, even someone with only a name. Mailing ZIP, state and country columns now go into the address, and ZIP codes keep their leading zero. The Donors page has an Everyone, Donors and Contacts switch with a count on each, so the people you import before their gifts are always easy to find.
