title: You see every name before a group of emails goes
product: crm

When you send more than one email at once, from volunteer reminders, reviewed drafts, auction winners or reconnect links, Steward now shows you the list first: each person's name, address and subject, and anyone it won't send to and why. Nothing is ticked for you, and anything that changed after you looked is skipped and named. Volunteer reminders now only appear while the shift is tomorrow, and older drafts wait in their own fold.
