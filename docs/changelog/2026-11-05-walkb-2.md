date: 2026-11-05
title: See your pending invites, archive events, and clearer pledge years
product: crm
---
Settings, Team now lists everyone you have invited but who has not joined yet, with Resend, Copy link and Revoke, and a second invite to the same address is turned away with a reason. An event with no gifts or guests can be deleted from its page, and one with gifts can be archived while its gifts stay counted. A pledge now says which fiscal year its amount falls due in, counts the installments still unpaid, and an event guest who has given is never labelled as not a donor.
