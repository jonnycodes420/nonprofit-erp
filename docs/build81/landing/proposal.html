WHEN A CARD STOPS

A monthly donor's card expires. Most orgs find out when the deposit is short.

Steward catches it within the hour and emails the donor a link to keep going, in your name. The donor never logs in to anything.

You know what four fifty-dollar sustainers a month are worth to you by December.

DRIFT

And the ones who already went quiet.

The Thread keeps the conversations you're having. Drift finds the people you've stopped hearing from: each one named, with the reason in their own pattern. "$2,000 every July since 2019. Nothing for 14 months." That is the window where a phone call still works, and it closes quietly.
